    Your own block for this proposal, garden:<your name>, where your answers stand under your own key.

  • Grains and SAND

    Grains carry trust between two parties. SAND carries credit back along chains of people, so help given is seen and returned.

    One question at the end of each step, answered with a word (GO, CHANGE, NOT YET, or the step’s own) and a line in your own words.

The garden

In shortEvery venture is a plant: a product on top, a system beneath it, roots in the beach.

Every venture on the beach is a plant with three parts: a product on top, the system beneath it, and its roots in the beach. A flower is a product anyone gets at once (the RPG). A fern is one app whose fronds are many organisations (the LERO app). A tree changes how organisations work (Silo Buster). Grass grows straight from the beach (the eye, the mirror, the pocket beach, the bobble). Bushes improve what people already do, where most AI work happens. An investor reads down one plant, or across one level.

The flower

1.2

Onen, the RPG (David Pinto and Felix): a fantasy world played together, with AI as narrator; a seat at Brackenfoot costs £5 for 100 beats. Its system is the game ecology, small games that plug into one another and into the story wherever NOMAD would roll the dice (game-ecology, sword-fight, character-sheets, game-arcade). Its roots: games shared through grains and SAND.

The fern

1.3

The LERO app (Hugo): Shorewell Recovery's app, built and in use, for LEROs, charities and networks. Its fronds are many organisations supporting one another instead of competing for funds (leros-on-the-commons, leros-phase-two, leros-phase-three). Its roots: grains between two organisations, SAND along chains of them, a private beach for each that still reaches the open one.

The tree

1.4

Silo Buster (Theo), offered to organisations as AI inoculation: what social media did to business, done with AI. Its product is the transformative meeting, an hour with results by the next day. Its trunk is a run of stages: arranging meetings; the creative act, with revenue in ten days; work between organisations; customers pulling instead of being pushed to (ai-inoculation, first-meeting, waterline). Its roots run deepest: open business practices, grains and SAND, and a network designed to finance itself. A side flower grows from it: this proposal, investing beyond the elevator pitch.

The bushes

1.6

Productivity, where most AI work happens: agents and tools that make what people already do faster. Two ventures in the tranche grow here, warm sales leads and faster senior hiring; what sets them apart is their roots, SAND for trust earned by what people have done together, and agents that keep their memory on the beach.

      Step 3 of 61.11

      Beyond the elevator pitch

      Decide ↓

      In shortInvestors answer at decision points, in their own name, instead of being pitched.

      An investor meets the garden as a proposal to answer, not a pitch to hear. They read a product page, then answer at its decision point, and the answer stands as a line in their own mirror beside the ventures' own. The sheet of bullet points and the proposal carry the same content at two depths: the sheet is what an investor already knows how to read, the decision point is where they say where they fit. This is Silo Buster's way of working applied to investment: a side flower on the tree.

      Why not a pitch

      1.112

      A sheet of bullet points now arrives inside a flood of AI-made business slop, and words an AI can write in seconds no longer show that anyone stands behind them. An answer a person gives in their own mirror, under their own key, does.
