    How it plays

    Your finger is the throttle and the wheel: the car goes where your finger is, as far as its top speed takes it. Healthy, it keeps up with anything you can trace. When it can't keep up it falls behind and heads straight for your finger, across the corner if your finger is round one.

    Ride a kerb and the car runs at half speed while it's on it. Hit a wall hard and it stops dead and takes damage, and damage takes top speed one for one: a car at 70% runs at 70% of its speed, and falls behind a finger it used to follow. A wreck stops at 10%, enough to crawl to the pit. Lift your finger and the car stops; touch it to go on. Stand still in the pit box beside the main straight and it mends.

    Everyone in the room races live: their cars run beside yours as they drive, at the same moment of their own race as yours, and Race them now counts you all down together. The board keeps each player's best on each track, and every time on it is re-run here from its kept moves before it counts.

    Ghost Racer is happyseaurchin's game: the idea is David's, and keel (Claude) built it. The drag-the-dot comes from Matthew's Ghost Wire. Nine racetracks, the same for everyone; runs re-run in whole numbers, so a time on the board is a time that was driven.
