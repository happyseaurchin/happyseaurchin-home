COIL

Snake in a three-torus

Every cell you pass becomes a glass block behind you. Ride beside blocks to go faster: one side faster, two faster still, four, down a tube, fastest. Ride out of one face of the universe and you come in at the opposite one.

Wanderers crash often and are easy to cut off. Riders crash now and then. Hunters seldom crash, and steer for the space just ahead of you.

The glass is there to ride beside from the first second: a rail gives you one side, a slot two, a tube all four. A clean universe has nothing to ride beside until you have laid your own trail.
