Your shell — what you carry.

The inside of a handle: the passport as its face, and every organ beside it — history, stash, purpose, what it knows and has witnessed, the mirrors it keeps — each openable, each yours to edit under your key. Whose shell?

No passport yet? Type the name you will go by; the next screen founds one — the face is the first organ.
