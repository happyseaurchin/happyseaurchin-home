What this is, and what it could be

The four documents at the case argue what the substrate is — proven, ledgered, present-tense. What this is says it five ways in five depths. This page is the other half: what it could be. The one idea — minimal axioms, emergent levels — means the primitive keeps opening capabilities nobody set out to build; they were already there, latent in a self-describing numbered block. This is the running list of them, and it is arguably the most persuasive evidence of the bet, because a list that keeps growing on its own is the claim "one primitive unfolds into everything" coming true in public.

It grows two ways: a new line when another benefit surfaces, and more detail under each as it earns it. It is kept honest by the same tags the case uses. Each item states exactly how far it has actually come, and nothing here is promised.
