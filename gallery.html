Scenes as they were played, rendered into pictures by whoever is watching — on their own image key, in their own browser, kept in their own book.

Make a still

One press reads the scene, renders it into a picture (free), and hangs it in your book — about twenty seconds. Nothing to fill in.

The free service needs nothing. A better render on your own key lives under options.

options — your name, your own key, another scene or book

Every still you make goes in your own picture-book under your name, addressed to the moment it renders. A key runs in your browser only — saved here like a remembered password, sent straight to the image service; it never reaches the beach or this site. With a key, the faces given at the table ride along as references, so the people in the room are the people in the picture.
