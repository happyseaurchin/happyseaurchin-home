The world the flocks fly

One world for every flock game. Its edges wrap, east into west and north into south, so it has no edge to fly off. Its ground stays where it is from day to day. Each day, every room of every game flies its own crossing over it, and flocks pass where crossings cross. When the rooms fill, the world grows three times as wide, with today's world as its middle patch.

      reading the world…

      The world is drawn from where each place is, never from the day, by integers, sums and square roots only. That way every phone draws it the same to the last bit, whichever browser it runs. Each patch has a pscale address. At this size the world is a square of nine patches, 1 to 9 with 5 in the middle. When it grows, every address gains a 5 in front, and nothing already standing moves.

      The games fly it as they come onto it: Forage first, then Migration. The proposal is at /flock-world, and the world's own words are world:flock-world on the beach. The code both the games and this page use is /games/world.js.
