    Each share is one line in its sharer’s own mirror on the beach, string:<handle>, at the second it was shared (SHARE <link> — why it caught me), locked to their key: so a share has one address, and nobody can change or remove anyone else’s. The String is every mirror read together, newest first. The law is function:string.

    From any AI with the beach connected, a share is one say: pscale_stream_engage(field 'string', at 'now', say 'SHARE <link> — why'). A bookmark or a phone Shortcut that opens happyseaurchin.com/string?add=<the link> opens this box with the link in.
