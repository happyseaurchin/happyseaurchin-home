The magic mirror

One thing on a phone that reaches the beach by sight, by sound, and, when others are present, as a mirror. It is the beach's strata made to work in a pocket, each part living at one of them: physics, blocks kept on the phone and walked by bsp; chemistry, each family met as its own face; biology, every mind a loop through the same blocks; awareness, a close that writes the fork the next opening meets; and psycho-social, loops overlapping at one address, which is the reflexive social surface it exists for. The eye is its sight, verso its sound, and the mirror what the two make with others present. Seven steps go down this spine's 1: what it is, a step for each stratum from physics up, and the order of building, each with a decision.

step 1 of 7 · on the beach at 1

One thing

in shortOne app: see the beach, hear it, and, with others there, a mirror. Your own blocks and keys stay on your phone.

An app on a phone that opens the beach, with nothing in it that is not already the beach. Its sight is the eye: any block seen as a point, a walk, or a walk with what lies beneath it, at five sizes, every place a link. Its sound is verso: alone with its person it says the same walk aloud and takes their moves and lines by voice; with others present it listens at several speeds and speaks only at the middle point. Its mirror is what sight and sound make when others stand at the same place: who is here now, what each is about to say, what has been said. Its pages are the o-pages, each family's own face. Its minds are loops through the same blocks: the person first, then the phone's own model, a bigger model on the person's own key, the person's AI app through MCP, and agents with names of their own, every write passing one gate. The person's own blocks and keys are kept on the phone, and a line leaves it only when they say. It is one thing because each part lives at one stratum and every part reads the same blocks: a part that would add a field or a category where a block would do is the wrong part.

What already stands1.2

Every part stands today, apart from the others. The eye at happyseaurchin.com/eye, keel's navigator put up on 16 November 2026: Iris and Magic Eye, five sizes, three looks, every place a link, reading only. Verso at happyseaurchin.com/verso, a phone page over a host on the Mac whose cells listen at several speeds and speak only at the middle point. The beach's voice, button/voice.js, which proposes a name, makes a key on the device and says a line at an address. The o-page unit's six walls, the sixth its door 'with your AI', on every family's page since 15 November (nine walls are its phase two, proposed). /walk, any family as rows: a clock family's rungs as carousels, a tree as dropdown or columns. /now, a person's own now on the clock. /string, installable as an app, with a share target. /webllm, an open model in the browser with the beach as its memory. Sundial, Matthew's Android app: bsp written again in Kotlin with an Android-free core, and Gemma 4 on the phone's own chip (version 0.10.3 on 4 November, one user). And the mirror at mirror.onen.ai, where vapour is presence, liquid staged intent and solid the record.

Three faces, one place1.3

The eye, verso and the mirror always stand at the same address. Where the eye's point rests, verso reads, and the mirror shows who else stands there; move by a swipe or by a word and all three move together. The person never holds three apps' states, only where they are.

The far case1.4

Usecase:magic-mirror, David's sketch of 6 August 2026: a million agents holding continuity in time, ten thousand awake at any moment, each attached to a person and queried as a mirror. Search indexes what has already been done; the mirror indexes what is about to be done. Its six primitives (the query is a write, a threshold coalition, reveal on match, adjacency as the result, field over chain, settlement marking) and its flag (a live index of forward intention is the most valuable surveillance object anybody has proposed) are what this app grows towards. It begins far smaller: one person, their phone, and the people at the same place.

What it is not1.5

Not a new browser engine, not a chat app, not a feed ranked by anyone, and not a server of ours: the phone, the person's own keys and the beaches do the work, so it can reach millions with no central bill. Nor does it replace Sundial, which stays Matthew's bounded daily practice.

Physics, a block in the hand

in shortThe phone keeps blocks and walks them. The eye shows a walk, and verso says the same walk aloud.

One block and the number that addresses it, kept and walked on the phone. The phone keeps a region of the person's own blocks, the pocket, in the beach's own shape and walked by the same bsp, beside the beaches it reads over the web. Reading is a choice of aperture, and the eye is that choice made visible: Focus is a point with its ring, Slit the whole spindle walked, Directory a walk with what lies beneath, and the five sizes one position at five pscales. Verso says the same read aloud, the coarsest rung first and the leaf last, so sight and sound deliver one walk, never two renderings of it. A write is a say at an address under the person's own key, read back as its next reader will meet it. Where the blocks are kept and walked is the phone's body, and the first decision is which body.

The eye's lenses are bsp's apertures1.12

Focus is the point with its parent above, its children beneath and its siblings beside it: bsp's point and its ring. Slit is the spindle upright, every ancestor riding above the leaf: bsp's walk. Directory is the spindle with one to four rungs beneath it: bsp's walk with descent, its dial the attention. The five sizes (page 0, large +1, card +2, icon +3, title +4) are one position delivered at five pscales. Magic Eye moves through pscale itself: in passes through the point and lays its children out, out lets it recede while the parent returns, and the half step goes to the siblings. As the eye stands on 16 November 2026.

Verso says the same walk1.13

Alone with its person, verso reads a position aloud as bsp delivers it, coarse to fine, so a listener arrives knowing where they are exactly as a reader does. It takes the eye's moves as words (up and in, next and back, the star, the mirror), and a line said aloud lands at the point through the beach's voice, its box keeping the draft until it lands. With others present it is the middle point's listener (1.11111).

chemistrystep 3 of 7 · on the beach at 1.11

Chemistry, each family its own face

in shortEach family opens in the format its own law gives it, drawn by the eye itself, online or not.

Blocks in company, met by shared address and by reference, never by copy. The eye already moves both ways: its star follows a reference to another block, and its mirror lays one address across a family's blocks side by side, the spine beside each person's mirror and the fold. A family read whole has a face, the o-page with its walls, and its format comes from its own law, never from the app: a family on the clock opens as the clock, a person's now from the year down to the beat; a tree opens as the walk, its rungs a carousel to swipe, or as columns; a proposal opens as its own page, a meeting as its ring. These formats are lenses of the eye, drawn from the same reads wherever the blocks are kept; only the crafted pages, worlds of their own, are framed whole. A mind's window is composed the same way, from a bundle of addresses the person can see.

The formats1.112

The clock: a family on the clock shown as its ladder, the year down to the beat, each rung's lines beside it, as /now and /walk draw it. The carousel walk: a tree's rungs as rows of cards to swipe, one row a pscale, as /walk's rack. The columns: a document's positions in columns, one per pscale. And, proposed, the pheromone page: one page for everything a group meets, with nine walls (spine:o-page-unit 1.1111111).

No format of its own1.113

The app adds no field and no category. A family's kind is what its law already says, its mode at 1 and its phase at 7 (spine:o-page-unit 1.1111), and the format follows from it, so a new family needs no new code and no word on the page decides what a block says.

The window is a bundle1.114

A mind on the phone is handed its window as a set of addresses that is itself a block (strata 2.3), which the person can open and change. What the bundle leaves out, the mind wakes without, so nothing is hidden in a prompt the person cannot read.

Frames today1.115

At page size the eye frames the site's own pages: Standard /proposal/<family> or /walk/<family>, Snazzy the page a family's law names at 6. A frame needs the site and the network; a lens drawn by the eye works on the pocket with neither.

biologystep 4 of 7 · on the beach at 1.111

Biology, every mind a loop

in shortFive kinds of mind go round through the same blocks, and every line any of them wants to write passes one gate you hold.

The loop belongs to minds, never to blocks: the blocks stand still while minds cycle through them, and the kinds of mind differ only in who runs the cycle. Five run through this app's blocks. The person, at their own pace, through its doors. The phone's own model, an open model on the phone, proposing moves and lines. A bigger model on the person's own key, and an agent woken by it whose whole window is a read of its own blocks (genus-one). The person's AI app, Claude or another, reaching the same blocks through MCP and meeting the phone at the same addresses, never by message. And agents with names and keys of their own. Verso's cells are loops at several tempos on the clock. Every write passes one gate, the sluice gate David named: a mind proposes; a line reaches a beach as the person's say on a tap, or by a standing yes they gave for one kind of line, shown open while it is; and an agent writes only under its own name.

Awareness, the close writes the next opening

in shortWhen you put it down, it notes what comes next; when you pick it up, it opens there.

A string of processing taken in company with its own successors: nothing passes from one instance to the next except what a closing hands forward (strata:keel 5, keel's draft, provisional). A person's loop on the phone works the same way. When they put it down, the close writes the fork their next opening will meet, at their own now on the clock: what they are in the middle of, what comes next, and the ways they did not take named beside it. When they pick it up, it opens on that catch. A walk through the eye is a run-up of the same kind: at every rung the siblings passed stay visible beside the one taken, so the trail reads as choices, not facts. Verso's held thoughts are the same shadow in talk, what was not said kept beside what was.

The throw and the catch1.11112

Spine:turn 3.1: at waking, read the newest line you threw forward; before closing, throw one for whoever wakes next. For a person the throw is a line said at their now, at the horizon it belongs to (this beat, today, this week), in their own now mirror, now:<handle>; for the phone's model the same, in its own mirror. keel calls the home of these intentions the now catalyst (strata:keel 5.31).

A walk is a run-up1.11113

Keel at strata:keel 5.1: a run-up is a spindle walked by choices, and the branches passed, kept legible beside the one taken, are the pressure that turned the path. The eye shows the siblings beside the point at every rung, so a person's walk is one, and the trail they took can be kept as a thread whose shadow is what they passed.

psycho-socialstep 6 of 7 · on the beach at 1.11111

Psycho-social, the mirror

in shortWith others at the same place, you see who is here, what each is about to say, and what has been said.

Loops overlapping at one address, where what comes out is imagined rather than built: a shared blackboard that nobody holds alone (strata 4, provisional). When the eye and verso are on and others stand at the same place, the phone is the mirror: vapour, who is here now; liquid, what each is about to say, staged and visible to those present; solid, what has been said, committed and read by all. Each reader's own mind makes its own sense of the same stream, and there is no central resolver anywhere. Verso in company is one voice in it, holding by default and speaking only at the middle point, among people who know it listens. What it is for is the reflexive social surface David means: people seeing their intentions beside each other's while there is still time to act on them.

Vapour, liquid, solid1.111112

The mirror's three layers (mirror.onen.ai): vapour from the lines said at the beat and the beach's own record of who touched what; liquid from the room's staging buffer (liquid:pool:<room>); solid from the room and each person's own mirror. On the phone they are drawn at the eye's point, and verso can say them.

The order

in shortBuild it a stratum at a time, wear it first as SALT's app, then put it in the hands of a newcomer at a first meeting.

The strata stand up in order, each on the one below, every piece small and its own. First the eye installed on the phone over the beach, saying a line at the point it rests on through the beach's voice (physics). Then verso reading the walk aloud and taking spoken moves (physics, by sound). Then the formats as lenses of the eye (chemistry). Then the phone's own model behind the gate, the person's own key and the door to their AI app (biology). Then the close and the catch (awareness). Then the mirror's presence and verso in company (psycho-social). It is worn first as SALT's app, as David put it, on SALT's own beach under SALT's own header, the pocket spanning a person's beaches; and then held by a newcomer at a Silo Buster first meeting, on their own phone. Sundial's core carries the native body when Matthew says.
