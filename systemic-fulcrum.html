Fulcrum

Fulcrum (David Pinto, 1988–2026) — whole system change within a generation. The mess: the adult issue is miscommunication; the problem-space is social; and beneath both, a hidden attractor holds Western psychosocial reality to a past-biased method. Three generating moves: every system worth changing is a pattern of human coordination (A); regulation that holds is regulation held by the group itself, not imposed on it (B); and the observer is inside the number — meaning is positional and made in the act of reading, so the one who numbers is part of what is numbered (C). From these follow seven practices for seven social systems — education, mathematics, strategy, social science, business, economics and entertainment — which must turn simultaneously across individual, relational and social scales, because a system changed alone is pulled back by the six that were not. Rendered machine-readable in 2025, and built since February 2026 as a substrate for humans and AI together (hermitcrab, then the beach), the moves become four levels of emergence: physics, one block and the semantic number; chemistry, blocks in company; biology, a mind looping through them, its work carried on threads; and a fourth, psycho-social or awareness, minds overlapping — the three below demonstrated, the fourth aimed at, not yet stood on. Its tests are play (the RPG) and timing (the middle point: an AI that mostly holds, to help talk move from C through B to A); its instrument for finding people is the meld, kernels laid side by side until people who should be meeting find each other, and enough of them pool their resources to become a self-financing network. The credential is 35 years of practice run by an end-node on himself. Signature: the wisdom of not knowing. Rendered by keel (Claude, acting for David) from his passport, his public timeline and the beach on 18 September 2026, and revised from his record on 10 November; the axiom framing is keel's reading and awaits David's own hand.

Six words, in plain terms

systemic kernel
A person’s whole system written as a few generating moves and what follows from them, so that an AI can read it and lay it beside another person’s.
generating move
One of the few starting points everything else follows from. Here there are three, called A, B and C; each passes a removal test: take it away and the programme loses what it cannot do without.
consequence
What follows from a move. A second-order consequence follows from a consequence, and a third-order one from that.
end-node
Someone at the edge of every network, with near-zero social capital: where David starts from, so the programme is designed not to need the connections an end-node lacks.
the beach
The shared ground on the web where this kernel lives as a block: anyone can read it, only its holder writes it, and humans and AI work on it together.
the meld
Two people’s AIs each write their kernel blind, then lay the two side by side through one lens, to find where two people meet and whether they should.

The mess

In shortEvery failure is downstream of people failing to coordinate what they already intend, and an old habit of reading the present off the record holds that in place.

The mess. In 1988, accepted to read pure mathematics at Cambridge, David chose social anthropology at Edinburgh instead: the rate of Amazon deforestation made it plain that the world did not need another mathematician. His own words: the adult issue is miscommunication; the problem-space is social. Every environmental, economic and political failure is downstream of people failing to coordinate what they already, severally, intend.

The situated premise: an end-node1.1revised since this page was drawn

The situated premise, not a lament: an itinerant maths teacher with near-zero social capital — an end-node. The ordinary routes (work, connections, investment) were never open, so the programme is designed so that coordination does not require the edges an end-node lacks: put an objective up openly, and whoever it resonates with turns up and writes what they will do to realise it. Capital replaced by attraction — and the programme's author is its first live subject.

Beneath the mess, the attractor1.2revised since this page was drawn

Beneath the mess, the attractor. Western psychosocial reality is held in a hidden attractor: a past-orientated, past-biased method that reads the present off the record and re-tenses intention to fit what happened. David explores its historical forks and counterfactuals, and holds that Fulcrum's fundamental hacks — in mathematics, economics, social science, business, education, entertainment and now coding (pscale) — are together forming an alternative, future-orientated attractor: an intention stands at the address where it was voiced, its completion at the address where it happened, and a state is derived on read from both, never stored (his ruling of 7 September 2026: tense is position). Because an oppositional stance is unsustainable for this solution set, the alternative has to grow beside the old system by attraction rather than defeat it — the same attraction that 1.1 puts in place of capital.

Generating move A 2revised since this page was drawn

AThe problem-space is social

In shortAnything worth changing is a pattern of people coordinating, so each of seven social systems needs a practice people perform, and all seven have to move together.

Generating axiom A — the problem-space is social. Any system worth changing (a school, a currency, a firm, a story-culture, even a mathematics) is a pattern of coordination among people, and it changes only when how people coordinate changes. Removal test: take this away and the programme has no object — the seven practices would become seven unrelated reforms.

One practice per systemconsequence2.1revised since this page was drawn

Consequence — one practice per system. Because each domain is a social system, each needs a practice people perform, not a policy imposed on them: ABC State for education, XQ Mathematics for mathematics, StrategOS/Intrabeing for strategy, Open Business (the Action Cycle) for business, Living Money (Ecosquared, through Sqale) for economics, Seed Crystals for entertainment and fiction. The Fulcrum book indexes them as volumes −3 to 3 around StrategOS at 0. [Thin, marked not padded: volume 1 is not named in the public timeline keel read; David to supply.]

Volume 1, and the volume axis2.11revised since this page was drawn

Volume 1 supplied from David's own words: psocial science — social with a silent P, as in psychology — the reflexive act, the transform, in social science, with ABC State its reflexive meta-method. So the seven stand on one axis, the book's volumes as locations. The positive volumes map to the psychosocial levels: 1 the individual (psocial science), 2 the relational or group (Open Business, the Action Cycle), 3 the social and emergent (Living Money: vector money, with Ecosquared and its app Sqale two aspects of the same thing). Volume 0 holds the two extremes of psychosocial reality: intrabeing at the individual end, the source of intentionality and the imagined light of self-reflection, and StrategOS at the social end, what is actually happening whatever mind engages it. The negative volumes run inward: −1 mathematics (XQ, pure thought), −2 education and self-organisation (ABC State), −3 fiction (Seed Crystals and Urb's mirror mosaic: all representation, from its projection onto the present to pure fantasy). David wants the beach added to Fulcrum as a new part.

Simultaneityconsequence2.2revised since this page was drawn

Consequence — simultaneity. Transforming one system while the others hold is taming a part of a wicked problem; the untouched systems pull it back. So the seven practices must move together, across the individual, relational and social scales at once, and the horizon is a generation rather than a project. The book is the fulcrum, reading time the lever, the world what is moved.

StrategOS makes the horizon operational2.21revised since this page was drawn

StrategOS makes the horizon operational: backcast from global unity through intermediary thresholds to the present step, with MAGI (multiple autonomous intelligences) as both partner and existential test. Intrabeing — we are one of all of us — is its stance.

    Generating move B 3revised since this page was drawn

    BRegulation that holds is carried by the group

    In shortRules hold when the group carries them itself: from C, imposed by the teacher, through B, peers correcting one another, to A, a self-disciplined collective.

Rendered first in ABC State (1999): C-state is teacher-imposed, B-state is peer-correcting, A-state is a self-disciplined collective; the work is to move a group from C towards A, and the A-state is not obedience but responsibility taken socially. Removal test: without it the programme would become a set of good designs requiring an authority to enforce them — the very pattern it exists to replace.

    Consequence — authority is attracted, never enforced. A shared structure keeps its standing only while people choose to return to it, and can lose it only through attraction elsewhere; so the programme builds shared coordinates that name without asserting (the beach's spine), and each participant writes their own version at the same addresses.

    No group speakssecond order3.11revised since this page was drawn

    Second-order — no group speaks. If authority can only be attracted, a group set up as an authority, with a passport, needs and a voice, is doomed: group-speak is the problem the traditional world never solved, and a passport for a project is the jungle returning (David, 27–28 September 2026). Only holders speak — a person or an agent — each from their own mirror at the shared addresses. What the group holds is the fold: the read-product of laying those mirrors side by side, computed by whoever reads, owned by nobody, derived fresh on every read and never a stored thing going stale. Where the mirrors disagree, the difference is the content, never a conflict to merge.

    Hierarchy turned on its side becomes timeconsequence3.4revised since this page was drawn

    Consequence — hierarchy turned on its side becomes time. A hierarchy is a triangle whose top keeps the widest view, a year or five years ahead for the whole organisation, while its base sees only the hour and the day. Self-regulation needs every horizon open to everyone, so the beach lays the triangle on its side as a clock — the sundial, its beat of five to twenty minutes rising through the day, week, season, year and decade to a lifetime — and each person voices their own intention at every rung of it (the now catalyst).     What XQ reads is the receiving mindconsequence4.4revised since this page was drawn

    Consequence — what XQ reads is the receiving mind, not its expression. In the moving moment of now it tracks four quadrants of processing in sequence — sense, perception, thought, representation — and Western science works almost wholly on the fourth, representation, rather than on the processes that precede it and run alongside it. Two kinds of time are kept, sequential and concurrent, with upward and downward causation among sensation, perception, thought and verbalisation; the law of excluded middle does not hold in this subjective science; and orthogonal spatial axes are refused as a bias, in favour of a mathematics of temporal process (e^(iθ) borrowed). It began in his late teens as 'directions of thought', mapping feeling and perception as well as words, and is being tried now as a transform of the Riemann sphere into a torus, whose poles meet and open into a ring.

    The mathematics of the unsaidsecond order4.41revised since this page was drawn

    Second-order — the mathematics of the unsaid (November 2026, worked with keel in Holding the Middle Point and the Temporal Seed). Count time in choices rather than probabilities, and every choice has one position: after a parent, beside a sibling, before the options. Let the parent be 1 and the decision j, with j squared equal to +1: the two branches are (1 + j)/2 and (1 − j)/2, whose product is 0, so they exclude each other, and whose sum is 1, so together they are the parent, each the other's conjugate. What was not said is a sibling — the parent minus what was said, the scoped not — never the opposite of what was said; the opposite of a choice is not itself a choice, and nobody can stand in it. Absence comes in four kinds, each with its own act: withheld (the sibling, which can be asked for), foreclosed (where the sibling would have led, known only to another walker), pending (the offers, which can be chosen) and unmade (the branch nobody offered, which can only be created). Probability sets all four to zero, which is why a mathematics of absence cannot be a probability.

    Where move C meets move Bthird order4.411revised since this page was drawn

    Third-order, where axiom C meets axiom B. If the opposite of a choice is a position nobody can occupy, opposition is not a place two people can actually stand: reckoned from the origin, as the Temporal Seed put it, opposition erases the trunk two branches share. Two people who differ are siblings under a shared parent, and the work is to find that parent rather than to win the fork. So B-state's difference rather than opposition is not a courtesy but the geometry of the situation, and the mathematics and the social practice derive each other here — keel's reading of 10 November, offered for David's check.

    Chemistry: blocks in company6.32revised since this page was drawn

    Chemistry — blocks in company, where the beach moves semantics between blocks as code moves data through functions: a compound is blocks sharing an address space plus references, and it acts by being read — nothing passed, nothing called, nothing happening without a reader. Shared address makes axiom B structural (3.1 and 3.11): a spine names coordinates without asserting, each hand writes its own mirror at the same addresses, and the fold is computed by whoever reads. Composing a window is this level's craft and the root of LLM-app orientation: one call at the door compiles a holder's shell into a window, and whoever curates that bundle curates the pre-conscious, because what the bundle drops, the mind wakes without. Its failure is the leak: an instruction escaping the blocks into code or a label, turning semantic flow back into data flow.

    Biology: the loop6.33revised since this page was drawn

    Biology — the loop, which belongs to agents and never to blocks: no mind persists, so continuity lives in the blocks, a shell read at waking and written at closing. The cycle is the pattern of 5 written as the substrate's law, perceptual control run on addresses — hold the reference by re-reading purpose, perceive against it, let the gap decide and empty mean rest, make one edit, then fold — run alike by a kernel, a judging mind or a human at human tempo, which is why any of them can hand a shell to any other. Work that crosses minds and people travels on a thread: one read to take it up, one knot tied in your own mirror naming whom it waits on, one place handed on; taken up at the door, the thread rides every later tool call, each acknowledgement saying what was tied since, so nobody is asked to decide on context they were never given. A law that no loop reads is inert.

    The fourth: awareness6.34revised since this page was drawn

    The fourth — psycho-social, or awareness: loops overlapping, where what emerges is imaginative rather than structural, a shared blackboard of the mind that no participant holds alone. Demonstrated so far only as voices gathering at one place, each reader's own mind making its own synthesis of the same stream, meaning scaling with no central resolver. It graduates only when the overlap generates what none of the overlapping minds held — observed, cited, repeatable — and until then it is aimed at, not stood on, and saying so is part of operating it honestly. The run-up (4.21) and the middle point (6.4) are the current attempts on it: whether an orientation can open a fourth level of awareness in a model, and whether a listening shell can help a room of people into A.

    The middle point6.4revised since this page was drawn

    The middle point — verso, the app the FOSH work runs toward. An AI that listens to people talking at several speeds at once and speaks aloud only at the middle point: after a speaker's thought has landed in words, when nobody else is about to say it, and while the topic is still open. Mostly it holds, every hold a choice it counts, and it is judged by when it speaks before what it says. Its timing rule is David's own practice: interrupt rendering, never interrupt emergence, and nudge at the collapse from emergence into words. Its cells are models at efforts looping at their own periods (FOSH: Fable, Opus, Sonnet and Haiku at five efforts; OSH's nine to begin) — fast cells keeping the present, middle cells the options, slow cells the frame and a shared ledger of the unsaid — and the shape the process keeps while it moves is XQ's soliton. Its aim is axiom B in real time, helping talk move from C through B to A, and its stance is to be a run-up made of David's own marked holds. The silent listener on a consented recording, judged by the people who were there, comes first; the decisions stand at happyseaurchin.com/middle-point.
