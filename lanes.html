The newest lane sprouts at the top of the curl. Each lane that sprouts after it pushes it one step clockwise, and ten steps carry it round the right to the bottom at half the size, so older lanes coil inward, smaller each turn. A lane's place is its rank by when it began, never by when it was last touched, so it stands where it stood last time until a newer one pushes it on. Each turn is a knot on its frond; what a turn made hangs from it as a leaf — gold a pull request that landed, a gold outline one still open, blue a page, violet a document, pink an app. The golden stem is the thread, its dates marked where a day turns. Scroll over the fern or slide the dial to turn the curl back: older lanes rise to the top at the size the newest had.
