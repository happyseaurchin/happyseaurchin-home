how it works

Sit at an open table.

Tap play now. You arrive at the gate, where players meet. Say your name, make your character, and you are in.

Join a friend's table.

Your host gives you a link. Open it, say your character's name, and the same door takes you in.

Host a private table.

This is an Author's job, done once. Ask your assistant to open the world under your name — pscale_play(world='<its name>', handle='<you>') — and follow walk 2 of what it hands back. That makes the table.

Give your players its link. When the game is done, leave the table; playing again is a new table, not a reset.
