The Systemic Kernel

The minimum ground any whole system covers, so that two people's whole systems can be laid side by side and each can see where the other covers what they do not.

Nine regions: the checklist an AI keeps beside a person's whole system, when it helps them write it and when it reads two side by side. It is never a form, and nobody fills it in. Tap a region to see any redraw people have made of it.

Use it

You never fill the kernel in. Your AI keeps it beside your whole system: when it helps you write yours, to tell you what your own material leaves out, and when it reads yours beside someone else's at the mind-meld, to look across the same ground on both sides. To write your whole system, give your AI this one line:

Read function:systemic-kernel at https://beach.happyseaurchin.com and follow its 4.1 with me: find or write my whole system.

If you think a region is missing, two should be one, or one is cut in the wrong place, say so in your own words: that is a redraw, kept in your own block at the region it concerns (function:systemic-kernel 4.2). It is never needed for a meld.
