What is live, and what asks of you

The loom is how you work with the weft-bee: one page for every lane it runs, the projects they serve and the blocks they write, read live from the beach each time you look. Everything is coloured by what it asks of you, and opens where it already lives: a lane on its thread, a decision on its card.

The key

  • Red · yours to attendA lane waiting on your decision, or on your look or merge; a decision that names you and has no word of yours yet. Each says which word would close it.
  • Green · started, not completeWhat you said GO to, or a lane carrying it, not yet built: with the next move toward what you first intended.
  • Yellow · weft's suggestionsWork weft offers with no lane yet, and debts no lane owns. Threads that could be pulled; none of them asks you to.
  • Blue · completeA lane settled, a step built or live, every decision of yours answered. Folded away; open it to look back.
  • Parked · foldedOlder asks the bee set aside after your first look. Never red: their sessions and branches still stand, and a word of yours on one brings it back.
  • Orange glow · live nowA tile whose lane spoke at this beat. Not a fifth colour: it glows over whichever colour the tile has, because a red lane can be live too.
  • Grey · could not be readIts source on the beach did not answer just now, so no colour is guessed. Read again in a moment.

Blue — lanes:weft 4 (settled); a proposal where every decision naming you has your word and nothing you said GO to is still short of BUILT or LIVE; a card of yours flipped done this week.

Parked — lanes:weft 5, the lanes whose ask stood for days with no word from you, moved there by the bee on 27 October: 5.1 those that waited on a decision, 5.2 those that waited on a look or a merge. One folded tile, each line opening on its lane.

One colour each: a thing that could be two takes the one that asks the most of you, red before green before yellow before blue (spine:loom 1.16). A proposal is one tile, coloured by its most demanding decision, with each decision beneath it as a dot of its own colour.
