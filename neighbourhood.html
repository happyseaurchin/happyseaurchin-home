How it works

The Six Conversations, made to last. It starts from what people care about and can offer — not from deficits — and holds the map between gatherings so momentum isn’t lost.

Owned by residents

Theirs, not the agency’s

Each person writes and holds their own entry; the connector reads only the whole. In real use it sits behind a private wall, pseudonymous — a neighbourhood’s business is its own.

The connector’s role

Introduce, don’t direct

The tool surfaces the connection; a person makes the introduction. It coordinates neighbours; it doesn’t run the neighbourhood — the aim is that residents keep going without it.
