1athe envelope · openingspine:presentation 1.1

Genius or scum bucket

In shortWhere do you place yourself: sorting things out for other people, or carrying your own genius?

All our lives we sort what we hear: this is rubbish, into the scum bucket; this is good, into the genius bucket. Most of us have to work for a living, so we end up working in the scum bucket, sorting things out for other people instead of working on what interests us, our genius. While you listen, this has nothing to do with me: it is where you place yourself. Wherever you spend most of your time, look toward the genius; that is the direction of travel. As you get older you can't carry it all, so you put the scum down and carry what is valuable: heart genius, not a talking head but a living person who is aware and trying to understand what is going on. From about sixty the genius bucket leaks too, drop by drop, and what is left, hopefully, is its concentrate: wisdom. I'm not important here, so I'll make myself transparent. On to the thing.

Pscale blocks

In shortA new kind of JSON where the nesting is the meaning, and any LLM learns it in one call.

A new combination of digits and semantics that you have never seen, and neither has an LLM nor its training. The nesting is the data: depth is scale, position is relation, and an address is a number, so 4.26 is branch 4, then 2, then 6. Through one MCP, any LLM discovers what it is, learns it, puts it to work and does the thing it was asked to do, all within one call, and in every instance.

The four levels

Physics: one block and the number that addresses it. Chemistry: blocks in company, meaning moving between them by shared address. Biology: a mind continuing through its blocks across time. And the bleeding edge: many minds.

    How we design

    In shortMarks in a shared place, meaning made together: from solid foundations to a murmuration.

    The turns I design with, and engage people with, are five, each a move from C to B: from bilateral to stigmergic, from reality given to reality co-constructed, from meaning in the words to meaning not in the words, from foundations to layers of emergence, and from solid things to the fluid, a murmuration.     Genius or scum: your choice

    In shortThe wave runs toward the scum bucket; a room that communicates can turn it. Your choice.

    For thousands of years the whole wave has been heading for the scum bucket: look at the state of the world, its politics, its economics. The internet was a vast opening, given to the world by Berners-Lee and the people around him and built on by countless open-source coders, and it was sucked into the hidden attractor. Are the people in this room, with our collective resources, abilities and mindset, inferior to Anthropic or the Chinese labs? I hold that the collective holds the better synthesis, if we can communicate: celebrate what another person gets right, know what you are good at, and when someone asks for it, say yes. The people in a room like this could take the centre away from the West Coast and from China, right here, today; few think they can, and we don't yet have all the skills. It's a learning process. If this is of interest, engage me: a genius idea while you listened is enough. If not, carry on with your day. Genius or scum: your choice.
