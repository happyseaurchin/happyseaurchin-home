The arcade

Every game on the shelf, today’s best and its room

A game made with an AI plugs into the beach and keeps its names, its results and its room there, in the open, outside any one platform. This is the front door: each game on the shelf, who did best on it today, how many played, and what people are saying about it, with room for your own line; and the premieres, short shows you answer.
