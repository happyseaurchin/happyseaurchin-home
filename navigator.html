What the model does

in shortThe app does the walking, reading and writing. At each fork of up to nine, the small model chooses, and says what it finds in your words.

The forks. The blocks carry the procedure, and each place offers either one next act (a point) or a choice of at most nine (a ring). The walker, bsp, runs every point. The model decides every ring, in the person's words, and says what it finds in their register. This is the split the beach already runs at every scale. In genus-one the kernel composes the window and applies the fold, and the model is the pulse between them; in a session like weft's, bsp walks and the mind chooses what to read and write. Sundial reached the same rule from the phone (spine:pocket-beach 1.11117871): 'a point is executed by the walker; a ring is a choice of at most nine, given to the voice.'

What it can hold

in shortOne act at one place needs about 1,200 tokens; the phone's model is given 8,192. A whole family is too big to read, so it never reads one whole.

Counted on 16 November 2026 in Gemma 4 E4B's own tokens, over all 46 families on the list and their 2,508 points. One act at one point needs a median of 1,212 tokens and at most 3,581, none over 4,096, including a 404-token navigator and room for a reply. Each further step in one kept conversation adds about 184. A family whole is a median of 7,440 tokens and up to 55,135, so a family is never read whole. Mass may grow without limit and reach must not, and a point stays a point: one line of about 300 tokens or fewer, with the rest nested beneath it. The limit is time and attention more than the window.

Where it hands on

in shortAnything big goes to a bigger mind: the family's page, your own AI app, or a bigger model on your own key, with the place already found.

Whatever needs many places read at once, a long piece of writing or a judgement goes to a bigger mind, chosen by code by the act's size and kind. That mind is the family's page (a link, free), the person's own AI app (door.js's first message, about 100 tokens, written by code), or a bigger model on the person's own key, handed the same window at a wider dial. The navigator's part ends when the place and the way are in the bigger mind's hands.
