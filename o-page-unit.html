Five words, plainly

The rest of the page leans on these.

  • The o‑page unit

    The six things every page you are handed carries: the page drawn plainly, its crafted page, a place to answer, a room, its threads, and a door for your own AI.

  • The beach

    An open, shared place on the web where people and pages keep small blocks of words at addresses. Anyone can read a block; only the key that keeps it can change it.

  • Your own reading

    Your answers, kept under your own name at the same places as the proposal’s steps, so everyone’s can be read side by side and nobody writes over anyone else.

  • A thread

    A piece of work carried from hand to hand, each rung a place where one act stands, so you can follow what a proposal set going.

  • A connector

    What adds the beach to your AI, such as Claude: once it is added, your AI can read the beach, and write on it as you with your key.

What a person is handed

Decide ↓

In shortEvery page you are handed should carry the same six things, so learning one page teaches you them all. Five already exist, in different places; the sixth, a door for your own AI, is new.

One unit, the same on every page. Whatever someone is shown, a proposal, a schematic, a meeting, a walk or a film, carries six things: the page drawn plainly from its blocks; its crafted page, in a world of its own; a place to answer at the part they mean, a decision or a comment kept in their own reading; a room to talk in; the threads it set going; and a door for their own AI. A person who has learnt one page has learnt them all.

      In shortSomething you can do on the beach needs no new kind of page. It already has up to three faces: a way for your AI, a walk for you to follow by hand, and a schematic where its inside needs explaining.

      No new form. A thing a person can do on the beach has up to three faces. Its way, ways:<name>, is for the AI: text only, every step exact, and the door hands it over. Its walk is for the person doing it by hand: a proposal read as a document, the way keel's mind-meld is made, each step a place they reach and their answers marking how far they have come, its first choice how they come, with their own AI, by hand, or walked through by someone. Its schematic, where the inside needs explaining, is the schematic form's: how it works, drawn for what already runs.
