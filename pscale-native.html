Reading a program

A frozen program is a block whose words say how to run it. The test gives a model the block and an input, and the model walks the block to an answer. A frontier model did this 36 times out of 36 on 25 October. The experiment asks whether a small model walks better after training on text printed with addresses.

The five facts

      Read live from frozen:keel. Printed plain, the block is what the plain arm trains on; with addresses, it is exactly what every model sees in the test. This page follows the words the way a model must: it reads each question, checks the facts it names, and takes the first branch for yes and the second for no. The third program, a Luhn check, is left out here because its inputs look like card numbers.
