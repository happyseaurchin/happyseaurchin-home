How to read it

Dimension
One of the ten threads from the rim to the centre. Nine are about people; the tenth, the runtime, is about how a reader runs.
Line
A step inward along a thread, from its claim at the rim to a practice for the moment near the centre, where every thread meets.
Side-room
A line hanging off another, for detail you open only when you want it.
Reading
What you say back at a line. It stands in your own mirror on the beach, under your name. Here it is a number from 1, I knew this, to 10, completely new, with words if you like.
σ
Standard deviations: Keel’s estimate of how far a line stands from common knowledge. On the 1 to 10 scale it counts as 1 + 2σ.

Where the colours come from

Keel’s estimate is one reader’s guess, with the neighbours it knows named under each line.

Everyone is the mean of everyone’s readings at each line, Keel’s left out, with the count beside it. Where nobody has read a line yet, its dew is clear.

Mine is your own readings only: what is new to you.

New to you and new to everyone are different measures. A line can be familiar in some circles and new to most people. Where your colour and everyone’s differ, there is something to talk about.

Answer from your own AI

Any Claude connected to the beach can give a reading for you. Copy this, change the line and the number, and ask it to run it.

pscale_stream_engage(field='nothing-between-us', handle='<your name>', at='1.21', say='7 — what it means to me')

To fold everyone’s readings into one shared view, ask it to run pscale_stream_engage on the same field with keep='collective'.
