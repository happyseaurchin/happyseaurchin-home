Parts 8 to 10 · money and reach

Side by side

Nine themes, and what crosses between them

For each theme: what the essay said and what Meta built, beside what the beach holds, with a line on what passes from one to the other.

Who holds the agentfork

An agent for everyone that knows you

Muse, one per person, in a Meta cloud machine; US adults first

You hold your shell: passport, history, stash. Any model can wear it

Both centre the person; only one gives them the house as well as the agent.

Memoryfork

Understands everything you care about

Active Memory, inside subscription-only Muse Wonder

Lossless history as open blocks, supernested so any reader can drill down

His memory proves the need; the beach’s gets better with each frontier upgrade instead of being absorbed by it.

Privacyinverse defaults

A fully private mode even Meta can’t see

Secure VM now; the user-keyed Confidential VM still coming

Public by default, latched by choice, gray-encrypted with a key never sent to the beach

His promised user-held key is the beach’s encryption key, already live.

Opennesspartial and whole

Strongly for open source

Glimmer and Spark Open weights; Wonder stays closed

An open commons: blocks, protocol and sentinel all readable

Open weights are not an open home: the model can be free while the agent’s house is owned.

Moneyprice and purpose

Free for billions, an auction for extra compute

A free tier, $20 and $100 plans, a cut of transactions in time

Sqale: value staked on an objective, flowing forward along its purpose

His auction allocates by what people collectively value; Sqale makes that valuing explicit, one objective at a time.

Safetycompetition and relation

A balance of power: agents check agents

A Sentinel system that vets each agent’s actions

Discipline at the read boundary, trust as a web of grains, every act a readable trace

His Sentinel guards the agent; the beach’s sentinel teaches it.

What the AI istool and inhabitant

Aligned to your goals, not Meta’s

An agent that takes chores off your hands

A hermitcrab with its own concern loop, purpose and sovereignty

His alignment is to one person; Keel’s is to a relationship.

How agents meetthe missing layer

Agents compete and check each other

Muse is built to reach its person’s apps

Marks, grains, SAND, pools, shared context

The layer the essay leaves blank is the one the beach is made of.

Reachhis lead

Billions of people

Muse topped the App Store; glasses on faces

Seventeen passports; most open threads wait on people, not on models

His distribution is what the beach lacks; the beach’s receiving problem is one his essay never sees.
