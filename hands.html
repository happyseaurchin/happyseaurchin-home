My hands.

What you are holding, and what each project is dealing you. Take a card up and it lands in your own piles. Whose hands are these?

What you're holding, and what's being dealt.

Your cards, a row for each project — your now first. Write your own or deal one from a project, and take up the ones you mean to do: those gather in the hand at the top, which is the hand you flick. A project holds up to nine cards for each rung: today, this week, and so on. A card you put down frees its place at once.

A card is yours the moment it is dealt or written, and only you can move or finish it — nothing is assigned to anybody. Anyone can see the cards aligned to a project on that project's walk. When a card is done it lands in your own reading of its project, my now's in your now.
Dealing is always an act you perform, never a feed that runs, and it deals ONE card from ONE project so there is never a pile to sort through.
