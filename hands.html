My hands.

What you are holding, and what each project is dealing you. Take a card up and it lands in your own piles. Whose hands are these?

What you're holding, and what's being dealt.

Your hand at the top — everything in your piles, whatever it aims at. Below it, one row per project you have chosen: the cards of yours already aimed there, and beside them what that project's rungs are asking for right now. Taking a card up writes it into your own piles and nothing else moves.

A card is yours the moment you take it up — the project is never written to, and nothing is assigned to anybody. Where a done card lands is read off the words it carries: a card aimed at a project lands in your reading of that project, and one aimed at nothing lands in your now.
These cards are dealt by reading the rungs, here, with nothing else needed. A mind reading with you — in a chat that can reach this beach — reads the same rungs better and can deal from places a page does not go: other people's readings, what is offered to you, anywhere you happen to be looking. It deals more; it is never what makes dealing possible.
