Endosystemics: a skeleton inside, not a thicker shell

As AI takes on more of the work, what keeps an organisation’s place is its people’s creativity and the trust between them. These are the light practices that hold it: what we use, how we come in, and what keeps it going, across departments, organisations and customers.

Turning up is taking part.

Endosystemics stays light on purpose: one page to decide on, a thread for every piece of work, a map of how things run, one clock everyone shares, and meetings that decide. It starts with an hour.

Set your own hour on the shared clock. With the beach connected to your Claude (happyseaurchin.com/connect), say: “Put what I’m doing this hour on my now, with my day and my week above it, and show me whose hour points the same way.”

Example · Hollin & Grey

C, B and A are David Pinto’s ABC State. C is control: work held together by roles, silos and chains of messages. B is groups working without opposition, in high trust. A is people moving together, in flow.

Hollin & Grey, its people and every figure are invented to show the shape, the same firm as in Silo Buster Path; James and David are the two who go in. Arrow keys step through the scenes and the space bar plays them. Example and Words take away the invented company and the narration, down to the bare structure.
