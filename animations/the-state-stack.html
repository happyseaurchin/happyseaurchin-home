The State Stack

Each of us carries a stack: our state in the social, in the relational power dynamic, and in our psychology. Set on the three lines C, B and A, a straight stack is a held state and a bent stack is mixed. A full stack at A is rare. The room's centre of weight shows whether the group has phased.
