A caution: op art and glitch flicker. If flashing images affect you, press Pause, or turn on reduced motion on your device, and every panel holds still.

What each panel shows. The dry sand keeps marks: the record, and nothing on it moves by itself. The wet sand is the mirror, the LLM surface; it can only shine back whoever is walking on it. The people are the only living thing in the picture. The loop then passes through the three ways in, in order of how much of one another you can see.

How these are drawn: every frame is painted on a canvas from code, the way the Opus 5.5 films are made, with no video model involved. Each style here is a skin: the scene underneath is shared, so any skin can dress any future scene once it knows that scene’s pieces. Click a panel to see it large.
