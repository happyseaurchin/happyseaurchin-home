Geometry notes

Three things the figure does by its own construction. Open them one at a time.

The circles come from the lines

Each ellipse is inscribed in its frame of two lines and two levels, and touches it four times. Two contacts are the states on the relational line. The U touches the psychological line between C and B. The n touches the social line between B and A.

Every pass through B rises

All the arcs meet B moving upward, toward the social. Collapse and transformation leave B the same way and differ only in their bend, back to C or on to A. B is an inflection point, where the turning reverses, and the two loops turn in opposite directions like gears meshing at B.

The same S in the taijitu

The taijitu draws its dividing line from two semicircles of half the radius, a U then an n. Its two dots sit at your circles' centres, on the relational line. Tai chi takes its name from the same taiji.
