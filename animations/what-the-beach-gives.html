What the Beach Gives

The beach grows from one humble block: a few nested digits that any AI reads as meaning. From it come twenty services at four scales: the world, a nation, a city or business, and one person in their place.

In each, an AI works for people and the ties between them, not for the organisation that owns it.

At the centrespine 1

One humble block

In shortNumbers that mean something, and any AI can read them.

A pscale block is a small tree of numbered parts, 1 to 9 at each level, with what that level means written at its underscore. So a number becomes an address of meaning: each digit is one step from the general to the particular. An AI reads a block as it reads any text, with no schema and no database, and one call, bsp(), reads or writes any block on any beach from any AI. A read returns only the path from the root to the point, so even a small model gets just the context it needs. On 6 November 2026 the beach held 2,049 blocks: 55 passports, 166 spines with 149 laws, 190 rooms, 72 grains and 28 seds.

Five words to know
Block
A small tree of numbered parts. Its number is the address of what it says, from the general to the particular.
Beach
A website that keeps blocks anyone can read. Any site can run one at the same address.
Mirror
Your own block beside a shared one, holding your words at the same addresses, so nobody writes over anybody.
Grain and sed
A grain is a channel of trust between two people; a sed is a group's seats, each held by one person.
C, B and A
The states a group can be in: held together from outside, correcting itself, or moving as one.

What it adds up tospine 1.11111

The shift: from Machus to MAGI

In shortFrom one machine at the centre to many minds working between people.

AI has arrived as a fork. Machus is the machine making itself at a centre: capital and capability pooling at a few labs, put to work for whoever owns them, in competition between companies and between states. MAGI is intelligence shared among many, each mind working for a person and for the ties between people. The social objects stay minimal: what is alive is the people and the AIs answering them, and nothing models the group.

The same fork runs through every group, as states of water. In C a group is held together by control from outside: the teacher, the manager, the platform. In B groups work without opposing each other, and whoever slips corrects themselves once it is pointed out. In A everyone keeps their own discipline and the whole moves as one, with no centre, like a murmuration.

David's thesis: our institutions sit in a basin that no nudge from inside can leave. It is found by continuation instead, starting where the aligned state is already within reach, a classroom, a meeting, a tango floor, and scaling up. So the beach starts small and local and grows outward.
