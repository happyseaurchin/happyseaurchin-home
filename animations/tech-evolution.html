Reading it

  • Width is modelled weight, not market share or money. A record's level (how fundamental it is) sets its full width; an adoption curve grows it from a point at its origin, and decline thins it. A strand is as thick as the records it holds.
  • Colour is heat: bright where innovation is happening, grey once it has become ground everything else stands on.
  • Nesting is lineage: a technology grows inside the field it opened from, and can swell until it fills it.
  • Threads are fusions: other ingredients that came together at the origin.
  • Dashed rings are claims: significance asserted for a young technology, drawn apart from what it has shown so far.
  • Pulses are arrivals, taller for more fundamental ones. Clusters are the punctuations.
  • Gold coins are money where it touched a strand: solid where it changed hands, open where it was valued or lost, with gold arcs where it moved between strands. Coin size is on a log scale, so small sums stay visible beside huge ones; hover a coin for its amount.

Stems

    The seed

    On the log clock, each band of the background is one order of magnitude: the last ten years, the last hundred, the last thousand, out to three million. Accelerating change spreads evenly across it, so the punctuations show as clusters rather than as a crush at the right edge. Switch to Linear to see the crush.

    The Shell winds the same clock into an equiangular spiral, one whorl per order of magnitude. The present is always the aperture; as time plays, the recent past pours inward. The form never changes as records or years are added. Only what fills it does.

    Forks that bypass a saturated bloom

    • Iron forks from copper smelting, not from bronze. Banking forks from credit, not from coin.
    • Photography forks from image-making and sound recording from speech, both bypassing text. The voice line runs on to voice-native AI.
    • Tanks grow inside cavalry's own field until they replace it.
    • Mobile money forks from electronic payments and skips the bank branch: M-Pesa reaches millions with no bank account.
    • Fiat money grows inside the Bretton Woods system, which grew inside the gold standard; each regime is the soil of the next.
    • Open source forks from gift and reciprocity rather than from the firm, and turns copyright into a guarantee of sharing. It now runs most of the world's servers and every Android phone. Its history is grown into a seed of its own: Open Seed.
    • Projected, 2026: pscale forks from place-value notation, the beach from kin bands by way of Fulcrum, and vector money from reciprocity. Each fuses with the language-model bloom. They are drawn as thin, because that is their observed reach so far, with dashed rings for what is claimed.

    What paid for the spread

    Each technology here waited for a package of finance to pay for its spread: public debt for gunpowder armies, the joint-stock company for ocean trade, venture capital for the chip, the ad auction for the web. Tech-finance evolution pairs thirty-one of them with their packages and measures the waits.

    What the widths are

    Each record carries a handful of numbers: origin, peak, level, fate, lineage. Reach and heat are modelled from those, so the picture is a structured hypothesis rather than measurement. To grow it, append records; the form holds. Measured curves can replace modelled ones stem by stem from three open sources: the Historical Tech Tree (lineage links), CHAT (adoption in 150+ countries since 1800) and Seshat (technologies by polity across ten thousand years).
