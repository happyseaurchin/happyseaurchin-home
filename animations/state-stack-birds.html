How the birds read a stack

Psychological is the urge to fly. Insight comes first, so a room on the turn grows restless on the sand before anything else moves.

Relational power is how a bird stands with the others. Held at C, it keeps its patch, chasing one bird off and giving way to another; toward B it lets the others close, and in the air it follows them more nearly.

Social is how long a lone flight lasts before the structure brings the bird back down.

The room is read as the State Stack reads it, by its middle. Below the decision point every flight ends on the sand: nobody moves alone. Past it the flock holds whoever leans up, and a bird still near C stays on the sand, seen but not named.
