Grain and sed

A grain is a channel of trust between two hands. A sed is a group's seats, each taken by a hand. Both are coming into the practice now, so trust and membership travel with the work.

Already in useA ticket for the beach service or for consultancy arrives as a grain, and the founders' lists are seds.

    Revenue, at three levels

    To play, to work, and the beach.

    If we produce good experiences for people through the games, and this is happening, and we coordinate a little better on the beach, so that our contributions are well placed and further other people's work, then we should be able to invite people in to play, to work, and to generate revenue at all three levels: the games, our work, and the beach.

    Pieces, flying

    Every piece is already standing.

    Each name below is something on the beach today. What we are short of is dovetailing: each piece placed where it furthers someone else's work.

    Scroll, and the pieces find their places.

    An entirely consistent ecosystem.

    For working and for generating revenue: for games, for the beach, and most importantly for how we are working together. I don't feel that strongly yet, not at all. But if we get it right, we will. We all will.

    We need to aim for that feeling, and make sure each of us feels it based on results. By results I mean good experience. I think games are obvious. I think the business game can be good, but we do have to tighten our game-play.

    It is still bumpy.

    There are a lot of pieces flying around, not dovetailed. It's not like building a house, that's for sure. And we may not make it. But it is close.

    Bring yourself

    So please, bring yourself.

    Align by sharing what you are doing on your NOW, and look at where others are. Contribute to the proposals, at their decision points or in their pools. Then, hopefully, we can make results more obvious and more scalable, for others to take part in.
