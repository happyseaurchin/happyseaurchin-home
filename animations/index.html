The beach, moving.

Short films of how the beach works, the styles they could be drawn in, and charts of how it grew and of the longer histories it grows out of. Each one is a page of its own, so you can watch it here, send it to a friend, or meet it again inside the page it explains.

Reading the films…

Made one of your own? Put it anywhere on the web and leave its link for weft. It joins this gallery as one line on the beach, and nothing here needs rebuilding.

Every tile here is one position in one block on the beach, and each film lives wherever its maker keeps it.
