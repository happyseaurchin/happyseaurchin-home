Before It Breaks: the AI wave, and how an organisation catches it

Now is the sweet spot.

The longer you leave it, the faster and riskier the catch. One hour to meet, a few hours in a workshop, results within a week, and within ten days, the aim: more customers and more revenue.

Learning to surf is not about learning from what you get wrong. It is about learning what you get right, getting things done with others. The measure is a decision: a commitment to an objective, or a customer who buys.
