Full bandwidth

Set yourself to meet another person. You can only enter in B; the other decides.

Set yourself to meet another person, above all for the first time, so that the talk can leave the letterbox, where words are posted through a slot and the rest of each person stays behind the door. Nobody can open another person: you can only enter in B yourself, and the other decides, by what they do, whether it holds, falls back to C or rises to A. So the preparation is mostly within you, offered between you, and made visible to anyone who walks in. Read it before you meet; answer it afterwards.

Six words in plain terms

  • C, B and AThree states of any group, a class or a meeting of two. C is held together by control from above; B corrects itself among equals without opposition; A moves as one, with no centre.
  • The four phasesSensation, perception, thought and words: one moment at four speeds, the first two running before you are aware of them.

Step 1 of 6 · on the beach at 1

The letterbox and the full band

Where you are ↓

In shortMost first meetings pass only words. You can't open another person; you can open yourself and invite, at three levels at once.

Most first meetings run in C: words first, each position held up like a card, one talking while the other waits, every idea heard as X or Y. Through a letterbox only words pass. Full bandwidth runs the other way: perception before words, difference without opposition, X and Y. It cannot be made to happen to someone; it is entered, offered and made visible, at three levels at once, within you, between you and in the open, and the turn holds only while all three run together (the Fulcrum Turn).

The four phases · 1.2

Every moment runs through four at once, fastest to slowest: sensation, Q1; perception, belief and value, Q2; thought, attention and decision, Q3; words, expression and action, Q4. The first two are pre-conscious, and belonging lives in Q2. In the natural order what happens rises toward words; in the civilised inversion words come first and stir concepts, then feelings, then the body. The inversion is how people coordinate across distance and is not wrong in itself; it becomes a disorder when nobody notices it running (Fulcrum V−1, XQ; the psycho-social typography).

The three states in talk · 1.3

C leans on the parent, rendering history: a known pattern repeated and the frame obeyed. B faces the options beside its siblings: difference without opposition, X and Y where C hears X or Y. A is siblings converging on a shared objective from different positions without copying one another (solution-set:happyseaurchin 3.5). A comes and goes, falling back to B but never to C; B falls to C when a lapse is not self-corrected (V−2; the Fulcrum Turn).

Each phase, two forms · 1.4

Each phase runs in a positive form or an oppositional one (David, 13 November 2026). Words, Q4: logic, or argument. Thought, Q3: affirmation, or negation, the oppositional state proper, for the negative is a pure form of thought. Perception, Q2: like, or dislike, the emotional state. Sensation, Q1: aligned, or not. Talk starts at the top, in words, which is everyone's normal; full bandwidth goes down through the phases, keeping the ones above open. Two people both at Q4 talk as law and academia do; both at Q3, as political decision in government and business does; both listening at Q2, as therapy, religion and perhaps teaching do (V−1).

Why people frown · 1.5

C is the default because it works: whoever does well in C has every reason to keep the letterbox, and arrives in zero trust, wanting to understand before taking part. A frown, a block or a status move is C doing what C does, not a verdict on you. Talking about B changes nothing; it has to be induced and maintained (David, 14 October 2026), and it is induced by being in it.

The kernel, the meld and the meeting · 1.6

A kernel draws a person as a system (solution-set:<name>; David's is drawn at happyseaurchin.com/systemic-fulcrum). A meld lays two kernels side by side, blind first, and ends where live talk belongs (spine:mind-meld 1.111112). Silo Buster's first meeting turns on a newcomer's receptive state, which releases the money flow between the other two (spine:first-meeting 1.7). This walk is the human half of any such meeting, with or without a meld before it: kernels can be melded; only people can meet. A thread ties the four (function:full-bandwidth 5).

What your answer does · 1.7

A STRANGER, AFTER A MELD or AGAIN: on to setting yourself, with whom you are meeting in mind. NOT YET holds. Every answer in this walk is a reading of your own state, never of the other person: name no one.

Step 2 of 6 · on the beach at 1.1

Within you

Where you are ↓

In shortBefore you go in: start from what you share, settle into perception, meet the person rather than your file on them, and decide now to turn whatever triggers you.

The one level wholly yours, set before you meet and held throughout. Start from what you share with the other: the same moment, the same training in words first, the same freedom to set only your own state. Then phase toward Q2, perception before words, with three practices run together. Attend lightly to the breath as it happens, less effort rather than more (Induction). Read them live, as if into a blank page with everyone reading now: meet the person, not your file on them (Reflexive Reading, V1). Abandon Negative: decide before you go in that whatever triggers you, a frown, a block, a status move, you will turn; the intent made beforehand is what acts in the moment (StrategOS, V0).

The mutual conditions · 1.12

What you share with the other before a word is said: the same moving moment, where perception and belonging run ahead of thought; the same training, words first and C by default; the same freedom, each setting only their own state; and behind each, a world of roles, organisations and history. Orient to these: expect C, offer B, take no frown personally, demand nothing.

Blind first, here too · 1.13

The meld's one law is that your kernel is written before the other's is read, so that any agreement found later was reached independently (meld; solution-set:happyseaurchin 5.2). Its human form: prepare yourself, not your picture of them. If you have read their kernel or a meld's reading, carry what appeared between you as the agenda, and hold their kernel as their own self-portrait: the person in front of you is more than it, and this time is new.

The doorway · 1.14

David's first lesson with a new class (V−2): little planned, the liveness of the relationship put first; standing at the door as they arrive, saying hello, following a shy one a step in to catch their eye; then back to his own preparing, not watching them; at last a deep breath, his head lifted, and they are all looking at him, seated, silent and calm. 'I didn't have to ask for your attention … You gave me your attention.'

Turning, not suppressing · 1.15

Multiplying by −1 turns a quantity through half a turn: on the complex plane −1 is e^(iπ) (Euler). Two arrows pointing at each other are the oppositional state; turn yours through half a turn and the two point the same way. Abandon Negative neither denies a trigger nor buries it; it reverses its direction, from opposing the other to moving with them. Valuing the difference in front of you, Celebrate Other, is what gives the turn its energy (V0).

Two ways into Q2 · 1.16

The soft way: attend lightly, so that sense, thought and feeling are all attended and attention settles back into perception. The hard way: one point, the senses renounced (V−1). Fulcrum also names first-time experiences and reading people on first meeting among the ways in, though less dependable than practice: a first meeting already sensitises you, and the practice keeps it from being spent on a script.

What your answer does · 1.17

NOT AT ALL: perception held, and the meeting carried itself. A LITTLE or A LOT: where you compensated is where a level was missing; say where in your line, naming no one. NOT YET holds.

Between you

Where you are ↓

In shortFrom the first moment: let them lead, value what's different, build with "yes, and", and pause when it tips into one over the other.

Yours to offer, theirs to take up: B can only be invited by one person and empowered by both (V−2). Offer power-with. Let them lead first: the follower makes the leader, and a voluntary I < you invites collaboration (V−2); if both of you only defer, nobody meets and nothing moves, so take up the lead when it is offered back. Celebrate Other: value the difference in front of you, not merely allow it (V0). Yes, and…: no opposition, no judgement, no splitting (Action Cycle, V2). And when the talk tips into one over the other, yours or theirs, pause: the yellow card's half-minute of quiet, each considering their own part, yours first (V2).

Two stances, three pairings · 1.112

Meeting, each takes a stance: I > you, or I < you. Both above is conflict; both below, each placing the other first, is what the book calls distance: it invites fellowship, but until one of you takes up the lead there is no meeting and no progress (David, 13 November 2026). One above matched by one below is chosen or held: chosen by the one below, it is power-with; held in place, it is the steady pair of dominance and submission that, institutionalised, becomes C's chain of command. Add I = you and the pairings become nine; A comes when there are enough strong I = you relations, made between people, never declared in a charter (V−2, Power Shift from C to B-State).

The voluntary I < you · 1.113

The key is the I < you that is chosen, not forced: the student makes the teacher, the follower makes the leader (V−2). Tango, learnt as mutual following, has the leader follow the follower. Offered freely it invites collaboration; extracted, it is C.

The five rules, for two · 1.114

The Action Cycle's rules hold for any two people (V2): no oppositional state, just 'yes, and…'; no judgement, if it is simple assume it is genius; no splitting, keep the unity of the two and postpone nothing to divide it; no brainstorming, find the opportunity unique to the two of you; no pulling, no fillers of historical justification, attention back to the present.

The yellow card, for two · 1.115

In an Action Cycle anyone may lift the yellow card when a rule breaks, and everyone is silent for thirty seconds, each considering their role in what is happening (V2). Between two people it is a pause offered, never a penalty: name the pattern, not the person, and own your part first. A lapse named and corrected without rancour, by either of you, is B's own signature: in B anyone points it out and the one who lapsed corrects it (V−2).

What changes first · 1.116

The quality of listening: just before a group finds an objective none of them could reach alone, the listening in the room changes state (Open Business, V2). It is the first sign of B to look for.

What your answer does · 1.117

IT CHANGED: B was between you, at least for a while. IT DIDN'T: say where it stayed the same if you like, naming no one. NOT YET holds.

In the open

Where you are ↓

In shortMake it visible: one thing you're both here for, something you could do by next week, something passed forward, and a place to come back to.

What you make together, visible to anyone who walks in. Commit to Unity at the scale of two: what are you both here for, today (V0)? An Action Cycle in small: what could the two of you do by next week, just beyond realistic, with what each already has (V2)? Share forward: pass something on, an introduction, a link, credit, money that travels with what is shared rather than traded for a return (Sqale, V3). And leave a place to come back to. B holds only while all three levels run at once (the Fulcrum Turn).

Someone walking in · 1.1112

The social test of B: would someone walking in now see it (the Fulcrum Turn)? Not an interview, a pitch or a debate, but two people making something. C holds decisions by position and value as exchange; B is visible.

X and Y · 1.1113

A proposal heard as X or Y is C's logic, the excluded middle, as if there were one space on a small shelf and one of the two must fall; read as X and Y it is B's, concurrency, additive by default, the and holding both on the shelf (2 November 2026). The cheapest practice: say whether what you offer adds or replaces, and at which level it is meant.

A place to come back to · 1.1114

On the beach a meeting can have a page of its own, with its record and its room (spine:o-page-unit 1), so that what began between two people can be found again by both, and by the next person either brings.

The social result · 1.1115

Read over the hour, the day, the week and the month: more people, more mindmass, more moneyflow, more time (the Fulcrum Turn).

What your answer does · 1.1117

YES: the turn was visible. NO: it may still have been felt; the two readings before this one say where. NOT YET holds.

When they frown or block

Where you are ↓

In shortIf they frown or block: don't push and don't shut your door. Invite once more in another way, then let it be.

A frown, a block, a status move, words posted back through the slot: an oppositional state, C answering. Hold B: don't push, which is I > you, and don't withdraw, which shuts your own door. Turn what was triggered (step 2). Offer the invitation once more, in another phase: from argument to an example, from position to what they care about, from talk to a question you do not know the answer to. Then let it be. The other decides, now, next time or never; your door stays open, and what you do next is still yours.

The turn runs backwards too · 1.11112

A lapse that is not self-corrected becomes a conscious I > you power play: it collapses the room and calls power-over back, one bad apple spoiling the barrel (V−2). Between two people the bad apple can be you: the push to be understood is an easy way to make C.

C is valid · 1.11113

C keeps much of the world going, and a well-held C+ is a valid ideal of its own (Educere, V−2). Someone doing well in C owes you no change. Never use C as a punishment, and never treat a person as their state: a state belongs to the room, not to anyone in it (Educere).

Name no one · 1.11114

When a class's state drops, the teacher names no one: they know who they are (ABC State). Between two people, point to the pattern, never the person, and say your own part first.

Once, in another phase · 1.11115

If words are blocked, move toward perception: ask what they care about, what they are living with, what they would like to happen, and listen to the answer before your next sentence. Once, not twice: a second push is a demand.

What your answer does · 1.11117

HELD: you stayed in B while they opposed. LAPSED: you were pulled into C for a while; a reading, not a fault, and it sets your stance next time. NONE CAME: no frown or block came. NOT YET holds.

Step 6 of 6 · on the beach at 1.11111

Afterwards

Where you are ↓

In shortAfterwards: name the state for yourself, without judging, and let it set how you come next time. The proof is what holds when neither of you is holding it.

Name the state the meeting reached, as an evaluation and never a judgement, and let it set how you come next time (ABC State). Three readings, each answered only for yourself: did I have to compensate (step 2), did the quality of listening change (step 3), would someone walking in have seen it (step 4). Then the evidence that counts: what holds when neither of you is holding it, in the next exchange, the next week, the next person brought.

The state sets the stance · 1.111112

As a teacher David named the state of each class after every lesson and prepared for it next time: 'I know the attitude I need to take when picking up the class … I always enter in B state, and the class decides in their collective behaviour whether it remains at B, collapses to C or raises to A' (abc-state:happyseaurchin, 28 October 2026).

A is not a goal · 1.111113

Chasing A as better collapses a group back into C, into goal-setting and representations; A is induced, the social equivalent of a flow state, and it comes and goes, falling back to B but never to C (V−2; the Fulcrum Turn).

Proven by someone leaving · 1.111114

Whoever brings a state cannot be its evidence; the others holding it without them is (an observation in 'ABC State: from C to B, from B to A', 2 November 2026). Between two people the evidence is the second meeting: whether the other comes back at full bandwidth, or carries on what began, without you holding it.

Over many meetings · 1.111115

Kept over many meetings, these readings are your own iterative social validation: nobody is asked to believe it, only to try it and watch the social result (solution-set:happyseaurchin 3.2).

What your answer does · 1.111117

A, B or C: your reading of the state the meeting reached, for your own record and your next stance. NOT YET holds.
