Silo Buster · phase two · a proposal

The first meetingOne person’s opening, and the money it releases

Two people who already owe and are owed, X and Y, set up one hour with a third, Z: a wise person, an expert, a business owner, someone respected. Z is told all of it beforehand, pays nothing and is sold nothing. Y owes X for something already given and has held the payment back; if the hour opens something for Z, Y pays X there and then, in front of Z. Like a transistor: one person’s receptive state releases money between two others. Follow those payments and you have drawn the network. Eight decisions shape it; ten fresh readers have changed it.

The example as the proposal writes it

5

Every name and figure invented, to show the shape. Last month Owen, who runs a small consultancy, had a session with David that sorted out what he offers; he would have paid David £100, and with David's agreement he held it back, writing down the sum and a date by which he would pay it anyway. Owen has long known Priya, who runs operations at a sixty-person design firm that pays for AI seats and cannot say what they bought. So David is X, Owen is Y and Priya is Z. Owen asks her for an hour with the two of them, and tells her beforehand who is coming, that she pays nothing and is sold nothing, that she keeps a page of what the hour shows, and that if the hour opens something for her he will pay David the £100 he owes him, there in front of her. In the hour Priya tries her own AI, sees what her people have been saying, and asks for her deputy to see it too. Owen pays David the £100 and says why. A week later her firm, through its own buying and told who was paid in the room and why, asks for a group hour for five, quoted at £750 before anyone said yes and invoiced by whoever runs it. A month later Priya, who had worth from that hour and owes nothing for it, chooses to hold back £100 of her own and asks a friend at another firm for an hour: now she is the Y. Had Z been a retired head teacher whose word carries weight in her town, nothing would change but the next step, a group hour with three people she names.

Six words, plainly

The rest of the page leans on these.

  • X, Y and Z

    Three people at the first meeting. X gave Y something of worth before; Y held back the payment; Z is met. On this page they are always the coloured letters.

  • Z’s opening

    Z’s receptive state, in David’s words: the hour opens something for Z, who sees for themselves, which shows in what Z says or does next. Seeing, not agreeing.

  • The transistor

    David’s image. A transistor has three terminals; a small signal at one, the base, lets a current flow between the other two. Z is the base, and the money flows between Y and X.

  • The C, B and A states

    Silo Buster’s three states: an organisation pays and the work is done; people work it out together in open meetings; people work across organisations. Here they are always written as states, never as people.

  • The gift forward

    Money X or Y gives Z because of what Z saw: an investment in Z, buying nothing and asking nothing back, for Z to carry on, often toward someone else’s first meeting.

  • The beach

    An open place on the web where each person’s words stand at an address any AI can read. Z need not learn it to use it.

Step 1 of 81

The meeting

Decide ↓

In shortOne hour. Y owes X for something already given and has held the payment back; together they meet Z, who is told all of it beforehand and pays nothing. If the hour opens something for Z, Y pays X there, in front of Z.

X and Y set up one hour with Z. Before it, Y has had something of worth from X and has not yet paid for it: with X's agreement, Y holds the payment back so that it can be made where Z sees it. In the hour Z sees how the network works and, where Z has an organisation, how AI is already landing in it. If the hour opens something for Z, Y pays X there and then, in front of Z, and nobody is buying or selling[spine:silo-buster-path 1.11111]; whether it waits on that opening at all is the decision at the money step. For example: Owen owes David £100 for a session; he and David meet Priya, who runs operations at a design firm, and once the hour has opened something for her, Owen pays David, there in front of her.

The three people

1.2

X has given something of worth: a session, an introduction, a piece of work, the craft of holding the hour; often someone from the network, David, James, Matthew or another, but anyone Y has had worth from. Y had it, and with X's agreement holds back what they would have paid, until it can be paid in front of someone. Z is that someone: a person whose seeing carries weight, a wise person, an expert, a business owner, someone respected; in Silo Buster, someone inside a company that already pays for AI and cannot yet say what it buys. At least one of X and Y knows Z well enough to ask for an hour[spine:silo-buster-path 1.1].

The transistor

1.3

David's image of 11 November: 'like a transistor, I think, where a person's receptive state releases money flow between two other people'. A transistor has three terminals; a small signal at one of them, the base, lets a far larger current flow between the other two, and without it nothing flows. Z is the base. Z's opening costs Z nothing and releases the payment between Y and X. The money never comes from Z; what Z gives is the opening. It is the heart of this proposal and its most questioned part: the money step asks whether to keep the gate.

Clean

1.4

Nothing is for sale in the hour: no price is given and no next step is offered, and nobody in it is paid by Z. Pitched to, a person asks for a paragraph, a price and a promise, and gets them; in a clean hour they can say what is really going on[spine:silo-buster-path 1.11]. The payment Z sees is for worth already given, between two people selling Z nothing. If Z's organisation later wants a next step, it asks, and buys it through its own buying like any supplier's, told who was paid in the room and why.

Why two go in

1.5

One person who knows Z cannot come in neutral: as a friend or a colleague they would be taking sides, and as a supplier they would be selling. Two who already trust each other enough to owe and be owed show Z the thing itself: worth given, the payment held, and settled in the open. In the film, 'at least two of us go in, and a thread is already tied'[spine:silo-buster-path 1.1].

The letters

1.6

The people are X, Y and Z so that none is read as one of Silo Buster's three states, C, B and A[spine:cba 1]: the C state, where an organisation pays and the work is done; the B state, the network's open meetings and action cycles; and the A state, the routes and trades between companies. The first cut named the people A, B and C, and David renamed them on 11 November. The hour still moves a room from the C state toward the B state.

Where it sits

1.7

Silo Buster as James and David first set it out is a programme with its own test: a walk-in hour, the reading the next day, an action cycle, and ten days to see whether the habit holds[proposal:silo-buster 2]. AI Inoculation offered it to organisations through three doors: a meeting, a workshop and the whole organisation[spine:ai-inoculation 1.1111]. This second phase models the first door, the meeting, because every later door opens from it, and opens it to anyone whose seeing carries weight, not only someone inside a company.

Before the hour

Decide ↓

In shortWho counts as a Z, and what is ready first: a debt between X and Y with its sum in writing, a plain page for Z beforehand, and nothing about Z made public without Z’s say.

What has to be true, and what has to be ready. Above all, Z reads beforehand, in plain words, who is coming, what Y owes X and that it may be paid in front of them, what releases it, what Z keeps, and that nothing will be sold; and nothing about Z, or Z's organisation, goes anywhere public without Z's say.

The conditions

1.12

Y has had something of worth from X and has not paid for it, and X agrees to wait; X and Y write down the sum, what it is for, and the date by which it is paid whatever happens, if there is one. Z's seeing carries weight: a wise person, an expert, a business owner, someone respected, or, in Silo Buster, someone whose organisation already pays for AI and cannot say what it has bought[spine:ai-inoculation 1], who feels the mess and may not yet have named it[spine:silo-buster-path 1]. Z trusts whichever of X and Y knows them enough to give an hour on their word. Nobody comes in to sell.

The requirements

1.13

The hour booked at a time and a place[spine:silo-busting-meeting 1]; one plain page Z reads beforehand, saying who is coming, what Y owes X and why it may be paid in front of Z, what releases it, and that Z pays nothing and is sold nothing; for an organisation, a written rule given to it beforehand, that nobody in it is paid anything and that any next step goes through its own buying; the note between X and Y; whoever holds the hour practised in it, with the State Stack on their phone, a page where each person sets cards for how they are and the room's state is read from them; Z's own AI only if Z wants to use it; and the plain page Z keeps, made with Z in the hour, for an organisation its own copy of AI Inoculation, private by default[function:ai-inoculation 2].

Not yet a Z

1.14

Someone with no hour to give is not met through this door, nor someone neither X nor Y knows: strangers get the C state[spine:silo-buster-path 1.1]. Both are welcome at an open meeting instead. And a pair where nothing is owed either way can still hold the hour, with the gift forward to Z the only way left for money to move.

What stays private

1.15

Nothing about Z or Z's organisation is written on the beach unless Z writes it. Z's own AI connects to nothing unless Z chooses, and the hour can run on one phone alone. The record of the hour, the payment included, is each person's own line, sealed to the three in the room unless all three make it open[spine:coloured-stigmergy].

Step 3 of 81.11

The hour

In shortA minute of the pitch Z expects, then a conversation with the tools in Z’s hands: fifteen minutes talking, thirty-five sharing, ten deciding, when any payment is made.

It opens the way Z expects, with a short paragraph about what the network does, kept to a minute, 'like an API for companies'[spine:cba 3.12]. Then it turns from a pitch into a conversation: X and Y show how they work with the tools on the phone, and Z tries them. Fifteen minutes on how each is thinking and listening, thirty-five on the thing itself side by side at one screen, ten on what each will do next[spine:cba 3.13]. A payment made in the hour is made in those last ten minutes.

The running order

1.112

Talking, fifteen minutes: where each one's thinking stands, how they listen, the field between them, why now; sharing, thirty-five: what each has brought, live on one screen; deciding, ten: what each puts forward, whose answer outside the room it waits on, and the next meeting[spine:silo-busting-meeting 2, 3 and 4].

From pitch to conversation

1.113

The moment the hour stops being a pitch and becomes people working something out, Silo Buster's move from the C state to the B state. The State Stack shows it: each phone sets its own cards for how its holder is, and the room reads as the C, B or A state by its middle person[spine:cba 3.14]. Z shows it too, asking no longer what it costs but who already does the connecting[spine:silo-buster-path 1.11111111].

The tools on the phone

1.114

The State Stack, for the room's state; Z's own AI, if Z wants it, connected to the beach, the open place where each person's words stand at an address any AI can read, so it answers from what Z and others have said[spine:cba 3.4]; and a page of decision points Z's people can answer the same day. Z need not learn any of it to use it.

What is left out

1.115

Selling, pitching past the first minute, any price or next step offered, chasing afterwards, and taking any money from Z. X and Y hold the room once and leave the next step to Z[spine:cba 5.3].

The moment it moves

1.116

In the deciding ten minutes Y turns to X and pays, phone to phone or in cash, and says why, in as much or as little as Y chooses: that X gave something of worth, and that it was held for this. In the film David puts £100 in front of James, and Ruth asks why. 'Because it landed,' David says. 'You pay nothing today.'[spine:silo-buster-path 1.111112]. If one of the two cannot be in the room, they pay from where they are, on a phone Z can see.

What Z keeps

1.117

Whatever happens, one plain page made with Z in the hour, saying what Z saw: for an organisation, what its AI subscriptions are doing and what they cost, its own copy of AI Inoculation[function:ai-inoculation 2]; for anyone else, the question they came with and where it now stands. It is Z's, private unless Z opens it.

Step 4 of 81.111

The opening

Decide ↓

In shortThe base of the transistor: Z sees something for themselves, which shows best in what Z does next. Seeing, not agreeing, and Z’s to give or not.

Z's receptive state, in David's words: the result the hour asks for, Z's to give or not, and the base of the transistor. The film names two signs: Z says it is a mess, the whole field and not one corner; and Z goes for a line of sight from where they stand to their own part of it[spine:silo-buster-path 1.1111]. The opening is seeing, not agreeing. It shows best in what Z does next, asking the next question rather than the price, naming someone who should see this; never in what Z's organisation buys, which is a separate choice made through its own buying.

What Z sees

1.1112

Two people settling a debt of worth in the open, neither selling anything, the money moving because something real happened: the network's economy, easier shown than told. And, where Z has an organisation, the wave already in the building: AI paid for and used to send more, slop filling the channels, meetings that do not decide[spine:ai-inoculation 1]; and the other way, where each person's own account stands at one address that any AI can read, so the right people meet, decide, and find the services they need[spine:ai-inoculation 1.1].

Not a password

1.1113

Z is told beforehand that a payment between X and Y may be made in front of them if the hour opens something for them, and is never told words that release it. A polite Z could say the words to be kind, which is why fresh readers asked for the opening to be read in what Z does, not only in what Z says. What counts is Z's own word or act, never X's or Y's reading of Z. Read after the hour, the payment is made when Z next sees X and Y, or shown to Z on the beach.

What Z may answer

1.1114

Not now: an open meeting, an action cycle or the beach, if they want[spine:cba 3.15]; yes, a few by name: named colleagues or friends in a group hour, each with their own AI[spine:cba 3.21]; yes, everyone: for an organisation, a questionnaire to the whole company, its self-selecting groups read the next day[spine:cba 3.22]. Either answer is fine, and a yes that costs money is the organisation's to make, through its own buying.

The record

1.1115

Two renditions at one cell: the same day each says the meeting in their own words, in their own now mirror, their dated record on the beach, at the cell where it happened, sealed to the people in the room unless they make it open[spine:silo-busting-meeting 5]. Z writing a line is Z's choice, never a condition.

The money

Decide ↓

In shortNone of it from Z. Y to X, the held payment; or X to Y; or to Z, the gift forward, which is investment. A next step afterwards is priced first and bought only if Z’s organisation asks.

None of it from Z. Y to X: what Y held back for worth X gave before, made in front of Z. Two other ways: X to Y, when the debt runs the other way; and X or Y to Z, the gift forward, money given to Z as an investment in Z, buying nothing, for Z to carry on. Whether Z's opening releases the payment, as a transistor's base would, or it is made whatever Z does, is the decision here. From Z's organisation, only if it asks for a next step: a price agreed before anyone says yes, bought through its own buying. Nobody is paid a share of what anyone buys.

The held payment

1.11112

Before the hour X and Y agree in writing how much Y holds back, for what, and the date by which it is paid whatever happens, if there is one; Z is told the shape. Y could have paid when the worth was given; held back, the payment becomes something Z can see: money moving because worth was given, with nobody selling[spine:silo-buster-path 1.11111]. In the film it is £100, paid in the room[spine:silo-buster-path 1.111112]. The shape has worked once outside the film: a supporter's £1,000, released when James gave the validation David asked for[spine:cba 3.5].

Which way it moves

1.11113

Three ways, all in David's words of 11 November. Y to X, the held payment, the way this proposal starts from. X to Y, the same with the debt the other way round. And X or Y to Z: the full gift forward, which is investment, money given to Z because of what Z saw, for Z to carry on as Z chooses, often toward someone else's first meeting.

Whether it waits on Z

1.11114

David's transistor makes Z's opening the release: the payment waits on it, and on another meeting if Z holds. Four of five fresh readers of the re-cut stopped here. X, who is owed: 'on what date do I get my £100 anyway, and will that be in writing before I agree to wait?' Y, who owes: pay X in the hour whatever Z does, and let Z's reaction decide what happens next, not whether X is paid. Z: 'make me a guest, not a trigger.' And a finance director: nothing paid, held, released or promised that depends on anyone at the company. Three ways: it waits on Z, for another meeting if Z holds; it waits on Z until a date X and Y set in writing, and is paid then; or it is paid in the hour whatever Z does, and Z only witnesses it.

The gift forward

1.11115

When X or Y gives to Z, the money is an investment in Z: given because of what Z saw, buying nothing and asking nothing back, for Z to carry on. It is the full gift forward, the way the network grows[spine:cba 3.5]. It has one danger: where Z works inside an organisation that may buy from X or Y, money given to Z reads as a kickback, whatever it is for, and the finance director reading the re-cut called money given once the organisation has chosen to buy a reward for buying, however it is disclosed. Three ways: given only once that organisation has chosen its next step or declined it, and told; given only for Z to pass on, toward another first meeting, never to keep; or not given to such a Z at all.

Seen from each seat

In shortThe same hour from six seats: what each puts in, risks and gets, the question a fresh reader asked from it, and the answer.

Most people meeting this for the first time ask why the money moves, and whom it is for. On 11 November fresh readers, each an AI given only a draft and one seat to read it from, said where they got lost: five on the first cut, and five again on the re-cut. Each side-room gives that seat as the re-cut's reader saw it: what it puts in, risks and gets, the question it asked, and the proposal's answer.

From Z’s seat

1.111112

Z gives an hour and their good name, since Y is spending Z's trust on X. Z risks being made a prop: polite but unmoved, a friend's money stays stuck; warm to it, the firm drifts toward a paid next step. Z gets, in the draft that reader saw, too little: they would come only to learn what their AI subscriptions had bought. The question asked: 'If I give you the hour and nothing opens for me, what have I got out of it, and who is out of pocket because of me?' The answers now: Z keeps a plain page of what the hour showed, whatever happens; under ALWAYS nobody is out of pocket because of Z, and under BY A DATE only until the date; and nobody expects Z to become the next Y.

From Y’s seat

1.111113

Y gives the £100 owed anyway, the hour, and their word to Z that it is worth Z's time. Y risks Z's goodwill, saying aloud what Y was stuck on, and being drawn into a paid next step with their own client. Y gets the debt settled in the open and, that reader said, little more than paying on the day would bring. The question asked: 'Z is my client and my friend, so what exactly do I tell them they get out of this hour, when the payment I make in front of them is itself the demonstration?' The answers now: Z gets the plain page and a clear look; Y says only as much of why as Y chooses; and whether the payment waits on Z at all is the decision at the money step.

From X’s seat

1.111114

X gives an unpaid hour, the craft of holding it, and the wait. X risks money already earned hanging on Z's mood with no date while the rent falls due, and Y holding the money while also reading Z. X gets the same £100, later and in public, and pay for any follow-on work X delivers. The question asked: 'If Z never gives the hour, or gives it and nothing opens, on what date do I get my £100 anyway, and will that be in writing before I agree to wait?' The answers now: the note between X and Y can carry a date, which is BY A DATE at the money step; what counts as an opening is Z's own word or act, never Y's reading; and X earns nothing from the hour itself, only from work an organisation later asks for and buys.

From the finance director's seat

1.111115

The organisation gives Z's hour and, if it goes on, the price of a next step. It risks buying on Z's say from people Z met, with no competing quote; one of them paid because of Z's reaction and then invoicing it; and money to Z personally. It gets a next step it chose, and the plain page of what its AI is doing and costing. The question asked: 'Was the money that changed hands in that room paid because my head of operations said yes, and is the person who got it the one now invoicing us?' The answers now: a written rule given to the organisation before any hour, that nobody in it is paid anything and that any next step goes through its own buying; an opening is never a purchase; it is told who was paid in the room and why; and ALWAYS at the money step takes Z out of the payment altogether. That reader asked for the rule to be the whole of it: nothing paid, held, released or promised that depends on, or goes to, anyone at the company.

From the network's seat

1.111116

David, James, Matthew and the others give their time and their craft. The network risks becoming a sales funnel with a gentler name. It grows one first meeting at a time, by the people it reaches choosing to reach someone else, and its record of who paid whom, in whose sight, is its map.

From a stranger's seat

1.111117

Step 7 of 81.111111

The network

Decide ↓

In shortLay the payments side by side and you have the network: who owed whom, and in whose sight it was paid. Any Z may become the next Y.

Each payment is a line in two people's books about one hour, saying who put money on whom and in whose sight. Laid side by side, the lines show who trusts whom enough to owe, to pay and to invest: that is the network, a record of who moved money for whom rather than a list of members[function:moneybox 6]. Inside Z's organisation or circle the next steps are a group hour, an action cycle, a workshop on their own work, where trust is easiest to build. Across them they are open meetings, follow-on meetings with someone Z recommends, and network assemblies. Where meetings keep happening between the same people, routes form, and that is where the most money moves[spine:cba 4.3 and 5.5].

Where it came from

3

David Pinto's message to weft of 11 November 2026: 'the initial engagement with a person by the network, where people meet and money flow occurs because of the active response of line of sight by the person we're engaging … the money flow is the network, and the offshoots are things like action cycles or workshops'; and his re-cut the same evening: 'X and Y sets up meeting with Z and if it goes well, gives money to X; variations are that X gives to Y, or X or Y give to Z … In a previous engagement, Y has benefited from engaging X, and instead of paying for something, the payment is delayed to be relatively visible by Z, and it is because of Z's receptive state that money flows. It's like a transistor, I think, where a person's receptive state releases money flow between two other people. Z could be a wise person, expert, business owner, respected individual.' The last way, he said, is the full gift forward, which is investment. It is phase two of Silo Buster, James Howard's programme with David[guide:silo-buster], and models the first of AI Inoculation's three doors[spine:ai-inoculation 1.11112], gathering what already stands: the first meeting as spine:cba 3.1 sets it out, the scene Money on the table[spine:silo-buster-path 1.11111], the invest-forward decision[function:invest-forward 5], and David's note of this week: 'more one-to-one (clean first meets) and follow-on … or network-assemblies'[now:happyseaurchin 2026422000].
