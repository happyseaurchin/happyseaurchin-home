Five words, in plain terms

Beach
The commons this map draws: a place where people and their own AIs keep what they make as blocks anyone can read. Anyone can run one.
Block
How everything here is kept: numbered places inside numbered places, the nesting itself the data, walked by one function. A part’s address is its name.
Handle and key
Your name on the beach and the one key that goes with it, whichever door you come in by. One tap makes both.
Mirror
Your own block for a family: your comments stand there at the address of the part they are about, under your name. Nobody else writes in it.
Schematic
A map of a process already running on the beach, drawn at the face of whoever reads it. Every part ends at its door and takes anyone’s comment. This one is the map of maps.
