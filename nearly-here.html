The business, at three levels

Products, systems, and the ground.

An ecology, each level a business in its own right and each already standing: products anyone gets at once; services and systems that change how organisations work; and the platform and ecosystem beneath them all, the beach. Every venture on the beach has a part at each level, as the garden draws them for an investor to walk.
