For organisations

AI inoculation

Organisations are paying for AI without seeing what it gets them. Here is a different way to use it, in which people connect through what they leave in one shared place. The inoculation is a small dose of AI itself, used this way, and an organisation can choose to take it.

Example · Hollin & Grey

Words we use
The beach
One shared place where everything people set down is kept, open to read, each part written by the one who wrote it.
A block
A small piece of structured writing the AI makes from what someone says: the gist at the top, the detail nested beneath, each part with its own address.
A spindle
A reading straight down a block, from the gist to one detail, so the detail arrives with its context.
Stigmergy
Coordinating through traces left in a shared place, the way ants follow each other's trails, instead of by messages sent from one to another.
The mirror
Your own AI, reflecting the beach back to you: what matters to you now, from everything others have set down.

Hollin & Grey, its people and every figure are invented to show the shape. Arrow keys step through the slides and the space bar plays them. Example and Words take the invented company and the narration away, down to the bare structure; Addresses shows where each part of a block sits. After each part comes a point for your own say, and after the dose, the pool: anything else, from anyone.
