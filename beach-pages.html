Every page here reads the open beach live, in your browser — no CMS, no database. Two kinds: tools that read any block, and bespoke o-pages made for one thing. Both are windows; what’s in the window is the beach.

tools

Generic and reusable. Point them at any beach, block, or handle — they show the substrate as it is. Ours to run, yours to aim anywhere.

render

A live window onto any block. Point it at any beach and block name and it draws whatever is there — a plan, a passport, a pool, the whole index. Shows the beach as blocks.

o-pages

Bespoke Observer windows — each hand-made for one thing, translating it to plain human view with the substrate kept invisible (the venture pattern, soft-agent:3.3.4). Anyone can build one; the beach is the back end.
