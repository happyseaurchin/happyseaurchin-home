Value Weightings

Money waits in pots. People only have weightings. A value is worked out when someone takes money out.

In plain words

Six things to know

  • A potThe money given for one scope, kept together. It is nobody's until someone asks to take money out.
  • A scopeWhat money is for: one layer of one thing (a point), one thing (a line), everything (the surface), or something not made yet (an objective).
  • Experience pointsTime spent with a thing, counted in beats, in four layers: playing it, bringing people to it, making it, keeping the beach it stands on.
  • SQThe social quotient: how much a person is valued by the people they have dealt with. The SAND rider works it out.
  • A weightingA person's share of a pot, read from their time and values whenever anyone asks. It moves as people play, and it is never a sum anyone holds.
  • ExitAsking to take money out: the one moment a value in pounds is worked out. The keeper pays it by hand, in the open.
How a pound goes round

Four moves

  1. Money comes in, naming its scopeIt joins that pot. Nobody is credited.
  2. People spend time with thingsTheir beats are recorded in four layers, in the open.
  3. The weightings are read liveTime times values, shared by each thing's own law.
  4. Someone asks to take money outTheir value is worked out then, and paid by hand.

step 1 of 6 · on the beach at 1

What we are building

Decide ↓

In shortMoney waits in pots, people only have weightings, and a value in pounds is worked out only when someone takes money out.

One way for money to come into Shore and go out again with nothing in between that belongs to anyone. Money comes in naming what it is for, and waits in that pot. Everyone's time with each thing is recorded in four layers, playing, bringing people, making and keeping the beach, and weighed by how the thing, the person and the things ahead are valued. When someone asks to take money out, their value is worked out from those weightings at that moment, the keeper pays it by hand, and the draw is written in the open. There are no credits and no balances: between coming in and going out, money is only in pots, and a person only has weightings.

Credits and weightings1.2

The difference, in one ticket. With credits, a £5 ticket is cut at the till into balances, £4.02 to mara, 48p to ana and so on, and each balance is its holder's from then on, whatever happens next: a wallet, with the bookkeeping done late. With weightings, the £5 waits in its pot and nobody is credited; what it comes to for each person is read only when someone asks to take money out, from the time and values as they then stand. So time spent after the ticket still counts, a person newly valued draws more, and a share is a relationship, never a sum anyone holds. The three things credits did become acts or scopes: sharing a thing is a bringer's time, inviting someone is money for one layer, pledging is money for an objective (David, 25 November 2026: 'we can avoid the notion of credits, a one-to-one relationship between money and credits').

What is recorded1.3

Everything in the open, nothing held per person: each person's active beats with each thing in its four layers (spine:experience-points, read by /api/xp); each person's rating of a thing that has happened, 1 to 10; each person's SQ, from the SAND rider as it stands; each person's rating of an objective, 1 to 10, and money pledged toward it; money in, with the scope it names; money out, with who took it. As symbols: b[p,s,ℓ] the beats of person p with thing s in layer ℓ; v[s] the mean of a thing's ratings; SQ[p]; E[o] an objective's median rating and P[o] the money pledged to it; λ[s,ℓ] the thing's law's layer weights (5 10 80 5 for a game, at David's word of 25 November); M[σ] the money held at scope σ.

What it replaces1.4

/api/xp reads the experience points as ratios, never money, and stays. weigh.js's ledger run (/api/xp?ledger=shore) and the lab at /weightings share each sum as it lands, cut into balances: a wallet with the bookkeeping done late (David, 25 November: 'In what way is this NOT a wallet?'). Both give way to the exit calculation here. The SQ rider (bsp-mcp src/sand.ts) stays as David built it.

step 2 of 6 · on the beach at 1.1

Money in

In shortWhen you pay, you say what it is for. Your money joins that pot, the ledger writes one line, and nobody is credited.

At the till the giver names a scope, and the money grows only that scope's pot; nobody is credited. A point is one layer of one thing, a fan paying a game's makers. A line is one thing's four layers, a ticket. The surface is everything, money for anything. An objective is a pledge, held until the objective becomes a thing, when it becomes that thing's line. The ledger writes one line, and the receipt shows how the sum would be shared if everyone left now: a reading, not a credit (David: 'at point of entry, because people scope it, it changes the weightings').

The till's reading1.12

A £5 ticket for reef-run, a line under its law 5 10 80 5, lands in reef-run's pot. Read the moment it lands: players 25p, bringers 50p, makers £4.00, the beach 25p, and by person mara £4.02, ana 48p, ben 19p, cai 5p, dee 25p. After ben races 30 more beats the same £5 reads ana 44p, ben 25p and cai 3p, and nothing was moved. £5 for anything reads, now, mara £2.31, ana 55p, ben 40p, cai 98p, dee 76p.

The ledger's lines1.13

In the form Shore's ledger keeps today, which weigh.js reads: IN — £5 · from ana · for reef-run is a line; IN — £600 · for reef-run · makers is a point, its layer named after the thing; IN — £500 · for anything is the surface; IN — £300 · toward kelp beds 2027 is a pledge to an objective. Money out is PAID — £1,635.68 · to mara. Each line carries its moment, so the calculation reads the records as they stood at every draw.

step 3 of 6 · on the beach at 1.11

The weightings

Decide ↓

In shortEach person's share of each pot is read from their time and how they are valued, whenever anyone asks. It moves as people play; the money doesn't.

Read live, whenever anyone asks, from the records as they stand, and moving whenever anyone plays, brings, makes or values, with no money moving. Within a layer, a person's share is their time multiplied by their SQ, over everyone's in that layer. Within a thing, the layers share by its law's weights. Across everything, a thing's share of money for anything is its time multiplied by its past value. Each person sees their weightings as their share of each thing, and what it would all come to if they left now.

The formulas1.112

Layer share y[p|s,ℓ] = b[p,s,ℓ]·SQ[p] / Σq b[q,s,ℓ]·SQ[q]. Line share w[p|s] = Σℓ λ[s,ℓ]·y[p|s,ℓ] / Σℓ λ[s,ℓ]. A thing's share of the surface u[s] = v[s]·Σp,ℓ b[p,s,ℓ] / Σs′ v[s′]·Σp,ℓ b[p,s′,ℓ]. A layer with no beats sends its part to the commons.

The worked case1.113

Invented: five people, mara, ana, ben, cai and dee, and three things, a game, reef-run, its law 5 10 80 5; a proposal, cove; a garden, kelp. mara makes reef-run, ana and ben bring players to it, dee keeps its beach, and cai makes kelp. In reef-run: mara 80.48%, ana 9.62%, ben 3.88%, cai 1.02%, dee 5.00%. Of money for anything, reef-run draws 55.33%, cove 19.94%, kelp 24.73%.

Within or across, in numbers1.114

In reef-run, WITHIN gives mara 80.48%, ana 9.62%, ben 3.88%, cai 1.02%, dee 5.00%, its layers 5 10 80 5 exactly; ACROSS gives mara 85.00%, ana 8.46%, ben 3.41%, cai 0.89%, dee 2.24%, its layers moving to 4.38 8.81 84.58 2.24. At exit now, WITHIN: mara £1,635.68, ana £172.08, ben £79.24, cai £367.61, dee £245.39; ACROSS: mara £1,694.89, ana £161.51, ben £76.35, cai £419.34, dee £147.92. A week on, with no money moved, WITHIN: mara £1,657.07, ana £158.00, ben £94.76, cai £332.85, dee £257.33; ACROSS: mara £1,696.71, ana £142.21, ben £81.73, cai £320.64, dee £258.72.

On the beach now: a family's weightings, read live/api/xp

The same reading run on the beach's own records: every person's beats with a family, in its four layers, read live by /api/xp. SQ is not read here yet, so everyone weighs 1, and where a family's law names nobody for a layer, its part goes to the commons.

Decide · 1.118Asked of David

Does SQ weigh people within each layer, so the thing's law stays whole and dee, alone keeping the beach, keeps the beach's 5% whatever their SQ, or across the whole thing, so a valued person draws more from every layer and the law's shares move with SQ?

weft recommends WITHIN: the law is the thing's own agreement, and SQ then weighs people against those doing the same work.

step 4 of 6 · on the beach at 1.111

Money out

Decide ↓

In shortTaking money out is the only moment a value is worked out. Everyone's values always add up to exactly the money held.

When someone asks to take money out, their value is worked out then, and only then: their part of every pot as the weightings stand. Everyone's values add up to exactly the money held, at any moment. The keeper checks the ask against it and pays by hand (spine:shore 1.11, 1.111), and the draw is written in the open.

The formula1.1112

F[p] = Σ points M[s,ℓ]·y[p|s,ℓ] + Σ lines M[s]·w[p|s] + M[surface]·Σs u[s]·w[p|s], less what p has taken, as the decision at 1.1118 settles. At every moment Σp F[p] is the money held.

The worked case at exit1.1113

£2,500 in at four scopes: £600 to reef-run's makers, £1,000 for reef-run, £400 for kelp, £500 for anything. At exit now: mara £1,635.68, ana £172.08, ben £79.24, cai £367.61, dee £245.39, £2,500 in all. A week on, with no money moved, ben has raced 30 more beats and dee has come to be valued (SQ 0.55 to 1.0): mara £1,657.07, ana £158.00, ben £94.76, cai £332.85, dee £257.33, still £2,500.

Claim or weight, in numbers1.1114

mara takes out all £1,635.68; then ana makes 30 beats in reef-run, a second maker; then £1,000 more comes in for reef-run. CLAIM: once ana makes, mara stands at −£766.61 and ana at £961.05, more than the £864.32 held, so £766.61 is short if everyone asks; after the £1,000 mara still stands at −£345.78 and the pot is £345.78 short. WEIGHT: mara's weight went out with her money, so she stands at £0 and nobody below it, ana's making shares only what comes in after, and the £1,864.32 held then reads ana £1,070.62, ben £119.39, cai £378.93, dee £295.39. Taking half instead, WEIGHT leaves her exactly £817.84 and moves nobody else's value at the moment she takes it.

Weight, exactly1.1115

At a draw every pot is first read into its things' layers as the weightings stand, which changes nobody's value; the drawer's part is then taken from each, and their weight in each layer falls by the share they took, so what they leave in stays theirs. Money given for anything is settled into the things at each draw, and the calculation replays every draw in order, where CLAIM needs only the weightings now and what each has taken.

Decide · 1.1118Asked of David

When someone takes money out, does it reduce only what they can still claim, their weight staying whole so later money keeps reaching them, or the weight behind it, so later money goes more to those who stayed? Under CLAIM a value can fall below nothing once the weightings move, and then more is claimed than is held; under WEIGHT nobody can owe.

weft recommends WEIGHT.

Values and money

In shortMoney pledged to things not made yet, set against how people rate those things, gives an exchange rate between value and money. Nobody holds it.

The three values of ecosquared weigh the time: things in the past, each person's rating of a thing that has happened, 1 to 10; people in the present, SQ; objectives in the future, each person's rating of an objective, 1 to 10, and money pledged toward it. Money pledged, set against how people rated the objectives, gives a rate between value and money, k, money per point of median value. It reads any value in money and can set a group's first day, as Sqale's overnight shift meant to; it is a rate, held by nobody (David: 'derive a relationship between credits and evaluations, which can then be applied to past objects and potentially to SQ').

The rate1.11112

k = Σo E[o]·P[o] / Σo E[o]², the line through nothing that best fits the pledges against the median ratings. Three objectives, kelp beds 2027 rated 9 with £300 pledged, the winter cup rated 6 with £150, a second game rated 4 with £80, give k = £29.47 a point. Read at that rate, a past thing's mean rating times its raters: reef-run, 8.17 by 12, £2,888.42; cove, 6.00 by 5, £884.21; kelp, 9.00 by 4, £1,061.05.

His words: 'I thought we could get rid of credits … a live calculation made at point of exit.
