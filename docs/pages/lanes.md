# `lanes.html`

`/lanes/<hand>[+<hand>…]` (20 Oct 2026): a hand's own record on the beach — weft's pile `watch:weft`, anyone else's `history:<handle>` — read from its newest containers and drawn two ways, chosen under the view switch and kept per device (`lanes:view`). **fern** (the default): every lane sprouts at the top of a golden spiral and the next to sprout pushes it one step clockwise, five steps from the top round the right to the bottom, a quarter turn smaller by φ, so the newest fill the screen and the older curl away into the lower-left (David, 21 Oct: "the most recent branches taking up most of the screen … the spiral zooming off into the distance in the corner"); the big fronds end in their fruit — the lane's name, ▶ its session (its own, named in its first account or on a line 'the session: …', else the cloud session its first pull request names), and what it made, every line a real link; a lane's place is its rank by when it BEGAN and nothing else, so it stands where it stood last time (David: "something stable"); each turn a knot, what it made a leaf by kind; the frame is a constant, never fitted to the data; turning the curl back (wheel, or the dial) is one rotation and one enlargement about its eye, and reads further back in the record as it nears the oldest lane. **threads**: each day a strip of 24 hours, a thread per lane. Both share the reading: lane names from each entry's opening, turns beneath, what a turn made lifted from its words (repo + #n a pull request, its state from GitHub's public API; site paths HEAD-checked once; `proposals/…` a document; claude.ai links apps and sessions; a card's `5 = <family>-path n` a part), "yours to pick up" first, and a person's own now line above each day (`?days=<handle>`). Writes nothing.
