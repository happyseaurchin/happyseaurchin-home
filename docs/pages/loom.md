Two views of one read, the first screen the page's opening and the three layers a second view (`#layers`). THE FIRST SCREEN (step 1, lane loom.1): one screen for what is live now and what asks something of David (spine:loom 1.1; his GO at the build order 1.11118 and NEW PAGE at 1.111128). It opens on now, every hand's lines at this gathering of the clock (each `torus-mirror:<hand>` touched in the last hours, read at the gathering with its seconds' stamps), newest first with how long ago, a line at the current beat glowing; then a key, then every lane, project and decision as a tile in four bands: RED yours to attend (`lanes:weft` 1 and 2; a decision at a step's 8 naming David with no word at that address in `<name>:happyseaurchin`), GREEN started and not complete (`lanes:weft` 3; a proposal where his GO or CHANGE stands and the step is not yet complete by its threads (where no registry thread serves it, a STATE line of BUILT or LIVE at its 9), its state the next move and the lane that names its address; his open cards in `ahead:happyseaurchin`), YELLOW weft's suggestions (`lanes:weft` 9.2, and weft's answers where the decision does not wait on him), BLUE complete (`lanes:weft` 4, folded; nothing of his outstanding; a card done this week). RED also holds every thread whose live end waits on the reader, read from the registry whether or not a proposal's law lists it (threads.js `waiting`, phase 2 of threads, 29 Oct 2026): a tile per live end, its words and each open option with TAKE, which says the option's words and ' · waits on <offerer>' into `thread:<you>` at the option's address, through the same `say`. A proposal is one tile coloured by its most demanding decision, each decision a line beneath it linking to its own card on `/proposal/<name>` (`#d-<its address>`, the card's id; until loom.3 they opened at the step's head). Every red tile carries the words that close it, each a button (step 3, lane loom.3, his BOTH at 1.118): a tap says the word where the thing already takes words, through the beach's voice (`siteVoice`, voice.js, the reader's name and key kept in their own browser), with no confirmation, and the tile repaints from the beach. A waiting decision's line carries its card's own words, said into `<name>:<you>` at its address as the card says it (the reader's own mirror read straight from the beach, since `/api/mirrors` is kept a minute); a red lane GO, NOT YET, CHANGE or STOP (function:bee 2's words) into `bee:<you>` at the lane's first entry in the pile, as the lanes page's word box says them, where the bee reads them at its next pass, and a lane with a word of yours goes green (GO, CHANGE) or blue (NOT YET, STOP); a lane with no entry in the pile takes no words of its own, so its word goes to `loom:<you>` 1.11 naming the lane. A line of your own goes where the tile's link lands. `/loom?as=<handle>` reads and says as another reader (a test's throwaway handle); otherwise the loom is David's, whatever name another page left on the device. The lanes the bee parked after David's first look (`lanes:weft` 5, nested 5.1 from ring 1 and 5.2 from ring 2) are one folded tile beneath the red band, 'Parked: ten older asks', never red, each line opening on its lane. Live is not a fifth colour: an orange glow over whichever colour a weft lane's tile has when it spoke at this beat (David at loom 1.18: 'orange perhaps, glowing'). A source that does not answer is a grey tile, never a guess, and a beat that cannot be read says so. Lane tiles open `/lanes/weft#lane=<name>`, which opens that lane's story at once, saying it is coming, and fills it when the lanes are drawn (`comingFromHash` and `openFromHash` in `lanes.html`); an offer opens on its lane once the lane has an account in the pile, else on its own words at `lanes:weft` 9.2. Everything is read live (the list at `proposals`, each `spine:` and `function:`, `/api/mirrors`, the reader's own mirror of each family, `bee:` and `loom:` of the reader, and `watch:weft` as far back as the lanes page reads it, six days or six containers, so both pages find one first entry for a lane); it keeps nothing, and writes only the words the reader taps. Re-reads every three minutes while in view. THE THREE LAYERS (step 2, lane loom.2, David's GO at spine:loom 1.8): projects above, lanes across, blocks below, read from the same pass and nothing else. Above, every proposal on the list as a chip with its furthest STATE and a dot for each decision in its colour. Across, every lane as a thread in its colour (the register's 1 to 3, its 4 and the accounts in the pile as far back as the lanes page reads, joined where one session wrote under two names, bee.1 then bee.2), settled lanes older than a day and the parked ones each folded into one row. Below, every block the drawn lanes' words name, as the beach's index holds it (the most specific name it has), grouped by the project it belongs to (its spine, law, room, files, mirrors, archives and the threads its law names at 5), then weft's own record, then elsewhere. A lane rises to the projects it serves (its name's first word, the families of the blocks it names, a step named by its address, a page named in full; a name or bare path in passing is no claim) and drops to the blocks it wrote, each binding a vertical thread from the chip to a knot on the lane's own thread (an SVG under the chips, faint, and one over them for the live and the chosen). Choosing a project, lane or block lights what it binds and opens its focus beside the loom (a sheet from the bottom on a phone): a lane's line, the word that closes it, its accounts, what it rises and drops to, its thread and session; a project's STATE step by step, its decisions, its threads, its lanes and blocks, its page; a block's born and touched, the lanes that wrote it at which addresses, read in words or as it stands. Where the register, the pile, the list or the index cannot be read, the layer says so in grey and draws nothing it would have to guess. A lane's focus carries the same words as its tile and says where a word of yours stands; a project's decisions carry theirs. The time-lapse (step 4) is not built; its place stands marked above the layers and at the page's foot. Wears `theme.css` and the site's bar (`siteDoors`, 'the loom' among the site's own pages).
