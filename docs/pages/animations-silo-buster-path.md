# `animations/silo-buster-path.html`

Silo Buster Path (24 Oct 2026): the film David brought for Silo Buster, James Howard's programme with him, thirteen scenes drawn in code, carried as it came with a head for the site, its still and a home line. Since the same day it takes comments through the kit (`says.js`, timed): it loads `theme.js` and brings its own ◐ (the home line is its `#create-your-own`), its player gives the kit its hooks on `window.__sb` (`scenes`, `current`, `playing`, `play`, `pause`, `onChange`), and the kit's accent is the A state's green. One button under the controls, Comment on this scene, works at any moment; the film never stops by itself to ask (the first cut's Pause for comments toggle and stop at each scene's end were taken out the same day at David's word: "too confusing … no auto stop for comments"). Each comment stands in `silo-buster-path:<handle>` at its scene's place on `spine:silo-buster-path` (the scenes' own words, each with its Hollin & Grey example at 2; law `function:silo-buster-path`, both under the shared project lock); no room of its own. The control row is tightened at 380px and below, where its five buttons had pushed the page sideways.
