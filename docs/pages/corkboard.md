# `corkboard.html`

every proposal on the beach on one board, laid out by what it shares with the others (20 Nov 2026, lane review.1, at David's 'Pr and merge and stick it on the beach!'; built first as a private artifact the same day, then moved here). At `/corkboard`, on the site's list (`site:happyseaurchin.com` 1, and `SITE` in `theme.js`), and among the places a hand may choose for its own tab in the go menu ('corkboard', shape `bare`, off until chosen under 'choose what shows').

**What it reads.** The live `proposals` list gives the cards, one per family, by name, with its line and kind (a family whose line says it is an inquiry, a walk, an explainer, a recipe or an overview, not a proposal, wears that word). The shared board is the block `corkboard`, under the shared project lock: a mirror of `proposals`, each card at its proposal's own number, its underscore the proposal and what its threads wait on, 1 its threads (`#53 waits on happyseaurchin · #93 complete`), 2 its strings, each one line naming the other card with its weight and its kinds (`pocket-beach — w 0.70 · names it 8 times · words: android, chip, native`; each string stands once, at one of its two cards), 3 its own page. The strings are of three kinds, each its own colour: one family's spine naming the other (by `spine:`, `function:`, `pool:`, `files:`, a page address, or its hyphenated name), a thread both families' laws name (or a registry line naming both), and the words their spines share (tf-idf over every spine, the beach's own common words left out). A card's state is read from where the viewer stands: their own handle is 'you'. The registry, `spine:thread`, is read when a card is first opened, for its threads' lines.

**A person's own board.** `corkboard:<handle>`, kept whole through the beach's voice (`beachVoice.keep`, field `corkboard`) under the person's own key, as `arrangement:<handle>` is: 1 is where each card landed (`<name> <x> <y>; …`), 2 where they moved cards since. A device that signs as no one keeps the board on itself (`localStorage` `corkboard:board`, positions only; no key is ever kept here). The page keeps the block after a move, a clearing or a recalculation, never on load.

**The physics lands cards and never runs while you look.** A first board lands whole (d3-force from a fixed spiral, 600 ticks: the strings as links weighted by w, a repulsion, a weak pull to the centre, and cards that never overlap), so everyone's first board is the same. After that a board moves only by its holder: a drag moves a card, or every picked card together (Select, or Shift-click); Clear my moves puts moved cards back where they landed; Recalculate my board lands the whole board afresh with the moved cards held where they were put, then finds each moved card a landing place of its own. A proposal newer than the board lands among the cards already there, every one of them held still; one newer than the shared reading has no strings until the next ('Newer than the reading').

**Refreshing the shared board** is a reading of the proposals list, the thread registry, every family's law (through `threads.js`) and every spine, written whole to `corkboard` under the shared project lock; the scripts that did it on 20 Nov live in weft's lane record (`history:weft` 518 on), not in this repo.

Colours are the theme's: pins take its accents (`--solid` waits on you, `--liquid` waits on someone else, `--vapour-dim` complete), the strings take hues at the theme's own family lightness (`--fam-line-l`), the ground's grain is drawn from `--vapour-rgb` and `--well-rgb`; no colour literal.

**Groups, pictures and fading (20 Nov 2026, evening, at David's 'it doesn't seem to have groups … what I've mapped with paper', 'a toggle that shows the graphic' and 'toggles that I can just remove').** Strings alone never made groups: almost every pair of proposals relates a little, so a board of pairs lands as one clump. The shared board now says each card's group at 5 (`in <group>`): nine groups by what each proposal is about, as weft read all of them together, a first reading David's own map may replace. A landing starts each group at its own place on a ring, pulls each group's cards towards their middle, and lets strings across groups pull lightly; each group is drawn as a soft region in its own hue at the theme's family lightness (`--fam-s`, `--fam-l`, `--fam-ink-l`), named above it, leaving out cards the viewer has moved or faded. Each card keeps only its three strongest strings. The state chips are switches, every one on: a tap fades that state's cards almost to nothing where they stand, and 'show all' brings them back (kept on the device). PICTURES shows each card's page picture above its name (the still at 4, `happyseaurchin.com/animations/<name>.jpg`, 47 of 61), the board drawn taller so the taller cards still clear each other; the pictures load only when it is on. A board kept on a device with no moves follows the newest landing (`v` 3); one with moves is its holder's own. Since 23 Nov 2026 (lane arcade-credits.1) the board also reads every maker's own list through `listed.js`: a proposal its maker listed in `proposed:<handle>` and whose family passes the check lands after the keeper's as the newest, marked '(listed by <handle>)', newer than the reading until the board's next one gives it a card at the shared `corkboard`.

**The finder** (25 Nov 2026, lane tidy.2, the scope at `pool:o-page-unit` 25, part 3; `spine:o-page-unit` 1.111111111118, David's GO with keel's CHANGE taken: 'the finder is the corkboard with a search box and "waits on you" at its top, not a new page in /proposal's place', no filters by kind or phase written into the kit). Under the title, a search box (`#find`): a card stands while every word typed is somewhere in its name, its line, its kind, its group or its threads (each thread's line in `spine:thread`, read once the first word is typed, and whom it waits on); the rest fade as a state chip fades them, the count beside the box ('one found', '11 found', 'none found'), Enter opening the first that stands and Escape clearing it. Beneath it, for a reader the board knows, 'WAITS ON YOU · n': each card whose threads wait on them, one tap to open it, on one line that scrolls sideways so the board keeps its place ('nothing just now' when none does). It searches what the board already reads, never every spine's words, and waiting is read from threads alone, not from whom a decision asks. Checked in the built-in browser served from the branch (68 cards; 'garden' one found, 'door' eleven, Enter opening Game arcade, Escape all 68 again) and headless with a known reader (35 waiting on happyseaurchin, the row one line), no script error.
