# `theme.js` colour tabs

**Removed on 27 Nov 2026** (lane go-menu.1, at David's 'Yes, build it'). The go menu no longer reads any colour when it opens: there is no colour tab and no ◐ colour key, and `readColours` and `paintColour` are gone from theme.js.

- **Why.** David doubted the tabs were ever his ask. What he wanted was a page to jump to: 'I wanted a page, so I could jump to a page and see what *I* am currently privy to'. Weft's record of 23 November names the ask 'a coloured admin dashboard', and weft's summary of his message (`watch:weft` 1138.6) lists 'a colour key on pages and an admin list in the go menu'. His own words from that morning cannot be read from here. Either way the tabs sat at the wrong level: they listed open pages, nothing a colour seals.
- **Where the colours went.** They stand on `/colours` (`colours.md`): every colour on the beach, lit where the device's key opens a copy and grey where it does not. `lists:colour:<name>` 2 now names where each colour's sealed lines are read: `colour:admin` the loom's admin band, `colour:outreach` the Factory, `colour:builders` none.
- **The catalogue.** `colours` joins the catalogue a hand chooses its places from, off until chosen. David's own list names it.
- **Places no menu shows now.** Of the admin tab's twelve places, seven stand in the site's list. The other five are in no menu: the health check, the threads, the families, the pscale lab and weft's lanes.

What stood from 23 to 27 November follows.

A colour's own places in the go menu (23 Nov 2026, lane colour-key.1, the coloured dashboards' step 3, at David's 'move on to step 3'): a colour keeps its list as a hand keeps its own, at `lists:colour:<name>` branch 2 (each rung '<address> — <its name in the menu>', the lighthouse's shape, an address on this site or a full https:// one), and every page's go menu shows it as a tab named for the colour to a reader whose device holds the colour, and to nobody else. When the menu first opens it asks the voice which colours this device holds (`beachVoice.colours`: each `colour:` block on the beach whose copy opens with the key this device keeps, under the name the page knows, else the name the voice last wore) and reads each one's list; a device with no key reads nothing beyond loading the voice. While no colour's list shows, a ◐ at the tabs' end is the colour key: a tap puts the reader's key on through the voice's `wear` (asked once, proved against their passport's published halves, kept wrapped as every key is; a device that knows no name asks for the handle first), and the colours' lists join the tabs. The first was `colour:admin`, David's and weft's, whose places were the loom, weft's lanes, the health check, the beach census, the reflection, semantic flow and the threads. The places are open pages; what is private on them is sealed on them, as the loom's admin band is. Members' doors keep their own menu and carry no colour tab.

One colour tab, admin (25 Nov 2026, lane go-menu.1, at David's 'Merge into one tab', reading admin and builders as one and the same, then 'Yes, remove it' of the outreach tab): two more colours had gained lists, `colour:builders` (David's, keel's and weft's: the proposals, the corkboard, the families, the threads, the eye, the pscale lab; 23 Nov, lane colour-salt.1) and `colour:outreach` (The Factory alone; 24 Nov, lane money.1), so David's go menu carried three colour tabs, in whatever order their lists' reads returned. Now `lists:colour:admin` 2 holds twelve places, its seven and then the builders' five not already there, while `lists:colour:builders` 2 and `lists:colour:outreach` 2 list none, each line naming what stood there and where it went; The Factory stays in David's own list, the catalogue's 'the factory'. The menu draws a colour's tab, named for the colour, only for a list with a place in it, so a place written back as a rung brings that tab back to the colour's holders. The colours themselves are untouched: admin seals the admin family (`function:admin` 7), outreach the Factory's rows, builders nothing on this beach. The change was made on the beach alone; the record is `watch:weft` 1171.
