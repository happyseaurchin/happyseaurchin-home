# `whole-system.html`

whole systems, as proposals are drawn (17 Nov 2026, evening, lane tranche.2, thread 49; at David's word: "like proposals … a standard beach version, and then a snazzy look version … a block of whole-systems would be useful"). A whole system is how a person, or an AI, understands things to fit together, in their own shape and words, written for an AI to read: a meld reads two side by side (keel's correction, pool:weft 623). THE LIST is its own block on the beach, `whole-systems`, founded that evening under the shared project lock and kept like `proposals`: a line each, '<handle> — what it is · <its snazzy page>', the page only where its holder has made one, appended when a whole system is shared (1 happyseaurchin, Fulcrum; 2 keel, the first AI's; 3 emojisword, SVOS, under its older name; `solution-set:iino` stands on the beach and is not listed). `/whole-system` draws the list (`/whole-systems` redirects there): a card each with two buttons, two addresses, each always itself (the proposals' rule): **Snazzy**, the page its line names, and **Standard**, with a link to meld with that person, and beneath them the line a person's AI takes to write one (function:systemic-kernel 4.1). THE STANDARD VIEW is `/whole-system/<handle>` (a rewrite in `vercel.json`; `?h=<handle>` works too): `whole-system:<handle>` read live, else the older `solution-set:<handle>` (the list's line says which, so the block that stands is read first), drawn as a document: its opening as title and lede, its first sentences shown and the rest one tap down; the part saying whose system and whose hand lifted under the title, once; the parts as a contents list and as sections; beneath each part, a short line standing as it is and a longer one, or one with parts of its own, folded under its heading. Headings come from the text itself (a capitals run before ' — ', else the first ' — ', ': ' or '. ' within its opening), and a heading in capitals is set as the block writes each word mid-sentence (proposal.html's learner, with headings themselves not learnt from). The addresses sit behind a switch in the contents box (kept on the device, `whole-system:addrs`); `#a-<address>` opens one. Its buttons: Snazzy where the list names one, meld with that person (`/mind-meld?with=<handle>`, the plain address for David), the book for David (crumful.com/spine), every whole system. `/systemic-fulcrum` redirects to `/whole-system/happyseaurchin` until David's snazzy Fulcrum, being made with keel, takes that address: then the redirect goes and its line in the list names the page. Every whole-system block on the beach, listed or not, is still gathered at `/view/whole-systems` (`view:whole-systems`). Reads only, no write box. Wears `theme.css` and the site's bar (`siteDoors`; 'whole systems' among the site's own pages in the go menu). Checked at 390 dark and 1280 light against the live beach: the list's three cards, Fulcrum, keel's, SVOS and iino's drawn, a missing handle told so, no script error, no sideways scroll, nothing written.
