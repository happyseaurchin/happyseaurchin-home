# `games/census.js`

THE COUNT of the flock world (27 Nov 2026, lane coil.1, thread 155 of `spine:thread`, at David's 'go ahead with the rest of the build'; `spine:flock-world` 1.1111 BEYOND YOUR ROOM and 1.11113 THE COUNT, keel's CHANGE at `flock-world:keel` 1.11118, a block per region from the start, and David's GO at `flock-world:happyseaurchin` 1.11118, 'Yes. Scalability through federated beaches.'; `world:flock-world` 4). You connect only to the birds in your room, and the rest of the world reaches you as counts. No server tallies the world: each room's host keeps one line, and every reader sums what stands. A pure module with no page of its own, loaded as a plain script beside `world.js` (`window.FlockCensus`): by Forage and Migration inside their sealed frames (the header's `script-src 'self'` lets it in, and it reaches the beach and nothing else), by the map and by the shelf.

THE BLOCKS: one per region of the world, a region being a patch at the world's rung, named by its address: `census:flock-world:1` to `:9` at rung 1, 5 the middle (Ings Downs), each founded by weft on 27 Nov with its law at the underscore and left open, as a room's liquid is, so any room's host writes it with no key. Each region's counts can then be kept at the beach that carries that region; today all nine stand at `beach.happyseaurchin.com`.

A LINE stands in one slot of its region's block, in the room plug's slot shape (`button/room.js` at the beach): its words at the underscore ('31 flying, heading north-east · 3 home · Sedge, Tern'), then 1 the room (`pool:migration`, `pool:forage:2`), 2 the day, 3 when it was written by the beach's clock, 4 how many fly, 5 how many are home (Migration's roost; Forage has none), 6 the heading in degrees from north, 7 where, `x,y` in km on the torus, 8 the players' names, only where a room holds five or fewer. A blank line ('' at the underscore) is a room ended; a line silent for ninety seconds is a room gone.

THE KEEPER (`FlockCensus.keeper()`), one per host: `put(o)` is called every frame and writes every half minute, or at once when the flock crosses into another region. It then blanks the old line and claims a slot in the new block: its room's own line first (so a host handing over hands its line on), else a blank or silent slot, the longest silent first, else it appends. A slot held through a long silence is claimed again rather than trusted. `leave()` blanks the line. Migration's host keeps one while its sky is live (the flying birds' middle, carried from the sky's frame to the world's by `skyToWorld`, or the roost once all are down; the crossing's heading), blanked when the host leaves the room. Forage's host keeps one while a flight together flies (the leading flock's distance along its crossing; how many birds are in or out of the flock; every player named where few), blanked when the flight ends or the room is left.

THE READERS: `FlockCensus.read(W)` reads every region of the world `W` at once and gives each live room's newest line, newest first; `sum(lines, game)` gives the rooms, the birds flying and the birds home. The map draws each flock where its host last said (`flock-world-map.md`). The shelf's card for each game sums its rooms (`games.md`). Migration's title and Forage's title and lobby say how many fly now, in the game's own rooms and in other games, re-read every half minute while they show.

SCALE, as keel weighed it: a line a room every half minute is one write a room, about 33 a second at a thousand rooms, shared across the regions' blocks rather than heaped on one. A reader reads every region at each look: nine blocks at rung 1, eighty-one at rung 2, where a reader would read only the regions near it (the next build, with the world's growth, thread:155).
