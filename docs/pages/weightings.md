THE WORLD is a river delta at dusk, where Shore's river meets the sea, in Shore's paper, tokens and type (Bricolage Grotesque, Atkinson Hyperlegible, IBM Plex Mono); the party colours are validated as a set by the dataviz skill's checker in each theme (creator aqua, distributors blue, the cup gold, the beach's server magenta, the commons a neutral), and every value beside them is written in ink. THE BAND is a canvas: a thousand gold glints come down the river from a ticket stall and split at a fork into four channels as wide as the law's weights, to mara's workshop, to five distributors' huts and the players who shared, to the cup's lagoon (which fills) and to the beach's lighthouse; what nobody can be named for runs out to the sea, the commons. Its counters fill from the run's own trace, the ledger's latest line stands in the corner, and a strip of the five counters beneath keeps them readable on a phone; under reduced motion, or at `?still`, it stands complete, which is the share image `animations/weightings.jpg` (1200 by 630, rendered headless with the page's own fonts). SECTIONS: the game's law (its support line as the reader reads it, one £5 ticket split, and who is who); after a thousand tickets (five tiles by column, the split of £5,000, the distributors one by one, every holder in pence); six experiments, each the same month with one thing changed and its finding written from the numbers: who brought the players (a slider), when it is read (as each ticket lands or once at the end, a dumbbell for each distributor, eli the late one), the card fee (Stripe's standard UK card, 1.5% + 20p: none, off the top, or added by the buyer), the players' 5% (a cup, or each player by beats, a skyline of 360 players), credits or the weightings (`spine:shore` 1.1118: whole pennies at each ticket against exact shares rounded once, for players by beats and for the players who shared), and ten times the tickets (100, 1,000, 10,000); your own month (tickets, price, the four weights with presets, cup or beats, how many were brought, the fee, and read as each lands, once at the end or as credits, the last refused past 3,000 tickets in a browser, the figures dimmed); the same code, with `ledger:shore` read live through `/api/xp?ledger=shore`; and where it came from.
