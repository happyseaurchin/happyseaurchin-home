# `verso.html`

verso (5 Nov 2026, lane verso.1), at `/verso`: the middle point's app, the page of the three pieces in the build record (bsp-mcp-server `proposals/2026-11-05-verso-the-build.md` §2.3; the family is `spine:middle-point`): a phone page over a host on the Mac (`verso/cells/` in bsp-mcp-server, `ws://localhost:8765`) whose cells listen at several speeds and speak only at the middle point. One WebSocket, JSON events (§3): from the host `words`, `floor`, `mirror`, `ledger`, `speak`, `stop`, `period`, `cost`, `done` and `host` (its answer to the page's hello); from the page `host`, `start` (`feed: live`, or `replay` with a `run`), `stop`, `settings` (`key`, `value`, `all`), `mark`, and the voice's own `spoken` and `stopped` (never `stop`, which only ever stops the run); the shapes are in the seam note the page's session left beside the record. ONE BUTTON, large and centred: enliven, press again to stop; rings round it pulse at each layer's own period (from `period` and the cells' lines), so a held silence breathes and a switched-off machine is still. A live run says 'listening.' once as it starts, so the room hears that it listens and a phone's voice is woken by that tap. THE SETTINGS of §4 under it as sliders and toggles, each sent as it changes and kept on the device (`verso:settings`), `safety` shown always on; window, line limit, attention and attachment start provisional (§4 tunes them on the replay), and a running host's own values are taken up. THE SCORE, a canvas after keel's band on `/middle-point`, moving past a fixed now: the people on the top staves, verso's own staff (a rest each bar it held through, a note each phrase it said, // where it was stopped, ◆ where it would have spoken on a replay), the held thoughts on ledger lines one a holder (a fermata while held, bright when ripe, a tick each release, a caesura when foreclosed, rising when voiced, a dotted arrow to the person it was handed back to), the floor and the urge each against its threshold dashed and pale where both pass, the cells keeping time, the words as a strip, three lamps at now. Prose only under 'show the words' (options). THE VOICE OUT is the group page's narrator carried over (this device's voice, or OpenAI or ElevenLabs on the person's own key, kept on the device; a narrator chosen on /group is taken up until one is chosen here), a phrase at a time, stopping at once on the host's stop (the device voice cancelled, the fetch aborted, the audio paused), or at the phrase's or the sentence's end where the stop names it. THE JUDGES' MARKS on a replay: a tap on the score marks a moment (spoke well, should have spoken, silence felt), a tap on a ◆ says whether it was a real moment. The host's address is asked once (`ws://localhost:8765` offered), kept as `verso:host`, changed under options ('the voice and the host'), set by `?host=`; a lost host is tried again on its own. An https page may open `ws://` only to its own machine, so from a phone the host needs `wss://` (a tunnel) or to serve the page itself, and the page says so; served by the host (`host.py --page`, the page at `/` on the socket's own port, over plain http), it connects to its own origin without asking. The host's own setting names are taken up as the page's (`cells: "osh9"`, `injector: { arm, block }`), and `line_limit` is lines, as the host reads it. THE DEMO: with no host, 'demo' plays a stand-in host inside the page, about two minutes of three invented people (Mara, Tom and Ines) through the same events (live it speaks once and is cut short; as a replay it marks where it would have spoken and takes the marks); `?demo` plays it at once, `?demo=idle` only switches to it. The screen is kept awake while it listens. Under the host's line, since 9 Nov 2026, one link: how verso works, and what every setting does, its schematic at `/verso-schematic`. Nothing here writes to the beach, and its body says `data-beach-button="off"`. Wears `theme.css` and the site's bar (`siteDoors`, 'verso' among the site's own pages).
