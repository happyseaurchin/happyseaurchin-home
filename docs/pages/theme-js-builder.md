# `theme.js` Builder

the switch for a page's meta layer (25 Nov 2026, lane tidy.2; the scope of `spine:o-page-unit`'s phase two at `pool:o-page-unit` 25, part 1, at David's word that day: 'a button at the bottom "builder" with a tool-tip that explains what it does … a pop-up which explains it and then toggle on/off -- and then the button is black/white … There may be other aspects which builder enables', and 'Builder effectively is creating a mirror to anything -- its a commentary on any other piece of content'). Off, the default, a page keeps to its content and its decisions; on, it shows the layer beneath, what people have said about each part, with a place to add their own. Two things stand only in builder mode, by one rule `theme.js` injects as it loads: a crafted page's 'Readings of this step' boxes (`.step-readings`, on `/o-page-unit`, `/web-llm`, `/host-proof`, `/pocket-beach`, `/first-meeting`, `/salt-app`, `/waterline`, `/coloured-stigmergy` and `/sword-fight`), and anything a page marks `data-meta`. The plain page, `/proposal/<name>`, puts such a place under every step, side-room and room around the proposal (`proposal.md`). The state is this device's, the word `on` or `off` at `localStorage` `site:builder` (a preference, never a key, so not `siteKeys`), the same on every page and followed across tabs; it is set on `<html>` as `data-builder` before the page draws, so nothing flashes. The switch, `.aid-build`, stands in the corner after Share on every family page (the door's `pill()`), and beside Share where a page keeps its door in its own header (`shareAlone(true)`); never on `/arcade` or `/games`, which carry Share alone, nor where the html or body says `data-builder-switch="off"`. Light when off, dark when on (the reverse in the dark theme), its label 'Builder' with a pencil, the label hidden under 520px so the three pills fit a phone; its tool-tip says what it does. Its first press opens a small window (`dialog.bld-dlg`) saying what Builder shows and what this page carries ('On this page: 12 places, one under each part', or, on a proposal's crafted page with none, a link to its plain page), with 'Turn builder on' and 'Keep it off', acted on as the button is pressed (the dialog's own close event can come late in a background tab); `site:builder:told` remembers it was said, and after that the switch turns at a tap, saying where the layer is when turning it on finds none here. `window.siteBuilder` gives `on()`, `set(bool)` and `toggle()`; a `site:builder` event on `window` carries `{ on }` each time it turns. Checked in the built-in browser served from the branch: `/o-page-unit`'s twelve boxes hidden off and shown on, the window's words and buttons, `/proposal/sword-fight` showing David's comments at 1 to 1.111 in builder mode and none off, the three pills side by side at 1024 and 375 with no sideways scroll.
