# `button/`

a forwarding shim only: the beach button (a floating frame any page carries with one script tag, the host page unable to read what is typed into it) moved onto the beach itself on 14 Oct 2026 and is served at `https://beach.happyseaurchin.com/button/button.js`; its source, security case and hostile-page test are in `pscale-commons/pscale-beach` (`button/`, `npm run smoke:button`). `button/button.js` here hands any page that carried the old address to the beach's loader. The o-pages carry the beach's own button through `theme.js` (`beachButton`, 15 Oct 2026), which since 18 Oct loads the beach's voice instead (`https://beach.happyseaurchin.com/button/voice.js`, its source and `npm run smoke:voice` in pscale-beach `button/`): every page with the bar, never in an embed or a framed page, and not on a page whose body says `data-beach-button="off"` because its bottom right is taken (earth, globe, field, social-brain, experience-ecology); while a page's `.sheet-back`/`.pop-back` is open the button's circle, edge and window step under it. **The button is off until a person turns it on** (David, 2026-10-18), with 'show beach button' / 'hide beach button' under options (or 'Or use the beach button' beside a chart's reading box). The choice is the person's, kept with their settings on the beach: `settings:<handle>` at 9.1 (the per-person layer of the beach's `settings` block; 9 is pages), wherever the device knows their name (`render:who`), so it follows them to every device; a device that knows no name keeps it in localStorage `beach-button` ('on', else off). The light or the dark is kept beside it the same way, at 9.2 (`'light'` or `'dark'`): ◐ writes it through the voice (`beachVoice.set('9.2', …)`), and every page with the bar takes it up from the person's settings once it has painted in the device's own (`view:theme`, still set before first paint). The arcade's look is kept at 9.3 (`'pier'`, `'neon'` or `'shelf'`, arcade.5, 27 Oct 2026): chosen under /arcade's options, written through the voice (`beachVoice.set('9.3', …)`, one write once the choosing settles), taken up by `beachVoice.setting('9.3')`, and in localStorage `arcade:skin` on every device, read before first paint. The reader's look, kept at 9.4 (`'maker'` or `'standard'`) from look.2 on 27 Oct 2026, was taken out on 28 Oct 2026 (two-buttons): nothing reads 9.4 or localStorage `view:look` now, and the rule that replaced it is in the `proposal.html` row. Never in `lists:<handle>`: that block's nine branches are the person's lists (1 projects, 2 the go listing, 3 the open row, 4 follows, 5 scopes), and a setting is not a list. **A write box takes words through the voice**: `beachVoice.say({ field, at, text, name })` (and `keep`, `ask`, `drop`) goes through the button where the person has it on the page, else the page's own box: the name it passes, and the key the browser keeps or asks for once. The charts are the first pages on it; the older o-pages' write paths (/now, /walk, …) still hold their own keys, kept wrapped since 5 Nov 2026 (theme.js `siteKeys`), and move onto it in time. Since 16 Oct 2026 the button is tapped as xstream's construction button is (+ opens the prompt, # settings, × closes; one window holding the reply, staged writes with commit, and the prompt), moved by the grip beside its circle, on the page itself.
