# `flock-world.html`

the Flock World proposal (24 Nov 2026, lane anteroom.1), at `/flock-world`, its snazzy view (`/proposal/flock-world` is its standard view, drawn from its blocks, `spine:flock-world` and `function:flock-world`): Forage and Migration grown into one flocking game thousands can fly at once, on a world that wraps and grows with its players, built so its hawks, fields and farmers can be added by anyone. Made by weft at David's word in chat, 'Yes, create a snazzy proposal version please, and the proposal has the shared variations', as `crafted-proposal-page` says.

THE WORLD it wears: a murmuration at dusk over a patchwork of fields that wraps at its edges, the world as a torus seen from above. Bricolage Grotesque, Atkinson Hyperlegible and JetBrains Mono; a field-mist paper and a night-field dark, the band and the instruments in the same dusk in either theme. THE BAND (`#world`) draws today's crossing, two points a third of the world apart drawn from the date, the shortest way round the torus; lights as many birds as there are players in the live rooms now (`liquid:pool:migration`, `:forage`, `:fotronic`, `:ghost-racer`, a slot with a mailbox heard within 25 seconds, the room plug's own count); a hawk on the flock's straggler and a farmer's shot on the crossing; and, when most of the flock is within reach of the roost (measured round the torus), the world growing by a rung, nine patches becoming eighty-one. Every patch's fields are drawn from its own address. Under reduced motion it stands still mid-crossing, and that still is `animations/flock-world.jpg` (1200 by 630).

THE STEPS, seven, each its words (read live from the spine, the page's own copy standing when the beach cannot be read: `data-sp`, `data-dec`), its detail one tap down, its decision card and its instrument: the game (MERGE, ONE WORLD, APART drawn), one sky (two phones, each phone's own chance or the day's), rooms as cells (players filling rooms of twelve on the map's three shapes, EARTH, TORUS, ROUTE, a torus growing by rungs), the wire (an introduction through the beach's blocks or the relay, timed), beyond your room (counts and who pays at ten, a thousand and a hundred thousand), the game that grows (six hawks and the farmer, each hunting its own way), built from extensions (CORE with sockets and plugs, unpluggable; PATCHWORK bricks, one taken out). THE VARIATIONS section draws the options of the three decisions with words of their own. No instrument reads or writes the beach.

ANSWERS go through the beach's voice (`beachVoice.say` into `flock-world:<handle>` at the decision's address), every box keeping its draft and taking dictation, the reader's own answer put back in its box once the beach is read ('Change your answer'); everyone's answers read through `/api/mirrors?family=flock-world`, the room's last lines from `pool:flock-world`, the threads (`data-threads`) above the room, and two lines to copy into one's own Claude, to answer and to fold. Addresses stand behind a switch, off by default.

BUILT from a template and the family by a small script (the words copied from the beach at build time, so the static copy is the spine's), with a preview of the same page carrying a snapshot of the spine and the room for a claude.ai artifact. Checked headless at 1280 light and 390 dark, motion on and off: no script error, no sideways scroll; two faults found and mended before it shipped (the band drew a flock it had just reset, and the hunters' buttons ran off a phone's edge), and the still made true (the flock's arrival measured round the torus, the farmer's shot put on the crossing).
