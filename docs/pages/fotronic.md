# `fotronic.html` + `fotronic/`

fotronic (26 Oct 2026), at `/fotronic`: Fortress as a minimal team snake, built as the spec at `spine:tronic-glass` 1.1 stands (its side-rooms 1.12 to 1.17; David's GO at 1.18). `fotronic/sim.js` is the simulation alone, an ES module the page imports: it takes (law, seats, events) and steps at a fixed 120 ticks a second, deterministic, the bots' only randomness one seeded LCG; the law is one settings object named as Armagetron names them (1.151), the map's numbers at its end, so a cup can hand it a law by address; inputs are events `{tick, seat, action}` with L, R, BRAKE_ON and BRAKE_OFF through `apply()`, and `botEvents()` returns what the bots would send so they go through the same transport; `resultLine()` is the line of 1.172. `fotronic/checks.mjs` runs the DONE WHEN checks of 1.162 headless (`node fotronic/checks.mjs`, a name's part runs one), plus determinism; all must pass before a push. The page wears the grid's own night (Orbitron, the Ladle's blue #4488ff and gold #ffff44), not `theme.css`, and loads no `theme.js`: no bar, no write box, no beach button, so none of their duties apply; its only text box is the handle. It is opened by the beach as `?match=&team=&seat=&handle=` (1.172) and plays locally without; the start screen picks the team size and the seat a person takes on each team, bots fill the rest; one human uses the arrows (↓ brakes), two on one keyboard blue A D S and gold the arrows, a phone taps either half of the arena and holds BRAKE; `transport.send`/`onEvent` is a local loop (1.17). PLAY ONLINE plays it with whoever is in the room (23 Nov 2026, lane anteroom.1, David: 'Build the quick path first'): the page carries the beach's room plug (`button/room.js`, `data-game="fotronic"`), and the start card's `<beach-here>` shows who is here and starts the match for all, its caller handing every page one seating (everyone here sorted by id, blue, gold, blue… by turn, bots in the empty seats; of two starts called at once, the lower id's wins on every page). The match runs in lockstep: every phone runs the same world from the same match id, the bots computed alike on each so they cross no wire; a person's press is stamped fifteen ticks ahead (an eighth of a second) and sent to the others with the tick it has spoken for; a phone steps a tick only once every other player has spoken for it ('waiting for …' on the screen while it waits); a press waits in the page's own queue until its tick, never in the world's inbox, which a new round clears; and every two seconds each phone says a hash of its world, a world that differs saying so on the screen. A room by link, `?with=<word>`, is a match among friends, apart from the open room. Proven with two headless pages over loopback steering and braking for twenty seconds, every hash agreeing, and, the other way, a world nudged a metre flagged on both. A pair joined only through the wire holds the match at the wire's pace, and a player who leaves mid-match is no longer waited for, their cycle riding on with no hand on it.
