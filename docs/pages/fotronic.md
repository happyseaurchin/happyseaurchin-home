# `fotronic.html` + `fotronic/`

fotronic (26 Oct 2026), at `/fotronic`: Fortress as a minimal team snake, built as the spec at `spine:tronic-glass` 1.1 stands (its side-rooms 1.12 to 1.17; David's GO at 1.18). `fotronic/sim.js` is the simulation alone, an ES module the page imports: it takes (law, seats, events) and steps at a fixed 120 ticks a second, deterministic, the bots' only randomness one seeded LCG; the law is one settings object named as Armagetron names them (1.151), the map's numbers at its end, so a cup can hand it a law by address; inputs are events `{tick, seat, action}` with L, R, BRAKE_ON and BRAKE_OFF through `apply()`, and `botEvents()` returns what the bots would send so they go through the same transport; `resultLine()` is the line of 1.172. `fotronic/checks.mjs` runs the DONE WHEN checks of 1.162 headless (`node fotronic/checks.mjs`, a name's part runs one), plus determinism; all must pass before a push. The page wears the grid's own night (Orbitron, the Ladle's blue #4488ff and gold #ffff44), not `theme.css`, and loads no `theme.js`: no bar, no write box, no beach button, so none of their duties apply; its only text box is the handle. It is opened by the beach as `?match=&team=&seat=&handle=` (1.172) and plays locally without; the start screen picks the team size and the seat a person takes on each team, bots fill the rest; one human uses the arrows (↓ brakes), two on one keyboard blue A D S and gold the arrows, a phone taps either half of the arena and holds BRAKE; `transport.send`/`onEvent` is a local loop (1.17). NOT YET (1.163): online play, accounts, chat, other maps, more teams, respawning, the win zone, spectators.
