# `fotronic-3d.html`

Fotronic 3D (24 Nov 2026, lane fotronic-3d.1), at `/fotronic-3d`: the Fortress match of `/fotronic` (`fotronic.md`) seen from inside the light cycle. David asked keel that day for 'a 3d version of /fotronic … first and foremost … the camera is from the target's perspective on the grid facing forwards'; keel (Claude Opus 5.5, max effort, in a claude.ai Project Keel chat) built it as an artifact, `CXK4fEjErrrBN5YpcBbv1t` version 1795532603-f9bb (143,687 bytes, sha256 `a6ee37b6…`), and left weft its terms at `pool:weft` 858, to host at David's word, which came with the session that hosted it. keel's own record is `claude/fotronic-3d-2026-11-24.md` in Project Keel.

PLAY IS THE FLAT PAGE'S, so a rider here and a rider on `/fotronic` play one match. The law, the seats, the bots and the result line are `fotronic/sim.js`, which the page's first module script imports (`import * as s from './fotronic/sim.js'; window.FotronicSim = s;`, keel's first term: the artifact carried the module inline, byte for byte, sha256 `bb8f543f…`, and the page reads it as `window.FotronicSim`). The transport, the keys (one rider the arrows, ↓ the brake; two at one keyboard blue A D S and gold the arrows, the screen split in two), the taps (either half of the screen turns, BRAKE held), the start card's team size and seats and `?match=&team=&seat=&handle=` are the flat page's, and the room plug keeps `data-game="fotronic"` (keel's second term), so both pages meet in `pool:fotronic`. Only the drawing is new.

THE VIEW, in WebGL 2: a sky with a few stars, a floor with the grid and both zones (each ring filling in the attackers' colour as the count runs), walls of light three metres high (the rim ten) with their glow spilled on the floor and a reflection under it, the cycles as two wheels of light round a dark hull, each zone's beacon seen across the arena, and a crash's sparks and its shock on the floor; drawn with four samples into half floats where the device has them, then bloom, and at fewer pixels while it runs below about forty frames a second. The camera rides inside the cycle (1.05 m behind the head, 1.15 m up, rolling a little into a turn, the field of view widening with speed), or outside it where the player puts it on THE CAMERA card (behind, beside, above, turning with the cycle or fixed to the grid, four presets); between rounds it rises to see the whole arena and comes back down for the start, and a crash holds it for 1.8 s before it rides with someone alive (C, or a tap, the next rider). Over the view: a screen edge lit in the colour of a wall beside you, brighter as you ride closer (as the law's side rays see it), the edges reddening as rubber builds, the distance to the wall ahead (red under 0.45 s of travel), names over the people and a mark over your team, the map in a corner (turning with you, or your end at the bottom), the score bar, and the speed, rubber and brake gauges. Sound in WebAudio: a hum rising with speed, a hiss beside a wall, a burst at a crash. The skin's settings (view, field of view, camera, map, sound, glow) are kept on the device under `fotronic3d` and change nothing of play. Behind the start card a match of bots plays itself, the camera circling one rider. With no WebGL 2 a card says so and points to the flat page. Under reduced motion: no roll, shake, speed kick, motes or flights between rounds, fewer sparks, and the circling slower. It wears the grid's night (Orbitron, IBM Plex Mono, the Ladle's blue and gold), not `theme.css`, and loads no `theme.js`, like the flat page: no bar, no write box, no beach button, so none of their duties apply; its only text box is the handle.

As handed in, the page took a flat player's LEAVE as nothing: it kept waiting for the leaver and so froze the match for everyone still in it (headless, the 3D page stopped at the leaver's tick plus fifteen and the flat page behind it), and it would have played the seat with no hand where the flat page played it as a bot. So the page now does what the flat page does. ✕ MENU (a chip before INSIDE and CAMERA) or Esc in a match asks LEAVE THE MATCH? (KEEP PLAYING or LEAVE; Esc again keeps playing): at home the match pauses while the question stands and the keys steer nothing; online it goes on for the others, and LEAVE sends everything said and then a quit naming the tick fifteen ahead, from which every page plays the seat as a bot with the brake let go and waits for them no longer, landing the player at the start card with the waiting room open. The end card plays again (at home at once; online BACK TO THE ROOM) or goes back (MENU; online LEAVE THE ROOM), Esc there being MENU, and the waiting room has LEAVE THE ROOM. Esc no longer opens THE CAMERA card mid-match, as it did in keel's build: the CAMERA chip does, and Esc still closes it. A start seats nobody heard playing another match in the last two and a half seconds, and a page mid-match given a seat all the same gives it up at once (a quit at tick 0), that match beginning with a bot in the seat.

THE LOCKSTEP is keel's, carrying Coil's three repairs (`games-coil.md`; keel at `pool:weft` 832 and 833), and the flat page now carries them too (keel's third term), so both speak the same words. What arrives for a match before this page's start fires is kept for it. Every press, this page's or another's, joins the queue in order of tick, then seat. And no word that comes late, or never, parts the worlds, which keel built as each page's words numbered and taken in the order sent. That third repair is carried another way here, on both pages, because held in order it stalls on the beach's relay for good: the relay (`button/room.js`, BeachWire) keeps at most sixty unanswered words to a peer and drops the oldest, so one word never comes, and every later word waits behind it, the leaver's quit among them. Seen live in this lane: the 3D page in the built-in browser and a flat page joined only through the beach, the 3D page holding 699 words behind a gap at words 5 to 16, the match stopped at tick 15. So every word now carries its page's presses of the last quarter second (`win`, `KEEP` = 30 ticks; half a second, 60, until lane fotronic-slot.1, below), each numbered (`n`), and a press is taken once by its number, so a word lost or overtaken costs nothing. No page can be more than `DELAY` ticks behind another, so any word that lets a page ride past a press's tick was sent while that press still rode in every word; a window that begins past the last number taken, or a press for a tick already ridden, says the worlds have parted. Every word still carries `ev`, each new press once, so a page from before the window still plays, taking presses as they come. The leaver's quit carries the window too. The early words are kept by match for ten seconds, where keel kept one list of 400: a page in the waiting room fills that within a quarter of a minute from a match running elsewhere in the room, and its own next match's early words were then lost. Coil's ride together carries the same window since lane coil.2, the same evening (`games-coil.md`).

THE SLOT (24 Nov 2026, evening, lane fotronic-slot.1, a side session of coil.2's, on its finding): a match relayed through the beach wrote more than the beach takes in one write, on this page and the flat page alike, and the cap that refuses it froze the match and then split it. A write with no latch behind it may be 64 KB (65,536 bytes) at most (`cap_write_bytes`, the beach's `settings` 8.3), and a refused write leaves the slot as it was.
