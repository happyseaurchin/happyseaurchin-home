# `presentation.html`

David's talk for makers at the code-sharing evening (10 Nov 2026, from 7.30, twenty minutes), at `/presentation`, with a stage view for the projector at `/presentation?stage`. weft's page (lane presentation.1), made at David's word ('Go') from his voice note of that morning ('Note Coders') and the order he gave in the chat: one core, and an envelope for each room. The envelope is at 1: tonight genius or scum bucket, its opening at 1.1, its closing at 1.2, where it returns in between at 1.3, and another room's envelope, the AI wave, at 1.7. The core is at 2 (pscale blocks 2.1, what we make with them 2.2) and 3 (how we design 3.1, the systemic kernel 3.2). The deep dive, chosen for the room, is at 4 (tonight temporal activation and verso, 4.1). In the room it runs 1.1, 2.1, 2.2, 3.1, 3.2, 4.1, 1.2. In a look of its own, not `theme.css`: a live gig at night (Bebas Neue, Permanent Marker, Work Sans). The band is the stage: a lamp per part on the truss and the talk's setlist taped to the floor, the spotlight walking it in order, and the crowd below raising a light for every answer and line said in the family, read live. An instrument stands beside each part, each the page's own drawing, and none writes to the beach. Carry it: a working life with a scum bucket and a genius bucket on a yoke; the scum bucket is put down at 55, its arm of the yoke going with it, and from 60 the genius bucket leaks drop by drop until only its concentrate is left, written over it as wisdom (David, 10 Nov). Walk this talk: the talk's own spine, read live, drawn as a tree walked digit by digit, every rung above coming along, with the shift's four turns beside it. The moment of creation: two years of source models, what is built on each replaced as the next arrives, and the four live faces at now. From C to A: bricks to a murmuration, beside the five turns from C to B set out in two columns. The in-between, 3a and 3b stand together on the stage's night (`.metaband`, the meta level). Two kernels in register. A spindle in time: `now:happyseaurchin`, read live from the millennium to the newest beat, an instance handed every rung above where it arrives. And where the room placed itself: the 1.18 answers in buckets, and a lamp over each part for every line left on it. Each part plays its films from `/animations` over the page, and its pages too (xstream's address of meaning, the pscale ecology, the globe at 3, AI Inoculation, the threads and run-up proposals), each with a still in `animations/presentation-stills/` or its own. QR codes, drawn from `/vendor/qrcode.js`, go to the page, one beside every place to say something (each to the page at its part), to the evening's State Stack room `/stack/beach-flyby?join` (the meeting `beach-flyby`, founded by David on 9 Nov) and to `/mind-meld`. Any of them fills the screen at a tap (`#qrBig`); the two that lead away from the talk wait behind a tap of their own, so nobody scans the wrong one. The stage view shows one part at a time. On the left stand its number, title and short line above the page's QR; at 3a and 3b the stack's and the mind meld's QR wait beside it behind a tap, and once shown stand alone. On the right its picture, framed: the setlist and then Carry it for 1a, tonight's State Stack room screen at 3a (`/stack/beach-flyby?join&screen`, the room the audience's code finds: a link with no beat and no `?join` opens the start page instead), the mind-meld still at 3b, and the part's films elsewhere. Along the foot run the setlist strip and a twenty-minute clock. Keys: → / space / PageDown move on, ← goes back, `.` shows the part's next picture, Q shows a hidden QR and then fills the screen with the QR in view, B blacks out, F goes full screen, 1–7 jump to a part; `?live=<url>` frames a live screen (the live stage's `mindflow/live`), and L toggles it. The words are read live from `spine:presentation` (law `function:presentation`, both under David's key; the room `pool:presentation` is open). The one question takes GENIUS, BETWEEN or SCUM at 1.18 (a second, 1.28, was withdrawn on 10 Nov at David's word before anyone had answered it), and a line at any part's own address is a comment on that part. All go through `beachVoice.say` into `presentation:<handle>` and are read back through `/api/mirrors` every 15 s while the page is seen. Every box carries `keepDraft` and `dictate`, and the page keeps clear of `.bar`. Its share image is `animations/presentation.jpg`, the stage view of 1a with motion off.
