# `says.js`

comments on a page's parts, as a kit any page carries (24 Oct 2026; David: "a minimal system that this kind of comment spine mirror structure can be inserted as a kind of kit"), so a page plugs it in instead of rebuilding the mirror code that Nearly There, Who owns the shell? and the proposals each carry their own copy of. A page loads it after `theme.js` (keepDraft and dictate for its write box) and makes one call; it loads the beach's voice itself where the page has not, and draws in the page's own `--paper`, `--panel`, `--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--rule` and fonts, with one accent the page sets as `--says-accent` (any `--says-*` overrides). **The timed kind**, `beachSays.timed({ family, scenes, player, controls })`, is for a film played scene after scene: one button under the film's controls, Comment on this scene, opens the window for the scene showing at any moment, playing or paused (a comment made at any moment in a scene belongs to that scene); the film waits while it is open and goes on when it closes. It never stops by itself at a scene's end to ask: a first cut did, behind a Pause for comments toggle, and both were taken out the same day at David's word ("too confusing … no auto stop for comments"). The window, a `<dialog>` (a sheet from the bottom on a phone), holds the comment the person has already made on that scene, so commenting again starts from it and the new words take its place, with everyone else's beneath it and the shared reading (the bare fold) above them where one stands. The film's side is a few calls the page gives: `current()`, `playing()`, `play()`, `pause()` and `onChange(fn)`. Scene i says at 1, 1.1, 1.11 … in `<family>:<handle>` through `beachVoice.say`, read back through `/api/mirrors` with the sayer's own mirror fresh, the beach straight where that fails; the family must stand first (`spine:<family>` with its scenes down 1, and `function:<family>`). The vertical kind, a pause after each part of a page (Nearly There's and Who owns the shell?'s own code), is the next to move into it. First on it: Silo Buster Path.
