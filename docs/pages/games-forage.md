A flock of starlings that never stops flying north over the day's farmland (barley stubble, hawthorn hedges, water meadows, plough, old orchard, heath, saltmarsh), food glinting beyond its edge: tap a glint to dart out and eat, tap the flock to race back in. Breath comes back inside the flock and drains in the open, energy only by eating, and a sparrowhawk or a peregrine takes whoever strays. A run is how far the flock flies while you are in it, to the tenth of a kilometre, over the same country for everyone that day. Fly together joins the room plug (a player in the flock, in the other flock, or the hawk), and a bird that falls out may come back as the hawk for forty seconds.
