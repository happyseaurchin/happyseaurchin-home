# `games/forage/`

Forage (23 Nov 2026, lane games.1), at `/games/forage`: happyseaurchin's game, built by keel (Claude Opus 5.5, max effort, in a claude.ai chat) from keel's own brief at `brief:forage` (22 Nov 2026, from David's description of the game), and handed to weft by David in a Claude Code seat with 'upload this game so it is listed in my games, and the arcade please, and it is plugged into the beach correctly'; `brief:forage` 7 names the same hand-off ('weft hosts it on happyseaurchin.com and founds its family on the beach'). A flock of starlings that never stops flying north over the day's farmland (barley stubble, hawthorn hedges, water meadows, plough, old orchard, heath, saltmarsh), food glinting beyond its edge: tap a glint to dart out and eat, tap the flock to race back in. Breath comes back inside the flock and drains in the open, energy only by eating, and a sparrowhawk or a peregrine takes whoever strays. A run is how far the flock flies while you are in it, to the tenth of a kilometre, over the same country for everyone that day. Fly together joins the room plug (a player in the flock, in the other flock, or the hawk), and a bird that falls out may come back as the hawk for forty seconds.

THE FAMILY on the beach, founded by weft as the recipe says (`ways:arcade` 2), under weft's key until David takes it: `spine:forage` on the clock (floor 10, its root ten underscores deep, as `spine:cairn` stands); the law `function:forage` (`board: FLEW, highest first, best per player`; the proof `fg3~<day>~<orders>`, re-run by the page's own engine; a `live:` line for the room); the room `pool:forage`, open; and the page as handed in at `files:forage` 1, in four parts, 182,982 bytes, sha256 `de26e2e0…`, byte for byte the file David gave (a page that size is written under a latch: the beach caps a write with no latch behind it at 65,536 bytes).
