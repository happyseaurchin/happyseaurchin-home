# `games/credits.js`

the credit card a hosted game's frame carries once the game's law names credits (25 Nov 2026, lane credits.games.1, a side session of tidy.2, at David's word that the beach's money stands on one first layer: money comes in only as a ticket at his ticket machine, the ticket turns into credits the moment it is paid, and credits are distributed by experience points, the games' case being contract type one, point-creators). Loaded by a frame page after `frame.js`, `<script src="/games/credits.js" data-game="<game>" defer>`; Migration's is the first (`games-migration.md`).

**The law says whether, never the file.** It reads `function:<game>` and finds two lines by their opening words wherever they stand, as the plug finds `board:`: `makers: <handle>, … — …` (who a go's credits land with, split equally: David at `spine:experience-points` 1.11118, 'Just go for makers split equally') and `credits: <own product>, <fund product> at <ticket machine> — …`. It asks the machine for each product's terms (`GET <machine>/share/<id>` as JSON, which carries a credit product's issuer and `credits`: the own product's `to: buyer`, the fund's `to: pool` with its `free` goes a player and what a `go` moves). With no credits line, no makers, a line naming no machine, or a machine that does not answer for those two products, the card never shows, `theme.js` is never fetched, and the game plays exactly as before: that is what every visitor to the live site gets while the products stand at a test machine alone. A test run names a machine on the same computer with `?machine=http://localhost:<port>`; no other address is ever taken from the page's own address, so a link cannot point a player's purchases or gifts at someone else's machine.

**The book is the only balance.** What anyone holds is read off `credits:<issuer>` on the beach, the book the ticket machine alone writes (pscale-commons/ticketing-agent, `lib/credits.ts`), in its order: what came to them less what went from them. A line is `1` the move, `2` the credits, `3` when, `4` the point, `5` to, `6` from (`grain:<pair_id>` for a ticket's credits, `fund:<product>`, or a handle), `7` what warrants it. The page writes no credit anywhere, and a line a player writes about themselves counts for nothing (the wallet finding at `pool:experience-points` 2: one receipt in your own passport makes credit, one credit spends twice, any grain mints).

**The card**, over the game before a go, in Migration's paper and dark: who is playing (the name this device signs with, `render:who`, else the one the game's voice made, `games:<game>:render:who`, else a proposed `starling-NNNN`, with 'not you?'); then one of three: a free go ('Your first 3 goes are free: other people put credits in for players who have put nothing in yet … free go 1 of 3'), which asks the machine to draw it (`POST /credits/draw`, the fund to the makers, three a player, the machine's cap, not the page's); a paid go ('A go is 1 credit, and you hold 10'), which writes the player's own gift and asks the machine to move it; or neither, with what they hold. Then always the two tickets, credits for their own goes and goes paid forward for others, each a form posted to the machine's own buy page in the player's name (Stripe's checkout, its test card in a test run), whose success page leads back here; then where the credits went (every credit spent on a go lands with the game's makers, each maker's total from the book, and what the fund holds) and a link to the book itself. A go is one sitting: the card goes when the go is paid, and comes back with the next visit.

**A paid go is the player's own gift**, sand-v2's shape (3.1) at their passport's 6.3: `{_, 1: probe id, 2: credits, 3: function:<game>, 5: when}`, written under their own key, then `POST /credits/spend { issuer, holder, probe }`, which the machine moves once, in order with every other move, and only as far as the book says they hold. The key goes through `siteKeys` alone (`theme.js`, fetched at that moment, as `wrap.js` fetches it on first opening; a frame page carries no chrome of its own): one this device keeps for any of the player's blocks (`forName`); for a name with no passport yet, one made here, the passport founded latched to it and the key kept wrapped as `passport-latch:<name>` (`beach-key:passport:<name>`, ciphertext `w1.…`); for a passport this device holds no key for, asked once. A passport keeps nine gifts: the gift takes the first empty slot of nine, else overwrites the oldest one the book has already moved (the book keeps every move for good). After a paid go `render:who` is the name, as any page that writes under a name sets it.

**Checked** (25 Nov 2026) in the built-in browser against the live beach and a test machine on David's Mac (`claude/credits-at-payment`, port 8797, its products `migration-credits-test` and `migration-goes-test` at the issuer `arcade-credits-test`), the purchase leg by the machine's manual driver until a Stripe test key stands: David's seed of ten free goes; a newcomer, `credits-games-tester`, taking free goes 1, 2 and 3 (the card counting down, the fund from 10 to 7, happyseaurchin's credits from 0 to 3); then 'Your free goes are used … You hold none'; ten credits bought, the book's line written the moment it was paid; a paid go (the passport founded latched, the gift at 6.31, the machine's line warranted by `passport:credits-games-tester 6.31`, the card reading 9, happyseaurchin 4); ten goes paid forward (the fund 17); and a second newcomer offered free go 1 of 3 from it. Without `?machine=` the page showed no card and fetched no `theme.js`.
