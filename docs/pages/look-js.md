# `look.js`

a maker's look, worn by any page that draws a family (29 Oct 2026, lane look.1; the law is `function:look`, its 2): `look:<handle>` on the beach, written by its maker alone, its tokens at 1 to 3 (the palette light and dark, the letters, the measure) and its grammar at 4 to 6 for a mind that renders the family in that look (`function:look` 3), never read here. A family's law names it at 6, 'look: <handle>', or tints the standard, 'look: accent #rrggbb'. `window.beachLook.wear({ look, accent, map })` puts the tokens on the root over `theme.css`: a line read in either hand ('ground: #hex' or 'ground #hex', 'text measure 30rem'); ink and muted kept only where they read at AA on their ground; an accent, the look's or the law's, that does not read on its ground is moved toward that ground's ink until it does, its hue kept (leros-phase-two's crimson in dark: #cf597c, 4.7:1); faces from the allowlist, loaded when a look names them (Cormorant Garamond, JetBrains Mono); the measure as `--measure` (the longest line of running text), the corner `--radius`, the scale `--body-size`, the headings' weight `--display-weight`, each used in a page's own CSS with its standard as the fallback; all of it cleared and set again when ◐ switches. `proposal.html` loads it; a page with its own vocabulary passes `map` naming its own properties for each token (`{ ground: ['--paper', '--sheet'], ink: ['--ink'], accent: ['--proc'], … }`) and the same checks run over them. The plain schematic page passed one until 4 Nov 2026; it now says its own names in `theme.css`'s and wears the default map. Reads only.
