# `ghost-race.html` + `ghost-race/`

Ghost Race (27 Oct 2026, lane ghost-race.1), at `/ghost-race`: the arcade's second game, the one David and Matthew chose at `spine:game-arcade` 1.11111178, made by weft from the recipe alone (`ways:arcade`, mounting Matthew's six rules at `function:arcade`), as a maker keeping their page on this site would. Three laps of the day's circuit, racing the day's fastest ghosts: one move, holding (the car turns right while held and drives straight when let go; every corner of a circuit is a right-hander, the convex hull of a few points drawn from the day's address), the clock starting at the first touch, which starts the car without turning it; touching a wall slows the car hard, running into one is a crash that stops it half a second (David's words at 1.11111158). `ghost-race/engine.js` is the circuit and the car in whole numbers and + - * / only (no `Math.sin`, `cos` or `sqrt`: fixed-point headings, an integer square root), an ES module the page imports and node runs; `node ghost-race/checks.mjs` runs its checks (every day of 2026 and 2027 fit to race, the generator and the car pinned so a change takes a new version, a kept run re-running to its step and a changed one refused, never turning at least two and a half times slower than driving) and must pass before a push. A run is kept as `gr1.<day>.<steps>.<turns>`. `ghost-race/race.js` is the page, reaching the beach only through the beach's two tags (`voice.js`, then `game.js` with `data-game="ghost-race"`): `beachGame.today()`, `top()` with its own re-run as verify (a line counts only if its run re-runs to a finish in the time it says), `result()` (a newcomer named and keyed in one tap), `room()`, `note()`, `wave()`. The family stands under weft's key: `spine:ghost-race` on the clock (ten underscores deep), the law `function:ghost-race` (board `FINISHED, lowest first, best per player`, page at 6, the circuit, the car, the ghosts and the skins beneath its 5), and the open room `pool:ghost-race`. It was listed in weft's own list `games:weft` at 7 until 2 Nov 2026, when it left the arcade's door at David's word ("get rid of the ghost race from the games list … because now we have ghost racer"; lane arcade.6), Ghost Racer standing in its place; the line at 7 now says so, and the page, its family and its runs stand as they were. The ghosts are the board's own runs re-run on the phone and drawn step by step (the fastest named, the next four fainter, the phone's own best in gold); `?track=<day>` plays any earlier day, `?ghost=<slot>` races that run, `?from=<name>` says who brought a newcomer. The board (top five, Show all), the wave with Where you stood, Bring someone, challenge links, notes and skins (a car, a boat; cosmetic only, never in a proof) are Matthew's shared extras (`function:arcade` 6).
