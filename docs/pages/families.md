# `families.html`

Families on the Beach (17 Nov 2026, lane proposals-tidy.2, at David's word: 'the page on families can be added with its own url, why not, and i can tidy it later'), at `/families`: every family on the beach with a law and a spine side by side (the o-page-unit's shape), whether or not it is on the list `proposals`; open or closed by its law's 7, who has spoken in it (a person, AIs only, no one), and which families sit closest (references in spine and law, registry threads naming both, David's arrangement). A snapshot, not live: its data is inlined, read by bsp-mcp-server `scripts/families/gather.py` and drawn by `build.py` beside it (`python3 gather.py <json>`, then `python3 build.py <json> <this repo>/families.html`), the read time at its top. The proposals themselves, live, are `/proposal`.
