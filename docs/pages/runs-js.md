# `runs.js`

a family's runs, drawn where a page asks (20 Nov 2026, lane pscale.lab, at David's word: "Show the most recent run, and allow the user to dropdown the others ... clicking on the list of runs can show the history of runs by that LLM", and of the o-page-unit showing what experiments find, "go for it"). A run is a line in a runner's own mirror, `<family>:<handle>`, that opens RUN (its number or not) and a dash; the lines beneath it that are not runs are its findings, each a title in capitals before a dash.

**Where runs stand is the family's law's to say**, and the reader takes either of the two shapes in use. Side by side, run k at the step's address and then k: pscale-lab's lanes, where the runs are variants of one test, at most nine to a runner. Chained, each later run at the 1 beneath the one before: health-check's run step, where each run answers the last, with no limit and the history arriving with the newest in one walk. A run belongs to the deepest place on the spine at or above it, so pscale-lab's runs at 1.11121 show at its lane, 1.1112, and health-check's at 1.11111 and 1.111111 at its step, 1.11111. An answer at a decision is never a run, whatever word it opens with (pscale-lab's decisions take RUN), and the bare fold is never read for runs. Results stand in mirrors and the passage on threads: who asked for a run, who ran it and what waits next are knots that point at the run's address (`function:health-check` 5).

**Order**: newest first, by the date the run's own line gives ('20 November 2026'), then by when its runner's mirror was last touched (`touched` from `/api/mirrors`, else the beach's index), then by its place in its runner's series (document order: digit order side by side, depth when chained). A run with no number in its line is numbered by its place in its runner's series at that step.

**API**: `beachRuns.of(mirrors, { spine, touched })` returns every run in the mirrors, newest first, each `{ who, ds, addr, at, n, head, finds, day, touched }`, `at` the place it belongs to. `beachRuns.draw(el, runs, { label })` draws one place's runs: the newest as a card (its line, its findings, a long finding or one holding a table folded to a line with + and opening to its words, tables drawn as tables, and where it stands); the rest in a dropdown closed until tapped, named by its runners ('Three earlier runs, by keel and weft'), with a chip for everyone and one for each runner and the count of their runs there, a runner's chip showing that runner's whole series at that place newest first; each earlier run a row that opens to its findings. A draw with nothing new keeps an open dropdown open, and the chip chosen stands across a redraw. It reads nothing and writes nothing: the page hands it the mirrors and the spine it has read.

**Drawn** in the tokens every page here keeps (`--foam`, `--vapour-dim`, `--line`, `--line-strong`, `--liquid`, `--card`, `--mono`, `--display`, `--body`), with `--bud` for a run where the page keeps one, so it wears each page's palette; its styles go into the head once, every class beginning `br-`. A grid item inside it may shrink, so a wide table scrolls inside its box instead of pushing the page past a phone's edge.

**Where it is drawn**: the plain view, `/proposal/<name>` (`proposal.html`), in a `.runs-under` slot in each step, one box per place that has runs; any family on the list or naming its phase shows its runs there with no code. pscale-lab's crafted page keeps its own copy of the same rule in its own look (`pscale-lab.html`, `runsAt` and `paintRuns`), and health-check's page its record of a card per runner with the runs pasted into its room; either can move onto this module.
