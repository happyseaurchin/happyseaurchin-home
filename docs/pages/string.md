# `string.html`

the String (27 Oct 2026, lane string.2; the phone app at David's word, 2 Nov 2026), at `/string`: what people are sharing, the sharing arm of the happening proposal (David's GO at `spine:happening` 1.1118: "the string is like an app to rival x/twitter etc for intentional sharing of old social media posts"). A share is one line in the sharer's own mirror `string:<handle>` at the second it was shared ('SHARE <url> — why it caught me', or 'SHARE <url> for <handle> — why'): its address is the beach's moment to the second (the ten digits, then the minute, the breath and the second beneath the beat, where the stream lands a say at now; bsp-mcp's `momentToAddress(…, 3)`, ported), one beneath at the first free digit where the sharer's own line or another hand's share already stands in that second. An answer is one line in the answerer's own mirror at that same address. The family is `spine:string` (the clock template, floor 10) and its law `function:string`, both under the shared project lock; the String's own proposal, folded into happening, stands at `archive:spine:the-string:2026-10-27`. The page is a phone app, one page moving by `pushState`. THE FEED: cards, every mirror read through `/api/mirrors?family=string` (the beach straight where that fails) with the reader's own read fresh, newest first, the clock's rungs read down it as headings (Today, This week, This month, This season, This year: an address's first eight to four digits; a beat's own line where lines stand beneath it is the stream's echo, never a share), thirty cards and more as the end nears. Drawn by key, and each part of a card only when its html differs (`draw`), so the minute's re-read never reloads a picture or a frame. THREE TABS at the thumb, under the bar on a wide screen: Everyone; Following, only the mirrors a reader chooses, at `lists:<handle>` 4 (the list the gallery's lens reads, written through `siteLists` under the key the device keeps for their string, so no second question), in localStorage `string:follows` until they have a name and moved to the beach once they do, with a strip of people and a box to follow by name; Yours, their own string. `/string/<handle>` is someone's string: their shares, and the shares they answered with their line above. `/string/<address>` is one share with its answers open, the link Send gives the phone's share sheet (`navigator.share`, else copied). Both come through `/api/string?page=` (rewrite in `vercel.json`) so that WhatsApp's preview, made without running the page, says the sharer, the thing, their why and the link's picture (`assets/string/card.png` where there is none). A CARD: who (a colour of their own from their name), when, Follow, their line (six lines, then more), the thing, ♥, answers, Pass on, Send. FOR SOMEONE ON THE BEACH (David's note at `grain:343bbac1d99d903c` 1.7, 31 Oct): the box takes a name, offered from the passports on the index, and writes 'SHARE <url> for <handle> — why', public like any other, which waits under 'For you' at the top of every tab and of their string, with a count on Yours, until they answer it (the-string 1.114). Pass on opens the same box on a card already there and shares that card's own address (`https://happyseaurchin.com/string/<address>`), which a reader sees as the one who passed it and the card itself read from its address, one level deep: nothing copied, its answers staying with it, and a name optional, so with none it is passed on to everyone. THE THING starts as its site's name and path and, as it nears the screen, takes what `/api/string?url=` gives: picture, title and words; a post on X its words as a quote and, where it has a picture or film, X's own frame (`platform.twitter.com/embed/Tweet.html`, sandboxed and cross-origin, so X's script never reaches the page's keys, which is why it is not `widgets.js`): it stands unseen behind the words until it posts `twttr.private.resize`, because a browser holds back a frame it thinks nobody can see, is given up after 45 seconds, and is not loaded under data saver. X gives a server no picture; a server read under a borrowed crawler's name or X's unpublished endpoints would, and the String does not do it. ANSWERS: ♥ and words are one line (`♥`, `♥ — words`, `words`), showing at once and read back after; ♥ off leaves the words, or says `WITHDRAWN` where there are none; never taken on the reader's own share, whose line stands at that very address (David, 2 Nov: hearts and answers are per person, and the sharer's line is the share), so its heart and comment say so, with Change; the box starts from their own line (rule 6). Every answer knows the hand before it is written: with the button on it is the button's holder, named by its `beachbutton:hello` or asked with `{ kind: 'who' }`, whatever name the device last signed with, and if the hand stays unknown nothing is said. A reader with no name who taps anything that writes gets the name sheet (`beachVoice.propose`, `enter`), and what they tapped goes on; a key made for them is shown once (`made`). ＋ opens the box as a bottom sheet (the link, and a line of why, with `keepDraft` and `dictate`), as `?add=<url>` does with the link in, and `?addtext=`/`?addtitle=` from the share sheet: `string.webmanifest` lets Android install the page as an app in its share sheet ('Add to home screen' under Yours where Chrome offers it); an iPhone has a Shortcut to `?add=`. After a share a toast offers 'Send its link'. A share goes through `beachVoice.say` (the button where the person has it on the page, else the name this device signs with, the key the browser keeps or asks for once). A reader's own shares come back to them under ⋯ (rule 6): Change opens a box starting from the words that stand and says the new line at the same address; Withdraw says 'WITHDRAWN' there, and the share leaves the String with its answers standing at its address; 'not you?', under Yours and in the box, lets the last reader go. How it works is folded at the foot.
