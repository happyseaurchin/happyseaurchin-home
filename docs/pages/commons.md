# `commons.html`

the beach's commons (25 Nov 2026, lane credits.apps.1), at `/commons`: the first layer of money for an app or a service, as David set it that day in tidy.2 — a ticket turns into credits at the moment of payment, the credits are shared by XP, and what is free is pre-funded by credits, the contract in the ticket's type. This is contract three, the whole ecosystem: money paid into the beach's commons is shared **the moment it is paid** across everyone's four-column XP (`spine:experience-points` 1.1, the columns named stars, hearts, diamonds and waves at David's 1.1128), and the part nobody can be named for goes into a **fund** that pays for the beach's free things, so that someone who has put nothing in can still use them. The one free thing it pays for today is **an answer on the shared key** for a handle with no AI of its own; a State Stack meeting is the next.

**Every credit stands in one place**: the ticket machine's book, `credits:<issuer>`, which the machine alone writes, one line a move in the order it takes them (`pscale-commons/ticketing-agent`, `lib/credits.ts`, lane credits.games.1, which found that a balance read from lines a holder writes about themselves holds nothing). The page and its function write nothing to the beach and hold no key. Two of the machine's credit forms carry this lane: a commons ticket's `to` is `https://happyseaurchin.com/api/commons?split`, so at payment the machine asks this site for the split and writes one line to each person and one to the fund; and the fund is a product with `to: pool` (its `point` the shared key, `go` the credits one answer moves, `free` the answers one handle may draw), drawn one answer at a time by `POST /credits/draw`, each draw paying the makers the shared key's law names.

**What it draws, from one read of `/api/commons`** (`api.md`): the fund (credits standing for free answers, whole on the page, to the hundredth on hover), what was paid in (and in pounds at the law's rate), what the fund has paid for, an answer's price; **whose it is** — each person's stars, hearts, diamonds and waves now, their share of the next pound, and what they have received from the commons so far, the fund's row beneath; **money in** — each commons payment with how it was shared at once, and each top-up straight into the fund; **what the fund has paid for** — each free answer, for whom, to whom; and **the ask box**. A **test credits** badge stands in the bar while the law's book branch says `mode: test`; `mode: live` only at David's word.

**The law** is the plain block `commons`, its lines read by their opening words as a law's `mode:` and `page:` are: under 1, the book (`issuer:`, `point:` the point a commons ticket's lines carry, `fund:` the machine's product whose fund pays for the free things, `rate:`, `mode:`, `top up:`); under 2, the split (`people:`, `beach makers:`); under 3, the free things (`point:` the shared key's family, whose law's `makers:` an answer pays, `price:` and `free answers:` as the machine's fund product sets them, `model:`, `max tokens:`); at 6, `page:`. The machine holds the fund's terms; the law repeats them for the page, and a draw the machine refuses is refused whatever the law says.

**The split, worked out on read**: active beats from the voices in every `pool:*` room (speaker at 1, stamp at 3, through every wrap), a beat a ninth of a ninth of the UTC day, counted once a person however busy; only the handles the law's `people:` names count (agents and names no one stands behind count nothing, 1.15). Stars, a person's own beats; hearts, none yet (no room records who brought whom, 1.1112), so that quarter goes to the fund; diamonds, each beat to the makers of the family it was spent in (a person's own room is theirs, any other family's makers its law's `makers:` line), a beat in two families half each; waves, every beat to the law's `beach makers:` by their own beats. The machine divides the ticket by the shares; what it writes is final for that payment, and later time changes only the next.

**The ask box** is a question to a model, never a say on the beach: the function draws one free answer from the fund at the machine first (so the fund is never overdrawn and the key's makers are paid for every answer made), then asks the model once; the question is written nowhere, while the book's line for the draw names the handle it was drawn for, as the machine keeps every free go. With the shared key off, the box says so and draws nothing. The box keeps its draft (`keepDraft`, `commons:ask`) and carries dictation (`data-dictate`); the handle starts from `render:who`.
