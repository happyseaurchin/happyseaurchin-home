# `commons.html`

the beach's commons (25 Nov 2026, lane credits.apps.1), at `/commons`: the first layer of money for an app or a service, as David set it that day in tidy.2 — a ticket turns into credits at the moment of payment, the credits are shared by XP, and what is free is pre-funded by credits, the contract in the ticket's type. This is contract three, the whole ecosystem: money paid into the beach's commons is everyone's by their four-column XP (`spine:experience-points` 1.1, the columns named stars, hearts, diamonds and waves at David's 1.1128), and the commons pays for the beach's free things, so that someone who has put nothing in can still use them. The one free thing it pays for today is **an answer on the shared key** for a handle with no AI of its own; a State Stack meeting is the next.

**What it draws, all from one read of `/api/commons`** (the function, `api.md`): the pot (credits standing, what was paid in and in pounds at the law's rate, what it has paid for, an answer's price), the top-up link the law names, **whose it is** (each person's stars, hearts, diamonds and waves and their part of the pot, the part nobody can be named for standing as the common part, with a bar of the shares), **money in** (each mint, its ticket checked), **what it has paid for** (each gift out, newest first) and **the ask box**. A gift paid but not yet kept by the commons shows as on its way, and the page asks the function to keep it (`POST {keep: true}`) the first time anyone opens it while the key is on. A **test credits** badge stands in the bar while the law's wallet says `mode: test`; the law says `mode: live` only at David's word.

**The blocks**: the law is the plain block `commons` (no colon, so the wallet's latch binds none of it), its lines read by their opening words as a law's `mode:` and `page:` are — under 1, the wallet (`issuer:`, `rate:`, `mode:`, `topic:`, `top up:`); under 2, the split (`people:`, `beach makers:`); under 3, the free things (`price:`, `model:`, `max tokens:`, `daily:`, `in all:`, `key holder:`); at 6, `page:`. The wallet is `passport:commons`, sand-v2's ledgers: receipts at 6.2 filed by topic, gifts at 6.3, both on shelves of nine (a digit takes no tenth append, and an entry's own fields fill the digits beneath it), each entry appended to take its slot and then written whole there, since an append stamps field 3. Read by credits.games.1's rules at `pool:experience-points` 2: a receipt counts only where the giver's ledger holds the gift; a mint only where the issuer the law names backs it with an unrevoked grain carrying `credits=` and a `[ticket-verified]` audit line, net of every earlier gift citing that grain; every gift the commons offers counts against it at once. bsp-mcp's own `computeBalance` reads the same ledgers but debits a gift only once kept (sand-v2 3.2), so it shows the pot higher until the key's holder keeps their credits; which reading stands is David's (the games lane put it to him).

**The split, worked out on read**: active beats from the voices in every `pool:*` room (speaker at 1, stamp at 3, through every wrap), a beat a ninth of a ninth of the UTC day, counted once a person however busy; only the handles the law's `people:` names count (agents and unknown names count nothing, 1.15). Stars, a person's own beats; hearts, none yet (no room records who brought whom, 1.1112), so that quarter stays common; diamonds, each beat to the makers of the family it was spent in (a person's own room is theirs, any other family's makers its law's `makers:` line), a beat in two families half each; waves, every beat to the law's `beach makers:` by their own beats. A part is a standing share of what is in the pot now; drawing it into one's own wallet is the next layer and David's.

**The ask box** is a question to a model, never a say on the beach: the question goes to the function and is written nowhere; the answer's cost is a gift from the commons to the key's holder, its id carrying a digest of the handle so the day's caps can be counted with nobody named. The box keeps its draft (`keepDraft`, `commons:ask`) and carries dictation (`data-dictate`); the handle field starts from `render:who`. With the shared key off, the box says so and asks nothing.
