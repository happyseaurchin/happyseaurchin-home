# `met.html`

a meeting after the hour, its standard view (9 Nov 2026, lane meeting.link; rebuilt the same day at David's 'Build it'), at `/met/<name>` (a rewrite in `vercel.json`; `?f=<name>` works too). One record, three readers: `/ring/<name>` draws the hour's shape, this page the meeting for the people who were there, and the crafted page `function:<name>` 6 names (`THE PAGE — <url>`) the same record in a world of its own. It reads `spine:<name>` (the meeting as held), the fold `<name>` (the account about everyone there, at the same addresses; each part's evidence one rung beneath, 'LIVE — what it shows: <url>'), the readings `<name>:<handle>` (answers: YES, NOT QUITE, ADD, NOT NOW), and the room the meeting's Where names, else its keeper's parlour, a line there carrying `spine:<name>` at its 2. The drawing is on one side (sticky on a wide screen): the people's strands twist into one cord, a knot per part of the longest phase sized by its minutes, each answer a small knot in the answerer's colour, the steps hung on below, with a words switch. The words are on the other side, at Easy (`?plex=1`), Normal and Detailed (`?plex=-1`). A recording line saying its speakers weren't labelled puts a note by what was said. A name new to the beach gets a key made on the device, shown once. `noindex`. The way any AI makes one is `ways:met` on the beach. `/met` with no name is every meeting held (10 Nov 2026, at David's 'Yes, build it'; `function:meeting` 2.31): one line per meeting in the form's room, `pool:meeting`, 'HELD — <name> — <what it was about>; met: <who>; <day month year>; by <handle>; <its page>', the latest line for a name standing, listed newest first, its page on any site (a GitHub page as well as this one). `/met?who=<handle>` is one person's: the meetings whose line names them or was added by them (named), and those they have answered in their own reading `<name>:<handle>` (answered), both shown. 'Add a meeting' appends a line from the page itself, a page of this site whose spine stands filling the rest; the ring and the State Stack read only COPIED and ARRANGED lines, so a HELD line changes nothing there.
