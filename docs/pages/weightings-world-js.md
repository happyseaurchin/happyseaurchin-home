# `weightings-world.js`

the weightings lab's invented month (25 Nov 2026, lane tidy.2): an invented race game, Reef Run, with its people, shares, races and tickets in December 2026, written as the blocks the beach would hold, so `/weightings` runs them through `weigh.js`. The blocks are the game's law, with its support line (`weights: 5 10 80 5` and `the players: reef-run-cup` by default); its room of finished races, a line a race, each in a beat of its own; the String, each share in its sharer's own mirror at its moment's address and each answer at the same address; and the ledger's IN lines, one a ticket. The draws are seeded (mulberry32), so the same settings always invent the same month. The defaults: 1,000 tickets at £5; 360 players (0.36 a ticket), one ticket each and the rest by a long tail weighted by how long each has had the game, with a session of one to three races after each ticket; 70% of players brought, 15% of those through another player's share (the chain) and the rest by five distributors by reach (ana early, ben all month, cai mid-month, dee from day ten, eli in the last ten days); the maker's six test races in the last days of November, so the makers' and the beach's columns stand before the first ticket (making counts where it lands, `spine:experience-points` 1.14); and a card fee of none, off the top (1.5% + 20p, the ledger holding the price less it), or added by the buyer. `runMonth(opts, how)` invents a month, prepares it and runs its ledger (`how` goes to `runLedger`, and `watch: true` watches the distributors and the players who shared). It gives the parties by column from each ticket's own parts (creator, distributors, players or cup, beach, commons) beside each named distributor's and the sharers' whole pence. It is UMD as `weigh.js` is (`window.weightingsWorld`; required, it requires `./weigh.js`). Every name and number in it is invented.
