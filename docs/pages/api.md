# `api/`

serverless functions (see below; genus-clock.js, egg-one's clock, unscheduled since 2026-10-23, is documented in its own header)

## api/ functions

- **`llm.js`** — POST pass-through to the Anthropic Messages API; caller supplies `x-api-key`; supports streaming.
- **`vault.js`** — BYOK key vault + Claude proxy. Keys stored as httpOnly cookies (`hsu_` prefix), routed by `service`: `set-keys` / `check-keys` / `clear-keys` / `claude`. Origin allowlist (happyseaurchin.com + localhost dev ports). The `claude` proxy answers on the caller's own key and nothing else (25 Nov 2026, lane credits.apps.1; before that it tried a server key first). An answer for someone with no key of their own is the commons' to pay for, metered by what has been put in (`commons.js`). `VAULT_KEY_CLAUDE` stays for `genus-clock.js`, behind `CRON_SECRET`.
- **`commons.js`** (added 25 Nov 2026, lane credits.apps.1, at David's word in tidy.2: tickets become credits at the moment of payment, shared by XP, 'free' pre-funded by credits; contract three, the whole ecosystem) — the beach's commons, its page `/commons` (`commons.md`). `GET` reads it on the spot and keeps it a minute at the CDN: its law (the block `commons`), its wallet (`passport:commons`) by the rules at `pool:experience-points` 2, and everyone's four-column XP from the rooms' voices with each person's part. `POST {handle, question, block?}` keeps what was given to the commons and not yet kept, then pays for one answer on the shared key for a handle with no AI of its own: refused past the law's daily caps or when the pot cannot cover its price; the answer's cost a gift from the commons to the key's holder, the question written nowhere. `POST {keep: true}` only keeps. Env: `COMMONS_SECRET` (the wallet's latch; without it nothing is kept or spent), `COMMONS_AI` (`gateway`, the Vercel AI Gateway on the request's own OIDC token, no key stored anywhere; or `anthropic` on `COMMONS_ANTHROPIC_KEY`; unset, the shared key is off), `COMMONS_BEACH` (the live beach unless a test rig names its own; never taken from a request, so the latch only ever travels there), `COMMONS_FAKE_MODEL=1` (the test rig's stand-in, never in production).
- **`pscale-walk.js`** — GET-only proxy to the `pscale_blocks` Supabase table; returns full rows (MCP `pscale_walk` truncates display at ~150 chars, losing `_` body text). Env: `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`. GET-only **is** the security boundary — the table's RLS is permissive-ALL, so the anon key is also a write credential and must never reach the browser.
- **`news.js`** (added 11 Oct 2026) — the news family's door (law `function:news`, re-founded on the clock that day; page `/news`, one item at `/news/<address>?s=<source>`). GET-only reads: `?rung=now|gathering|day|week|month|season|year[&at=<address>]` builds the wire live from the beach's own stamps (index born/touched, accumulator entries' position 3, `?tables`) and reads the keeper's folds from the bare `news` block; `?item=<address>` returns one item, the folds that named it, and its answers (each reader's `news:<handle>` mirror at the item's moment, floor 10). Edge-cached through `Vercel-CDN-Cache-Control` (10–120s) so a crowd from a shared link reads one copy, never the beach. `?keep=1` is the hourly keeper cron (vercel.json): founds `news` under `NEWS_KEEPER_SECRET`, seals closed days and climbs the ladder, re-folds today when the wire moved (never more than every 20 min); it chooses with `NEWS_ANTHROPIC_KEY` (its own key, for watching cost; absent = no folds, the page shows the wire unranked) on `NEWS_MODEL` (default `claude-opus-5-5`); `CRON_SECRET` guards it when set. Test rig only: `NEWS_HOUSE=probe:<name>` + `NEWS_FAKE_MODEL=1`. The QR code on item sheets is `vendor/qrcode.js` (qrcode-generator 1.4.4, MIT), loaded on first use. Since 27 Oct 2026 (lane happening.2, David's GO at `spine:happening` 1.18 and 1.118, his KEEPER at 1.8): `?where=1[&at=<address>]` is the strip at the top of the day on `/news` (`function:news` 4.3), each person's own now at the day (`now:<handle>`, read through `mirrors.js`'s `mirrorsOf`), people before agents, and while the day runs presence (a hand here for fifteen minutes, as `/dashboard` counts it) and the lines at the beat (`torus-mirror:<hand>`, live for one beat's width), with the day's tables; each part answers apart, so the page greys a part the beach would not give rather than drawing it empty; the rooms busiest first are counted on the page off the day's own wire; cached 20s (120s for a past day). The wire opens a family at the page its law names at 6 (`page: https://…`), else at the page the proposals list names, and notes the two makers' shelves by their entries' stamps at 3: the `animations` shelf (kind `animation`, its still at 2, its maker from the credit at 5, which moved there from 3 so the append's stamp stands) and the `proposals` list (`function:news` 1.5); a maker is named only where the thing's own words name one (the law's provenance at 9, a shelf entry's credit). The keeper chooses by the law at `function:news` 2.6, a line at each digit, its own copy in the code standing in where the beach cannot be read, and each pass logs what it did as `[keeper]` in the function's log (no secret in it). Checked offline by `node scripts/check-news.mjs`.
- **`mirrors.js`** (added 20 Oct 2026) — GET-only `?family=<name>`: every mirror of a family (`<name>:<handle>`, off the index, the latest touched first, at most 600) with the family's bare fold `<name>`, as one JSON, for a premiere's room. Read from each phone, a room of n costs every phone n reads at every look and a crowd the beach n² (300 phones at the Brighton room, FLIRT 2026, would have been some 120,000 reads in ten minutes); here `Vercel-CDN-Cache-Control: max-age=15, stale-while-revalidate=45` lets a crowd share one read, and a warm instance re-reads only the mirrors whose `touched` stamp moved. Writes nothing; every answer is still written by its person straight to the beach.
- **`board.js`** (added 26 Oct 2026; the fold since the same evening, lane arcade.3; the room's lines and the unranked room since 27 Oct, lane arcade.4) — GET-only `?game=<name>`, the arcade door's read of any game whose law stands on the beach (`function:<game>`, else `game:<game>` as Matthew's Arcade keeps one; the law names its page, its room and its board line, `board: FINISHED, lowest first, best per player`; a law with no board line, as Matthew's are, is ordered by its SCORE LAW's board sentence, 'fastest first' lowest and 'most rats' highest, a room line scored by its time or first number). A plugged game's board is THE FOLD: every player's mirror `<game>:<handle>` read once through the same path as `mirrors.js` (`mirrorsOf`), each player's line at the day (or their best beneath it), sorted as the law says, cut at ten; where no mirror holds a line at the day it reads the game's room instead, as Matthew's pages keep a run (a Ghost Wire run's time as steps over sixty, a guest named as the name it later took by 5, each player's best). Every answer carries `recent`, the room's newest three lines, notes and runs alike. A name whose law does not stand but whose room keeps runs is answered `ranked: false`, counts and lines only; neither is a 404. A law with a line 'ghost: none', anywhere in it (Coil's, beneath its board line at `function:coil` 4.1, since 24 Nov 2026, lane coil.2), keeps a game whose page rides no ghost and reads no `?ghost=`: it is answered `ghost: false`, and the door offers no ghost to race; any other law, `true`. `{ game, title, page, room, law, board, ghost, ranked, day, date, source, top: [{ name, line, score, time, slot, at, tries, proof }], runs, players, recent: [{ slot, name, text, when, at, run }], read, asKept }`. It re-runs nothing (`asKept`): the honest board is the plug's (`button/game.js` in pscale-beach), whose verify re-runs each proof on the maker's page. Cached as `mirrors.js` is (`Vercel-CDN-Cache-Control: max-age=15, stale-while-revalidate=45`). One address per game (anything else redirects), nothing stored or written. Checked offline by `node scripts/check-board.mjs` (the beach and the fold mocked).
- **`string.js`** (added 2 Nov 2026) — the String's one small function, GET only, two doors. `?url=<a link standing on the String>` is the link's card: oEmbed where the site publishes one (X through `publish.x.com`, for the post's words and author but no picture; YouTube, Vimeo, Spotify, SoundCloud), else the Open Graph and `twitter:` tags at the head of the page, read under its own name (`happyseaurchin-string/1.0`), at most 400 KB, through at most three redirects. `?page=<address or handle>`, the rewrite from `/string/:slug`, is `string.html` (`includeFiles` in `vercel.json`) with its title and preview tags saying that share or that person, because WhatsApp shows what a page gives without running it. It never becomes a fetcher for any address: a link is read only while a line says `SHARE <that link>` in some `string:<handle>` (through `mirrorsOf`), every host on the way is looked up and refused if it is not on the public internet, and only a few clipped strings come back, never what was fetched. `Vercel-CDN-Cache-Control` keeps a card six hours and serves it for a week while the next is read, and a page five minutes, so a crowd from a shared link costs one read. Nothing is stored. `node scripts/check-string.mjs`.
- **`sitemap.js`** (added 19 Nov 2026, lane eye.17; made simple the same day at David's word, 'we just want one pscale block with links to discrete pages. Not content') — a website as one pscale block of links to its pages, for the eye's third tab (the eye row says how the block is shaped and walked): GET-only `?site=<a site's name, or an address on it>` → the site's top, `{ site, origin, found, state, kind, frame, frameWhy, from, pages, maps, seen, elsewhere, more, read, block }`; and `?site=<site>&map=<the address of one of its maps>` → that one map, as the places beneath its place, `{ site, map, found, state, kind, pages, maps, seen, elsewhere, more, read, block }`. ONE FILE A READ: the top reads robots.txt and then one map, the one robots.txt names, else /sitemap.xml, else /sitemap_index.xml; a map of pages gives its pages (`kind: 'pages'`); an index gives its maps, and a robots.txt naming several gives those (`kind: 'maps'`), none read at the top, each '<its name> — sitemap: <its address>' (`mapNameOf`), read only when gone into (`?map=`, never a chain). A site that keeps no map is read the quick way (`kind: 'links'`): the `<a href>` links on its front page that stay on the site, the site itself or its www twin, fragments and anything not http(s) dropped (`linksOf`), links only, never its words. One request at a time, never two at once at one site (gnu.org stalls a second): robots.txt, the one map, then the front page, whose headers say whether the site may be shown inside another page (`frame`, `frameOf`: X-Frame-Options DENY or SAMEORIGIN, or a Content-Security-Policy whose frame-ancestors leave out happyseaurchin.com, is false, read as a browser reads them, an enforced frame-ancestors governing alone; null where the front page could not be read) and whose body is read only for its links, let go at once otherwise. Kept: the site's own pages (its host or its www twin; the rest counted as elsewhere) or the maps on its own name, at most 500 places, `more` saying where there were more (`siteOf`, `mapOf`, `treeOf`, `placesOf`). Guarded as `string.js` is, with string.js's own `mayRead` (exported for it): a name on the public internet only, every address looked up, over https only (an http:// map asked for as https://, no redirect followed down to http), each through at most three redirects, each looked at; a `?map=` must stand on the site's own name (the site, its www twin, or a name beneath it) and have a map's address (.xml, .xml.gz or .txt, or the word sitemap) (`mapAsked`), else 400 unread; robots.txt at most 200 KB, then one map of at most 1.5 MB once opened, let go once it has named 501 places and at once where it is a web page, not a map, or the front page's 1 MB, all within eight seconds; only the block comes back. A site with neither answers `found: false` with words saying so; one that does not answer, `state: down`; a name that is not a site, 400. Nothing is stored: `Vercel-CDN-Cache-Control` keeps a block an hour and serves it a day while the next is read (a site with nothing to walk a quarter of an hour, one that did not answer a minute). `maxDuration` 15 in vercel.json. Checked offline by `node scripts/check-sitemap.mjs`.
- **`text.js`** (added 20 Nov 2026, lane pscale.lab, at David's word: "link to downloadable readable text for LLM and humans who still want to read docs") — a family on the beach as one plain text, for a person to read or keep and for any AI to fetch: `GET /text/<family>` (a rewrite in `vercel.json`), and `?download` hands it over as a file, `<family>.md`. A document's steps are walked down its 1 (each a heading with its address, its side-rooms 2 to 7 inside it, its point at 8, its state at 9), a frame's regions side by side at the root (its law's 1 says 'mode: frame'), and any other kind (a game, a clock) is said to be read on its page; then any room around the proposal that asks a question of its own at its 8, read whole as a step is, its question a point with everyone's answers (`function:proposal` 3; since 21 Nov 2026, lane determinacy.2), then the root's other side-rooms, its law line by line and its room's latest nine lines, newest first. The shared reading (the bare fold `<family>`) stands wherever one does, tables and all, and every reader's lines stand at the addresses they wrote them (`mirrorsOf`), with what a mirror holds beneath a place the spine leaves open (a lab's runs and their findings) nested there. A title in capitals is written as the family's own words write each word mid-sentence (SALT, AI), a body after a title starts with a capital, a sealed line is named as sealed and never shown as its ciphertext, and a file kept whole in a block (a script, a long leaf) is named with where it stands, never poured out. The header and foot say when it was read by the beach's clock, its page (the law's 6) and its plain view (`/proposal/<family>` for a proposal's kinds, `/walk/<family>` for a walk, a recipe, a frame or a plain document). GET only (a HEAD answered as a GET, for a link checker), one address per family (a name in capitals, or a query it does not take, goes there by 308), a name that is no family 400, no spine 404, a beach that cannot be read 502. Kept a minute at the CDN (`Vercel-CDN-Cache-Control: max-age=60, stale-while-revalidate=300`), so a crowd from one link costs one read; nothing is stored or written. SINCE 23 NOV 2026 (lane share-string.1; the share wall, `spine:o-page-unit` 1.11111111112, David's GO at 1.11111111118) it also serves `/proposal/<name>` (a rewrite in `vercel.json` to `?proposal=<name>`; `proposal.html` is in its bundle, `includeFiles`): the page as it stands, its `<title>`, description and Open Graph tags written for that proposal (its `og:url` only then, since the door and the code card take it as the page's address), so a link to it says what it is before anything runs: its title its crafted page's own `og:title` (the page the law's 6 names, else the root's 2, else the list's line, read once at its path on this site and never on another), else its spine's root title cased as the text writes each word; its line the `proposals` list's, before its band's clause, prefixed with its kind from the law's 7; its picture its crafted page's share still, else its own knots where it has a step down 1, else none, its title and line alone (`twitter:card` summary). `/proposal/<name>/knots` (a rewrite to `?knots=<name>`) is those knots as a picture: the quipu `/proposal` draws (`quipuSVG`), its spine a cord across and each step down 1 (twelve at most) a cord hanging with a dash for each side-room and a knot for every answer in its readers' mirrors, coloured by the word it opens with as the page colours it (GO green, NOT YET gold, CHANGE violet, a family's own words in the four colours after, an answer in no word of its own grey, a sealed one among them), an open ring where a decision waits, a side-room's own decision on a cord beside it; threads and states are left to the page. Laid out to fit, drawn with soft edges on the night water and encoded as a 1200 by 630 PNG with nothing but node (zlib's deflate, its own CRC), so the project still has no dependencies. A family with no step has none (404) and a crawler shows the title and line alone; mirrors that cannot be read draw every ring open. Kept as the page is. Only what `/proposal` draws gets its own (on the list, the keeper's or, since 23 Nov 2026, a maker's own `proposed:<handle>` read through `mirrors.js`, the earliest made first; or a phase of the form's own); anything else, a beach slower than three and a half seconds, or a crafted page slower than two and a half, gets the page with its plain preview. Kept ten minutes at the CDN and served stale for a day while it is read again (thirty seconds after a failed read). Checked offline by `node scripts/check-text.mjs`.
- **`beach-relay.js`** (added 11 Jun 2026) — GET-only CORS relay for beach surfaces the browser can't reach directly; exists because the biome commons (`biome-commons-production.up.railway.app`) serves no CORS headers. Strictly scoped: https only, path must be exactly `/.well-known/pscale-beach` or `/.well-known/biome-beach`, no localhost/IP-literal hosts, redirects refused, 8s timeout. Used by filmstrip-3d for whole-beach rendering.
