# `games/coil/`

Coil (24 Nov 2026, lane coil.1), at `/games/coil`: happyseaurchin's game, built by keel (Claude Opus 5.5, max effort, in a claude.ai Project Keel chat) when David asked for Tronic Glass in three dimensions (`history:keel` 482; its second build, levels for the computer riders and a clean universe, at 484), and handed to weft by David in a Claude Code seat with 'upload this game and add to the arcade, and ensure it is plugged in to multiplayer correctly'; keel had left its terms for weft at `pool:weft` 832 and 833, to host at his word. Snake in a three-torus: a universe closed on all three axes, every cell a rider passes left behind it as a glass block, and four side rays on fotronic's law (`spine:tronic-glass` 1.132), so riding beside a block is faster, two sides faster still, a tube fastest; turn left, right, up or down, brake, never stop. Today's ride is the day's universe for everyone, two minutes, on the board; a free ride is a universe of 16, 24 or 32 with up to five computer riders at three levels; ride together is one universe for everyone in the room, in rounds.

A page that size is more than one write with no latch behind it may carry (65,536 bytes), and the signed connector latches a block's root by writing the whole block back, so the page went up as three open writes each under that cap and was latched at once with a write at spindle 0, the root's underscore, which governs the whole block (`lockKeyForWrite` in pscale-beach); read back whole, it matched the sha256, and a keyless write there is refused ('inherits the lock at its root'). Its thread is `spine:coil-plug` with `function:coil-plug`, under weft's key. Each rider's rides stand in their own mirror `coil:<handle>` at the day's address.
