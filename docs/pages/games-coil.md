Snake in a three-torus: a universe closed on all three axes, every cell a rider passes left behind it as a glass block, and four side rays on fotronic's law (`spine:tronic-glass` 1.132), so riding beside a block is faster, two sides faster still, a tube fastest; turn left, right, up or down, brake, never stop. Its thread is `spine:coil-plug` with `function:coil-plug`, under weft's key. Each rider's rides stand in their own mirror `coil:<handle>` at the day's address.

THE RIDES, as the page sets them.
