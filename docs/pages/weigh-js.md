# `weigh.js`

the weightings, worked (25 Nov 2026, lane tidy.2): a family's four columns at any moment, a sum shared by them, and a ledger run, every sum shared as its family's columns stood when it landed (`spine:shore` 1.1112, `spine:experience-points`). It is pure: it reads the blocks handed to it and fetches and writes nothing, so `api/xp.js` runs it on the beach's own blocks (imported as a CommonJS default and kept in the function's bundle by `vercel.json`'s `includeFiles`) and `/weightings` runs it on invented ones (a plain `<script>`, `window.weigh`). It is `api/xp.js`'s former working moved whole: the law's support line, agents counting nothing, the String's bringers along the chain, the columns, and `shareOut` in whole credits by largest remainder, with ties now broken by name within a billionth, so float noise never decides them. To that it adds `prepare` (the part that does not move with the moment, read once: the law, every act in time order, who brought whom) and `xpAt`; a support condition 'the players: <holder>', which sends the players' part to that holder, a game's tournament pot (David at experience-points 1.8: 'money distribution to players is through tournaments'), instead of to each player by beats; `partsOf`, a sum shared exactly; `parseLine` and `ledgerLines`, a ledger's IN and PAID lines wherever the beach's appends put them, the family a sum is for being the first word after 'for' on an IN line; and `runLedger`. `runLedger` shares every sum that names a family as that family stood when the sum landed, adds each person's exact part, makes whole pence once at the end, takes off what the ledger says each was paid ('could' is what is left), and counts a sum for anything, or for a word that names no family, as shared by nobody. It runs by a running index a pot (each sum's part over its column's whole weight, each holder settled at their weight times the index's rise while they held it), one step a sum and one a new beat, so a month of ten thousand tickets runs in a fifth of a second. With `slow: true` it goes sum by sum through `columnsOf` and `partsOf`, the reference, and `check-xp.mjs` checks the two ways agree to the penny on an invented month. `credits: true` writes whole credits at each sum, as a ticket machine would; `readAt` reads every sum at one moment, the lab's contrast; `trace` gives each sum's own parts, every holder's when it goes sum by sum and the watched ones' (`watch`) when it goes by the index or by one reading. Checked by `node scripts/check-xp.mjs`.
