# `theme.js` Share

Share, beside the door (23 Nov 2026, lane share-string.1; the share wall at `spine:o-page-unit` 1.11111111112, David's GO at 1.11111111118: 'Go.'): one link, 'Share', in the bottom-left pair with 'with your AI', opening the String's box with the page's address already in it (`/string?add=<the page>`, the String's own way in, `string.md`). The sharer writes a line of why and shares; the line lands in their own string, 'SHARE <the page> — why it caught me' (or 'for <handle>', to one person), and the String's toast offers 'Send its link', the share's own address, `https://happyseaurchin.com/string/<address>`, handed to the phone's share sheet (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email, anywhere) or copied. That link's preview says who shared what and why, with the thing's picture (`function:string` 5.1, served by `/api/string?page=`), and whoever opens it meets the sharer's card and is one tap from the page, the tap carrying `?from=<the sharer>` (`string.md`). Nothing is written anywhere but the String and nothing new is kept. The address shared is the page's own on `https://happyseaurchin.com`, whatever host serves it (a preview, a dev server), its query kept but for the `?from=` it may have arrived with (the share is the sharer's own) and the `?still` and `?share` that draw a page's share picture, no hash, no trailing slash, `.html` dropped; `window.siteShareUrl()` returns it. The String opens on the host serving the page. WHERE IT STANDS: on every page that carries the corner door it stands just right of it, the same pill filled in the door's violet (lilac in the dark), and moves with it wherever the door steps clear of a page's own fixed control (`keepClear(b, f)`: the door keeps clear, Share follows, and neither counts the other as a neighbour); where a page's own buttons open the door (`data-ai-door`, the eight crafted pages that once kept doors of their own) and the corner stands aside, Share takes the corner itself and keeps clear the same way; a page that is no family's face may ask for it with `data-share="on"` on its `<html>` or `<body>`, as `/arcade` and `/games` do. It hides with the door while a sheet, pop or dialog is open, steps away with it while a phone's keyboard is up, and is not printed. Never in a frame, never where the html or body says `data-ai-door="off"` or `data-share="off"`, and never at a members' door (`data-door` on `<html>`): what a member shares goes on their own beach's string, never this one. Its `<a>` is a plain link, so a long press or a middle click opens the String in a new tab. Checked with `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node scripts/smoke-door.mjs`, which passes with Share beside the door (nothing over the door, the door over nothing).
