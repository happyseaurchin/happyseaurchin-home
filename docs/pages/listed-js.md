# `listed.js`

the proposals on the beach read as one list (23 Nov 2026, lane arcade-credits.1, at David's word: "Go with the non-permission method; the listing on proposals page should go through the check and if it doesn't match, send message to pool of proposal"). Two kinds of block are read together: the keeper's list, `proposals`, under the shared project lock, and each maker's own, `proposed:<handle>`, a line `<name> — what it is · its page` at each digit under the maker's own key, which anyone keeps with nobody's permission (function:proposal 7 and 8). `beachListed.read(keeperBlock)` gives `{ keeper, makers }`: the keeper's lines as the list page has always parsed them, then every maker's line whose name is not already listed, the earliest list first (by its `born` stamp from `/api/mirrors?family=proposed`, the beach's index straight where that read fails), a proposal counted once where it is first listed. Each maker's line carries `by`, and `ok` with `fault`: its family is read (`spine:<name>`, `function:<name>`, `pool:<name>`) and checked against the battery's first faults in the battery's own order (pscale://proposal-test, `proposal_form.py`), as a page can read them: a name of lower-case letters, digits and hyphens; the spine and the law standing; the spine's root not nested, with a title before ' — ' and a first step at 1; the law's 1 'mode: document' and its 7 'phase: …'; every decision a page reads (each step's 8 down 1, and each side-room's 8) opening 'DECIDE — ', naming whom it waits on ('Asked of …'), its own words closing the line in capitals with no gloss in brackets, and words of its own keeping NOT YET; and a room. The first fault found is given in words a maker can act on. The module only reads. `/proposal` (the index and each person's sections) and `/corkboard` load it: a maker's proposal that passes joins the shelf after the keeper's lines, so it lands as the newest, marked '(listed by <handle>)', and on the board as a card newer than the reading until the board's next one; one that fails waits below the index's shelf with its fault. Makers' lists that take longer than three seconds are left for the next look, so neither page waits on them. weft's daily check (wake:weft 6.31) runs the whole battery over every maker's list and says what to mend in each failing proposal's own room, `pool:<name>`, once for each change of its faults; the battery's own list check (`proposal_form.py`, LISTED) still reads `proposals` alone, and its note on a maker-listed family is owed a change in bsp-mcp-server.
