# `games/wrap.js`

the wrapper (24 Nov 2026, lane coil.1, at David's 'Build the wrapper kit for hosted games'): the beach's side of every game hosted at `/games/<game>`, around the game and never inside it. David drew the line the same afternoon: the PLUG is a game's mechanics with others (the room plug's introductions and relay, matches, lockstep, rooms that split, scale), the WRAPPER its admin and the beach's text (share, its room and the lines left there, points, your own AI, the board). This is the wrapper, for hosted games first.

WHERE IT STANDS: `games/frame.js` loads it on every frame page, so no game's own pages change, and it lays one handle on the frame page's left edge, mid-height, where no hosted game keeps a control (Coil's and Forage's sit on the top and bottom edges), outside the game's sealed frame, so a tap on it never reaches the game. The handle, a ring and the word 'beach', carries a green dot while anyone is in the game's room.

THE DRAWER it opens is a `<dialog>`: a bottom sheet on a phone, a 400 px panel on the left of a wide screen, light or dark as the person's system is. In it, in order:
- **Share on the String**: the String's own way in, `/string?add=<the game's page>`, in a new tab (`string.md`, `theme-js-share.md`).
- **Send the link**: the phone's share sheet, else copied.
- **Ask your AI about it**: the site's door (`siteDoorOpen`, `door-js.md`) for the game's family where its law stands on the beach, its first message written for a game (played, never answered), handed the name the person plays this game under, since the page keeps their runs there (`<game>:<name>`); the device's own name, which the game never sees, only says who asks.
- **Today**: the board's top three and the day's count, from `/api/board` as `/arcade` reads them (a number is a time only where its word is s), and who is in its room now, from the room plug's liquid as `/games` reads it, re-read every half minute.
- **Money for the game, the till** (27 Nov 2026, lane coil.1; thread:155), for a game whose law carries a SUPPORT line (Forage's `function:forage` 8: weights 5 10 80 5, makers happyseaurchin), in place of the Experience Points card, as `spine:value-weightings` has money. A sum waits in the pot of the scope its giver names, nothing is held for anyone, and a part is worked out only when someone asks to take it out at Shore, the keeper paying it by hand. THE SCOPES (David: 'a single product-point (game), or column (xp group), or everyone (all xp distribution)'): The game, a line; A column, a point, with Players, Bringers, Makers (the first) or The beach to choose; Everyone, the surface, every thing the switch names. For each: £1, £5 or £10, and where it would go now, by name and column, from `/api/xp?quote=` (`api.md`). The pot is read two ways side by side, to test them against each other on real play (David: 'We need to test viability with xp and weightings versus credits'): each part as things stand now (`?ledger=&read=now`, value-weightings) and as each sum landed (`&read=landed`, what a credit would hold). A sum is one line in value-weightings' own form, `IN — £5 · from <name> · for forage`, `… · for forage · makers` or `… · for anything`. THE SWITCH, one for every game (David: 'I need a way to switch it on/off centrally, or to effectively pay for it myself in some way'), is the keeper's own block `till:happyseaurchin`, under his key, read as a drawer opens. Its 1, `till: off | keeper | on`, is who sees the till: nobody, only a device signed as happyseaurchin (by `render:who` or the game's own name), or everyone. Its 2, `paid by: player | <handle>`, is whose money a sum is: where a handle pays, the line is that handle's, `· chosen by <player>` added and the player at 6. Its 3, `money: test | real`. Test goes into the open test ledger `ledger:test-till`, anyone's line, ending `· a test, nothing charged`. Real goes into the beach's own ledger `ledger:till`, under happyseaurchin's key (David: 'operational for me … to flick it to financial'), so only the keeper puts a sum in, with the key his device keeps (`siteKeys.forName`), asked for once where it keeps none and kept as `beach-key:till:happyseaurchin` once the beach takes it; a stranger reads the pot and their part, with no put and a line saying why. Its 4, `things: forage`, names the things Everyone spans. A switch that cannot be read, or says nothing it knows, keeps the till shut. It was founded at keeper, player, test, forage. The till's buttons carry `data-gw-scope`, `data-gw-layer` and `data-gw-amt`, never `data-scope`, which `theme.js` takes for its own scope chooser. Checked (scratchpad `till2-check.mjs`, `/api/xp` run from the repository's own code against the live beach, every write held back), twelve checks: test and on, the game by default with its quote and the pot read both ways, a column's four choices, a column's line and everyone's line in the test ledger in value-weightings' form; real and on, a stranger reading the beach's ledger with no put; real on the keeper's device, the key asked for, nothing written without it, the keeper's £1 for the game going into `ledger:till` with the key and the key then kept; off, no till even on the keeper's device; keeper, no till on a stranger's; nothing thrown anywhere. The ticket machine's card payment, for anyone to put real money in, is the next step.
- **Your switch** (27 Nov 2026, lane coil.1, at David's 'operational for me (as owner of the game) to flick it to financial'): on the keeper's own device only (signed as `happyseaurchin` here, or playing under that name), shown even while the till is off, the switch itself: who sees the till (Nobody, Only me, Everyone: `till:happyseaurchin` 1 `off|keeper|on`), the money (A test, Real: 3 `test|real`) and who pays (The player, Me: 2 `player|happyseaurchin`). A choice rewrites its one line with the keeper's key, the word changed and the line's own words kept, and the till redraws from the switch as the beach then has it. Where no key is kept on the device it is asked for once and kept through `siteKeys` as the till keeps it; a key the beach refuses is said so and not kept. Checked (scratchpad `switch-check.mjs`, the switch answered and changed in memory): off on the keeper's device shows the switch and not the till; a flick with no key writes nothing and asks for it; a wrong key is refused and kept nowhere; Only me rewrites line 1 and the till appears; Real rewrites line 3 with the kept key and the till reads real money; Me rewrites line 2 and the put says paid by happyseaurchin; Everyone says on; a stranger's device has no switch; and the till's own twelve checks pass as before.
- **The room**: its last five lines, and a box to leave one, appended to the open room `pool:<game>` in the plug's note shape (`{_, 1: '<name>, a visitor', 2: the day, 3: when}`) as `/arcade`'s box appends one. The line goes under the name the person plays this game under (kept by the frame at `games:<game>:render:who`), else the device's, else one the beach's voice makes. The box keeps its draft (`keepDraft`, key `games:<game>:room`) and takes dictation (`dictate`; its glyph's rules are carried in the wrapper, since the frame page loads no `theme.css`, which would dress the frame around the game).
- **Experience Points**: one line on what play will earn as the proposal stands (`experience-points.md`), linked.
- The way back to `/games` and `/arcade`.

A game the beach knows nothing of (`/api/board` 404, as The Chain) shows no board and no room.

WHAT IT LEAVES ALONE: `theme.js` and the beach's voice load on the first opening and never before, so a game starts as it always did; the frame page sets `data-ai-door="off"` as they load, so the site's corner door and Share stay out of the game's corner, the drawer carrying both. It reads no message from the game's frame and adds nothing to the frame's sandbox or to `vercel.json`'s header for `/games/:game/game`, so the game can reach none of it, and nothing it loads reaches into the game. Never in a page framed by another.
