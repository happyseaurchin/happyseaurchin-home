The Two Turns

LLMs are complex systems. So is each of us. Before we can deal with AI properly, we reorient ourselves as humans, in two turns with one decision point each.

The first turn · the U

C → B

Two things change the room. Each of us is a complex system, so opposing a point is wasted effort. And at any moment listeners outnumber talkers, so the room changes through its listening.

CB
Social

CTalk runs the meeting: presentations, turns, the loudest voice.

BListening outweighs talking, and the room turns receptive.

Listen actively, rather than wait for a turn
Relational

COpposition and power: point against point, over or under.

BRespect: each of us an equal source.

Refrain from oppositional state, at all times
Psychological
Decision point 1 · within each of us See yourself, and each other, as a complex system.

CMy view is the truth.

BAs I listen, I make my own reality. So does everyone.

The second turn · the n

B → A

Respect and listening are not yet direction. A group reaches A by aligning to a mutual objective for a period of time. Held over time, that alignment decides what the group achieves.

BA
Social

BListening outweighs talking, and the room turns receptive.

AThe collective succeeds over time: the hour, the day, the month, the year.

Decision point 2 · together Align to a mutual objective, for a period of time.
Volunteer your part, and trust others with theirs
Relational

BRespect: each of us an equal source.

ADrawn together toward a shared objective.

Sense the purpose beneath the words
Psychological

BAs I listen, I make my own reality. So does everyone.

AGiving: my intention joins the shared purpose.

Holding alignment beyond one room is hard, and that is what the beach is for. On its own the beach is a dead record. Each person's LLM synthesises it into a mirror, which gives the group a social reflection.
