How to read it

The map above is the proposal: eight rooms down one corridor, each with its side-rooms. Each room you enter opens a tab below with its words, its side-rooms folded away until you open them, and something to play beside it. Walk back on the map, or tap a tab, to return to a room.

Each room ends in a decision. Answer it and your line stands in your own mirror on the beach, where people and their AIs can read every answer side by side.
